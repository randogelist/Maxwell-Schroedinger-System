GOAL
----
Identify the smallest mathematically coherent group-theoretic foundation
that (i) recovers Euler and viscous Navier-Stokes on a periodic domain;
(ii) supports product layers and spectral interactions; (iii) supports
advected fields and gauge-current ports; (iv) keeps Standard Model gauge
symmetries distinct from volume-preserving spatial diffeomorphisms.

No statement claims that the Standard Model equals NSE or that arbitrary
Lagrangians automatically give equivalent NSE equations.

0. DOMAIN, CONVENTIONS, AND TYPE INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------
M := T_L^3=(R/LZ)^3, with its flat metric and standard volume dx, L>0.
All fields below are smooth and periodic unless stated otherwise.
Physically rho>0 and nu>0, but set rho=1 for G01-G10 to simplify.

G := SDiff(M) = {smooth diffeomorphisms phi:M->M:
                 phi^*dx=dx}.
g := X_sigma(M) = {u in C^infty(M;R^3): div u=0}.
g0 := {u in g: integral_M u dx=0}.
Bracket convention on Eulerian vector fields:

  [u,v] := (u dot grad)v - (v dot grad)u.

The Lie bracket sign for group trivializations is fixed by this definition.
In the variational derivation below the right-trivialized variation has
delta u = partial_t xi+[u,xi].

P := full Leray projection onto divergence-free vector fields,
     retaining constant Fourier modes.
P0 := projection onto the mean-zero divergence-free fields.
A := -P Delta, positive on the nonconstant solenoidal modes.

All pairings <.,.> in G01-G10 are unweighted real L2(M) pairings.
Use only fields for which all integrations by parts are legal.

1. GROUP / ALGEBRA
------------------
G01  SDIFF_GROUP.

The volume-preserving diffeomorphisms form a group under composition,
with identity id_M and inverse phi^{-1}. Its infinitesimal divergence-free
vector fields form the formal smooth Lie algebra g.

G02  DIVERGENCE_FREE_LIE_BRACKET.

For u,v in g,

  div [u,v]
    =u dot grad(div v)-v dot grad(div u)
    =0.

Anti-symmetry and bilinearity are direct.

Jacobi:

  [u,[v,w]]+[v,[w,u]]+[w,[u,v]]=0.

Proof: The differential operators D_u(f):=u dot grad f satisfy

  [D_u,D_v] = D_[u,v].

The ordinary commutator of operators satisfies Jacobi; therefore so
does the vector-field bracket.

G03  MEAN_ZERO_IDEAL.

On the torus,

  integral_M [u,v] dx=0

for all u,v in g, because

  [u,v] = div(v tensor u - u tensor v)

with tensor convention
  (div(v tensor u))_i = partial_j(v_i u_j).

Consequently [g,g] is contained in g0. In particular, g0 is a Lie
subalgebra and an ideal of g. The full SDiff Lie algebra retains
constant-velocity modes; P0 discards them.

2. L2 GEOMETRY, EULER-ARNOLD AND PRESSURE
-----------------------------------------
G04  L2_COADJOINT_IDENTITY.

Define ad_u(v):=[u,v] and its L2 adjoint on solenoidal tests:

  <ad_u^dagger m,v> := <m,[u,v]>.

Integration by parts yields

  ad_u^dagger m
    = -P[(u dot grad)m + (grad u)^T m].

For m=u, (grad u)^T u=grad(|u|^2/2) and P grad=0:

  ad_u^dagger u = -P[(u dot grad)u].

G05  EULER_ARNOLD_VARIATION.

For a smooth flow eta(t) in SDiff,

  u = dot eta circle eta^{-1},

and an admissible variation xi(t) vanishing at both time endpoints,

  delta u = partial_t xi + [u,xi].

Vary the reduced action

  S_Euler = integral dt H(u(t)),
  H(u)=1/2 ||u||_2^2.

Then

  delta S_Euler
    = integral dt <u,partial_t xi+[u,xi]>
    = integral dt <-partial_t u + ad_u^dagger u,xi>.

Stationarity for all xi in g gives

  partial_t u = ad_u^dagger u
              = -P[(u dot grad)u].

Equivalently there is pressure p, unique modulo a time-dependent spatial
constant, such that

  partial_t u+(u dot grad)u+grad p=0,
  div u=0.

The Euler/SDiff geodesic interpretation uses the right-invariant L2 metric;
viscosity must be supplied separately.

G06  HODGE_PRESSURE_PORT.

For forced smooth NSE,

  partial_t u+(u dot grad)u+grad p=nu Delta u+f,
  div u=0,

the Leray-projected equation is

  partial_t u=-P[(u dot grad)u]+nu Delta u+P f.

Taking divergence gives

  Delta p
    =-div[(u dot grad)u]+div f.

Consequently, gradient forces P(grad phi)=0 only affect the effective
pressure when density is constant. This does not extend without
qualification to variable-density or compressible fluids.

3. LIE-POISSON / VISCOSITY SPLIT
--------------------------------
G07  LIE_POISSON_BRACKET.

For sufficiently regular cylinder functionals F,G on the smooth dual,
with solenoidal L2 variational derivatives, define

  {F,G}(u)
    := -<u,[delta F/delta u,delta G/delta u]>.

This is an antisymmetric Poisson bracket and obeys Jacobi on the
admissible functional class; it derives from the Lie algebra bracket.

For H(u)=1/2||u||_2^2,

  dot F = {F,H}

is Euler's projected equation. In particular {H,H}=0.

Caveat: a naive finite Fourier cutoff followed by bracket projection
need not preserve Jacobi. Do not treat a generic Galerkin truncation
as a finite-dimensional Lie subalgebra without proving closure.

G08  VISCOUS_SYMMETRIC_BRACKET.

Define a symmetric negative-semidefinite bilinear form on functionals:

  (F,G)_nu
    := -nu integral_M
         grad(delta F/delta u):grad(delta G/delta u) dx.

Then for smooth NSE solutions, formally

  dot F
    = {F,H}+(F,H)_nu+<delta F/delta u,P f>,

so the velocity equation is

  partial_t u = -P[(u dot grad)u]+nu Delta u+P f.

For H,

  dot H=-nu ||grad u||_2^2 + <u,f>.

This dissipative bracket is NOT the Lie bracket, and it is NOT by
itself a closed energy-conserving GENERIC construction.
A reservoir is required to conserve the total energy.

4. FOURIER TRIADS AND SPECTRAL LAYERS
-------------------------------------
G09  FOURIER_LIE_BRACKET.

Let k,ell in (2pi/L) Z^3 and a,b in C^3 satisfy
k dot a=0, ell dot b=0. For formal complex modes

  u=a exp(i k dot x),
  v=b exp(i ell dot x),

the bracket is

  [u,v]
    =i[(a dot ell)b-(b dot k)a]
       exp(i(k+ell) dot x).

Its coefficient is divergence free at wave number k+ell.

The symmetric convection cross-term, a DIFFERENT operation, is

  P[(u dot grad)v+(v dot grad)u]
    =i P_{k+ell}[(a dot ell)b+(b dot k)a]
       exp(i(k+ell) dot x),

where P_m = I-(m tensor m)/|m|^2 for m != 0 and P_0=I
for the full divergence-free projection.

Thus the Lie bracket is antisymmetric in the pair (u,v), whereas the
quadratic NSE cross-interaction is symmetric in (u,v). Conflating these
two operations would give an incorrect Lagrangian adapter.

G10  SPECTRAL_SUBGROUP_CLOSURE.

For any additive subgroup Gamma of (2pi/L)Z^3, define

  g_Gamma := {smooth real solenoidal u:
              support(Fourier(u)) subset Gamma}.

Since Gamma is closed under addition and negation, g_Gamma is a Lie
subalgebra under [.,.]. The NSE quadratic term, Laplacian, and Leray
projection preserve support in Gamma.

If Gamma is generated by at most two independent spatial wave vectors,
then an integer (hence periodic) spatial direction n is perpendicular
to all Fourier modes and

  (n dot grad)u=0.

Smooth NSE solutions preserve this translational symmetry.
The evolution reduces to the globally regular 2D3C system.

For THREE independent wave vectors, the support argument alone cannot
prove global 3D NSE regularity; special cancellations (e.g. Beltrami)
can still provide global smoothness.

5. PRODUCT LAYERS AND GRAPH EXCHANGE
------------------------------------
G11  NSE_PRODUCT_GROUP.

For N independent fluid layers, the kinematic configuration group is

  G_N := G^N,

with componentwise Lie algebra bracket

  [(u_i),(v_i)]_N:=([u_i,v_i])_i.

This is a group-theoretic base for NSE x NSE and arbitrary finite
NSE layering. Nonlinear exchange terms couple the equations and
must be specified in addition to this product algebra.

G12  GRAPH_LAPLACIAN_EXCHANGE.

Take rho_i>0, eta_i>=0,
kappa_ij=kappa_ji>=0, kappa_ii=0, and smooth div-free u_i.

Define the weighted graph operator

  (L_kappa u)_i := sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j).

Its quadratic form satisfies

  sum_i <u_i,(L_kappa u)_i>
    =sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2>=0.

For connected exchange graph,

  ker L_kappa = {(u,...,u):u in g}.

For layer equations

  rho_i (partial_t u_i + u_i dot grad u_i)
    =-grad p_i+eta_i Delta u_i+f_i-(L_kappa u)_i,

the energy

  E_fluid:=1/2 sum_i rho_i ||u_i||_2^2

satisfies

  dot E_fluid
    =-sum_i eta_i||grad u_i||_2^2
     -sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2
     +sum_i<u_i,f_i>.

The interlayer exchange force is reciprocal and cancels in the total
momentum integral. It dissipates relative kinetic energy.

6. ADVECTED FIELDS, DIAMOND FORCE, AND SEMIDIRECT PRODUCT
---------------------------------------------------------
G13  LIE_DERIVATIVE_REPRESENTATION.

For a smooth scalar field a,

  L_u a:=u dot grad a.

Then

  [L_u,L_v]a=L_[u,v]a.

A passively advected scalar obeys

  partial_t a+L_u a=0.

For differential forms use the genuine Lie derivative, not merely
componentwise transport. In ideal MHD the flux 2-form is Lie-dragged.

G14  DIAMOND_VARIATIONAL_FORCE.

Let a be an advected field in a representation V*, b in its dual V,
and define the diamond operation by

  <b diamond a,xi>
     :=-<b,L_xi a>.

For a scalar advected field, on solenoidal test fields,

  b diamond a = -P(b grad a).

Consider a reduced Lagrangian ell(u,a) and write
m:=delta ell/delta u, b:=delta ell/delta a.

For variations

  delta u=partial_t xi+[u,xi],
  delta a=-L_xi a,

the stationary action gives

  partial_t m = ad_u^dagger m+b diamond a,
  partial_t a+L_u a=0.

This supplies an exact algebraic FIELD-TO-FLUID FORCE PORT for
ADVECTED degrees of freedom. It does not assume that every dynamic
Standard Model field is merely advected.

G15  GAUGE_SEMIDIRECT_BRACKET.

Fix a trivial principal bundle over M and a finite-dimensional
internal Lie algebra k (e.g. u(1), su(2), su(3)).
For k-valued scalar fields xi(x), define on k-valued test fields

  D_(u,xi) a := u dot grad a+[xi,a]_k.

Then

  [D_(u,xi),D_(v,eta)]
    =D_([u,v], u dot grad eta-v dot grad xi+[xi,eta]_k).

Thus the pair bracket is

  [(u,xi),(v,eta)]
    =([u,v], u dot grad eta-v dot grad xi+[xi,eta]_k).

It satisfies Jacobi because commutators of differential operators do,
and the formula can also be verified directly.

For Standard Model gauge algebra choose

  k_SM=su(3)_C direct_sum su(2)_L direct_sum u(1)_Y.

This is a gauge-and-spatial semidirect algebra interface. It does NOT
identify internal SU(3) or SU(2) gauge symmetries with SDiff(M).
For nontrivial gauge bundles the global group may be a non-split
bundle-automorphism extension, so the local trivial-bundle
semidirect-product description is not asserted globally.

7. FIELD-THEORY VARIATIONAL PORT
---------------------------------
G16  VARIATIONAL_WARD_INTERFACE.

For an admissible local field action

  S_L[Phi,A,g]=integral sqrt(-g) L(Phi,D Phi,F,g,...),

define its Euler-Lagrange equations E_Phi and, with fixed conventions,
currents J_A^mu and stress-energy T_L^{mu nu} by variational
derivatives. Gauge identities and diffeomorphism Ward identities
are available when the action possesses those symmetries; anomalies
and gauge fixing must be treated separately.

An exchange port Q_i^nu is additional constitutive data satisfying

  nabla_mu T_L^{mu nu}=-sum_i Q_i^nu,
  nabla_mu T_fluid,i^{mu nu}=Q_i^nu,

up to whichever reservoir and interaction stress tensors are included
in the declared decomposition.

Then

  nabla_mu T_total^{mu nu}=0.

For the nonrelativistic incompressible fluid, the spatial component is
passed through P to produce the solenoidal body-force term P f_i.

The field-to-NSE map is NOT unique without constitutive and
coarse-graining specifications.

G17  RESPONSE_ACTION_ENDPOINT.

Given a specified field residual E_Phi=0 and specified fluid
residuals R_i=0, introduce response fields
tilde Phi, tilde u_i, tilde p_i and the deterministic constraint action

  S_resp
    =integral dt dx [
         <tilde Phi,E_Phi>
        +sum_i <tilde u_i,R_i>
        +sum_i tilde p_i (div u_i)
      ].

Variation with respect to the response fields yields exactly
the field and fluid equations by construction.
This is a response/constraint action, not a microscopic derivation
of the constitutive law or a quantum Schwinger-Keldysh completion.

8. TOTAL ENERGY AND RESERVOIR CLOSURE
-------------------------------------
G18  ENERGY_RESERVOIR_IDENTITY.

For the multilayer system define

  D_total
    :=sum_i eta_i||grad u_i||_2^2
      +sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2>=0.

Assume the external field-sector power is reciprocal:

  dot E_fields = -sum_i<u_i,f_i>,

and define an internal/reservoir energy by

  dot E_res = D_total.

Then

  E_total:=E_fluid+E_fields+E_res

satisfies

  dot E_total=0.

For a positive constant reservoir temperature T0,
an illustrative reservoir entropy S_res=E_res/T0 obeys

  dot S_res=D_total/T0>=0.

This last temperature law is a declared constitutive simplification;
it is not a derived quantum/Standard Model fluctuation relation.

9. FORMAL ASSEMBLY
------------------
For local smooth periodic fields and the stated algebraic/variational
assumptions, compose

  SDiff -> divergence-free Lie bracket
        -> right-invariant L2 Euler-Arnold
        -> Lie-Poisson
        -> NSE dissipative bracket
        -> N-fold product and Fourier spectral layers
        -> semidirect advected/gauge ports
        -> exchange/energy-reservoir identity.

Conclude:

  conditional(G01,...,G18) implies
  GROUP_STRUCTURED_MULTILAYER_FORCED_NSE_WITH_FIELD_PORTS.

This is a complete B_TIER derivation of the EXPLICITLY SCOPED algebraic
implication. It is not unconditional Standard Model derivation, nor
quantum NSE equivalence.
