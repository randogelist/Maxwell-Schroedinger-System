SCOPE
-----
Give exact rules for turning X separately indexed NSE degrees
of freedom into one coupled NSE^X SYSTEM without incorrectly
asserting that velocities, solution flows, or gauge field actions
multiply as group elements.

The first exact full theorem is for a FINITE nonempty layer set
X. Infinite X receives an algebraic product identity and a
separate explicit analytic-conditions register, not a false
unconditional infinite-network PDE theorem.

0. BASE
-------
M=T_L^3, periodic flat 3-torus, volume V=L^3.
X finite, nonempty, individual layers a in X.

For each a choose:
  rho_a>0 (constant mass density),
  eta_a>0 (dynamic viscosity),
  nu_a=eta_a/rho_a>0,
  u_a(t) smooth, div u_a=0, pressure p_a(t).

For each a!=b choose reciprocal symmetric conductance
  kappa_ab=kappa_ba>=0, kappa_aa=0.

Choose a smooth external/matched force density f_a.

The group base is

  G_X=SDiff(M)^X,
  g_X=X_sigma(M)^X,

with componentwise bracket. Common spatial physical diffeomorphisms
are diagonal, while the independently relabeled material maps
are distinct degrees of freedom. This is a configuration group,
not an active symmetry of the fixed flat Laplacian under EVERY
volume-preserving diffeomorphism.

1. COUPLING IS NOT VELOCITY SUPERPOSITION
------------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-01: PRODUCT_CONFIGURATIONS_VS_COUPLED_FLOW.

Direct product of independent solution semigroups would mean
  S_t^product(u_a)=(S_t^{NSE,a}u_a)_a.

When kappa !=0 or a nontrivial common field force is present,
the coupled flow instead obeys its own system
  S_t^coupled != S_t^product
in general. The time-zero derivatives differ by the force/drag term.

Neither the sum of two arbitrary 3D NSE solutions nor the weighted
mean of their velocities is generically a NSE solution; cross
advection produces P[(u_a dot grad)u_b+
                     (u_b dot grad)u_a].
The symbol NSE^X is an INDEXED PRODUCT OF STATE TYPES followed
by a separately declared interaction, not a free superposition
operation on PDE solutions.

2. MINIMAL ALLOWED EXCHANGE MODEL
----------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-02: RECIPROCAL_GRAPH_LAPLACIAN.

Define the graph Laplacian on fluid layer families by
  (L_kappa u)_a := sum_b kappa_ab(u_a-u_b).

Then
  sum_a <u_a,(L_kappa u)_a>_L2
    = sum_{unordered {a,b}} kappa_ab||u_a-u_b||_2^2>=0.

Also,
  sum_a (L_kappa u)_a = 0
as a vector field at each spatial position, because
each reciprocal edge cancels between its endpoints.

If the undirected conductance graph is connected,
  ker L_kappa = {u_a=u_common for all a}.

For X={1,2}, this operator is
  kappa * [[1,-1],[-1,1]],
eigenvalues 0 and 2*kappa as an unweighted layer matrix.

THEOREM NXC-03: MULTILAYER_VISCOUS_NSE.

Declare the actual coupled PDE system:

  rho_a (partial_t u_a+(u_a dot grad)u_a)
    = -grad p_a+eta_a Delta u_a
      -sum_b kappa_ab(u_a-u_b)+f_a,

  div u_a=0.

Equivalently, by full Leray projection,

  partial_t u_a
    =-P[(u_a dot grad)u_a]
     +nu_a Delta u_a
     -rho_a^(-1)(L_kappa u)_a
     +rho_a^(-1)P f_a.

For variable densities the simple projection with a scalar
rho_a^(-1) is no longer the correct pressure elimination;
constant positive densities are an explicit premise.

3. ENERGY AND TOTAL MOMENTUM
-----------------------------
THEOREM NXC-04: FINITE_NSE_POWER_X_KINETIC_ENERGY_LAW.

Define total kinetic energy

  E_X(t)
    :=1/2 sum_a rho_a ||u_a(t)||_2^2.

For smooth periodic solutions of NXC-03,

  dot E_X
    =-sum_a eta_a||grad u_a||_2^2
     -sum_{a<b}kappa_ab||u_a-u_b||_2^2
     +sum_a <u_a,f_a>.

Proof:
Multiply the a-th equation by u_a, integrate over M,
sum over a. Incompressibility kills individual advection
and pressure terms; periodic integration by parts gives
the viscous negative squares; exchange edges give the
graph Dirichlet form from NXC-02. There is no claim of
energy positivity for an arbitrary additional field
action without an independent stress-energy audit.

THEOREM NXC-05: GLOBAL_MOMENTUM_TRANSFER_CANCELLATION.

Define fluid total momentum
  P_X(t)=sum_a rho_a integral_M u_a(t,x) dx.

Then
  dot P_X(t)=sum_a integral_M f_a dx.

The viscosity, pressure, individual self-advection and
pairwise graph exchange integrate to zero on the torus.
Hence unforced exchange conserves total integrated momentum,
even though each layer mean velocity may change.

THEOREM NXC-06: RECIPROCAL_FIELD_POWER_AND_RESERVOIR.

Let a common field sector have energy E_field and
matching force port f_a with
  dot E_field=-sum_a <u_a,f_a>,
with all other electromagnetic/matter currents and
stress contributions either included or assigned to
explicit residual sectors.

Define
  D_X =sum_a eta_a||grad u_a||_2^2
        +sum_{a<b}kappa_ab||u_a-u_b||_2^2>=0,

and reservoir law
  dot E_res=D_X.

Then
  dot (E_X+E_field+E_res)=0.

This is a conditional algebraic law. For full
electron-to-NSE projection the missing current
  J_res=J_e-rho_q u_NSE
requires its signed power to be accounted for separately;
it is not automatically viscous heat.

4. VARIATIONAL CONSERVATIVE COMPOSITION
----------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-07: SPD_CROSS_INERTIA_EULER_POINCARE.

Let C=(C_ab) be a real constant symmetric positive
definite |X|x|X| matrix. For u_a in g_sigma, set

  ell_C(u)=1/2 sum_{a,b} C_ab <u_a,u_b>_L2.

Define conjugate momenta
  m_a=delta ell_C/delta u_a=sum_b C_ab u_b.

For variations induced independently by fluid
configuration paths in G_X,

  delta u_a=partial_t xi_a +[u_a,xi_a],

the Euler–Poincare equations are

  partial_t m_a=ad_{u_a}^dagger m_a,

where by the frozen Lie-bracket convention
  ad_u^dagger m
    =-P[(u dot grad)m+(grad u)^T m].

Proof: Vary ell_C and integrate the first term
by parts. The coefficient of arbitrary xi_a is
  -partial_t m_a+ad_{u_a}^dagger m_a.

The conservative cross-inertia energy satisfies

  dot ell_C
    =sum_a <u_a,partial_t m_a>
    =sum_a <m_a,[u_a,u_a]>
    =0.

For C diagonal with C_aa=rho_a>0, the equations
reduce to ordinary independent incompressible Euler.

For nondiagonal C, the system is a distinct
cross-inertia Euler–Poincare MULTIFLUID MODEL;
do not label it ordinary independently advecting
NSE without its modified momentum-velocity relation.

This demonstrates how genuine cross-layer
Lagrangian coupling may be added WITHOUT falsifying
the product Lie algebra or its Jacobi identity.

5. DIRECT SUM AND ASSOCIATIVE GRAPH GLUING
-------------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-08: DISJOINT_UNION_COMPOSITION.

For finite disjoint index sets X,Y,

  G_(X disjoint_union Y) is naturally isomorphic to G_X x G_Y.

With no cross edges, no shared fields and no extra
interaction action, the total fluid energy and action
are additive, and the PDE is just a direct product.

When cross edges are added, define the complete
conductance graph on X disjoint_union Y. The
dissipation is
  D_graph= sum_over_unordered_edges kappa_ab
           ||u_a-u_b||_2^2.

The value depends on the final set of weighted edges,
not on the order in which disjoint groups were first
parenthesized and then glued. Graph gluing with
DECLARED cross edges is associative at the level of
indexing and final Dirichlet energy.

There is NO canonical interaction generated by the
group product alone; the edges and coefficients are
external constitutive data.

6. GUARANTEED REGULAR COMPOSITION SECTOR
----------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-09: COMMON_2D3C_GRAPH_COUPLED_GLOBAL_REGULARITY.

Suppose X is FINITE and every layer's smooth
divergence-free initial velocity is independent
of the same torus coordinate, for example y:

  partial_y u_a(0)=0 for every a.

Assume f_a=0, eta_a>0, rho_a>0, kappa_ab symmetric
nonnegative and finite.

Because viscosity, individual nonlinearity, pressure
and pointwise drag respect y translations, the
coupled PDE preserves partial_y u_a=0.

Write
  u_a=(w_a^1,b_a,w_a^2), with
  w_a=(u_a^x,u_a^z), b_a=u_a^y,
  grad_2=(partial_x,partial_z), div_2 w_a=0.

Then
  rho_a (partial_t w_a+w_a dot grad_2 w_a)
    =-grad_2 p_a+eta_a Delta_2 w_a
     -sum_b kappa_ab(w_a-w_b),

  rho_a (partial_t b_a+w_a dot grad_2 b_a)
    =eta_a Delta_2 b_a
     -sum_b kappa_ab(b_a-b_b).

Define planar vorticity zeta_a=curl_2 w_a.
The vorticity system is

  rho_a(partial_t zeta_a+w_a dot grad_2 zeta_a)
    =eta_a Delta_2 zeta_a
     -sum_b kappa_ab(zeta_a-zeta_b).

Multiply each by zeta_a and integrate/sum:

  (d/dt) 1/2 sum_a rho_a||zeta_a||_2^2
    =-sum_a eta_a||grad_2 zeta_a||_2^2
     -sum_{a<b}kappa_ab||zeta_a-zeta_b||_2^2<=0.

These weighted vorticity and kinetic-energy bounds,
the finite coupled 2D parabolic regularity argument,
and the passively transported/diffusing b_a
provide global smooth evolution from smooth periodic
data. In particular, no amplitude smallness is needed.

By the standard weak-strong relative-energy argument,
every Leray–Hopf solution of this finite system with
the same smooth data coincides with the global strong
solution, assuming the usual energy inequality.

Thus the exact finite graph-coupled model has a
regularity-guaranteed sector of arbitrary finite
energy, provided the SAME invariant direction is
shared by all layers.

This assertion is NOT extended to arbitrary
3D-dependent field forces or an arbitrary added
nonlinear interspecies advection law.

7. SPECTRAL RANK RULE FOR NSE^X LAYERINGS
------------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-10: COMMON_FOURIER_LATTICE_CONDITION.

Let initial layer a have Fourier support in
an additive subgroup Gamma_a of (2pi/L)Z^3.

Let
  Gamma_total=span_Z(union_a Gamma_a).

The self-advection and graph exchange of NXC-03,
the Laplacian, and Leray projection preserve
Gamma_total. Individual layer supports generally
grow under mixing and need not remain in Gamma_a.

If rank_R(Gamma_total)<=2, all layers share a
translation-invariant spatial direction, hence the
NXC-09 2D3C theorem applies.

If rank_R(Gamma_total)=3, no general 3D regularity
conclusion follows solely from the group structure.
Additional Beltrami/axis constraints, smallness,
or a separate regularity certificate may still apply.

For a shared Standard Model field, its force spectrum
must ALSO lie in Gamma_total to preserve this sector.
Gauge covariance alone does not force that spectral
restriction.

8. STANDARD MODEL FIELD ADAPTER COMPOSITION GATES
---------------------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-11: GAUGE_COMPATIBLE_SHARED_FIELD_INSERTION.

To add a SINGLE physical Standard Model gauge field
and matter action to NSE^X, declare:

 (i) one principal gauge bundle P over spacetime Y;
 (ii) a common base-space gauge/coordinate action, with
      material relabelings kept independent;
(iii) associated-bundle representations and one
      gauge-invariant action S_SM;
 (iv) a declared interaction/coarse-graining map
      producing layer force densities f_a;
  (v) source-current and stress Ward identities;
 (vi) a partition ensuring charge and energy are not
      counted once in microscopic matter and again
      in the effective fluid without subtraction;
(vii) a current residual for electron-current
      projections (if used);
(viii) reservoir or field channels that receive
      dissipated kinetic energy.

Then the reciprocal field work plus NXC-04 and the
reservoir law give NXC-06.

A naive (SDiff(M)^X) semidirect Gau(P) with a
shared gauge connection transformed by the weighted
average of independent layer diffeomorphisms is
generally INVALID: the weighted average is not a
Lie-algebra homomorphism (NXG-05).

Neither this interface nor the group structure
determines transport coefficients (viscosities,
graph conductances), which require constitutive or
microscopic derivation.

9. INFINITE X: REQUIRED ANALYTIC EXTENSION
------------------------------------------
THEOREM NXC-12: INFINITE_NETWORK_FORMAL_EXTENSION.

For countable X with rho_a>0, define the weighted
fluid Hilbert energy space

  H_X={u:
    sum_a rho_a||u_a||_2^2<infinity}.

Let kappa_ab=kappa_ba>=0, eta_a>=0, and define
the graph quadratic form

  Q_kappa(u)=sum_{unordered {a,b}}
                  kappa_ab||u_a-u_b||_2^2.

To obtain a closed densely defined positive graph
operator and justify an energy identity, one must
choose conditions such as finite row conductances
(or a suitable closed Dirichlet form), density
weight bounds, sufficient layerwise NSE regularity,
and convergence of all sums. The abstract group
G_X alone does not provide any of these.

For a measured continuous index space (X,mu_X)
with symmetric nonnegative kernel kappa(x,y), the
formal graph dissipation becomes

  Q_kappa(u)
    =1/2 int_X int_X kappa(x,y)
       ||u(x)-u(y)||_2^2 dmu_X(x)dmu_X(y).

A natural state space is the Bochner space
  L2(X, rho(x)dmu_X; L2_sigma(M)).

For example, bounded symmetric measurable kappa
and finite mu_X, with rho bounded above and away
from zero, suffice to make the graph operator
bounded on the corresponding L2 space.
Well-posedness and global smoothness of the
infinite nonlinear PDE remain independent tasks.

10. STRUCTURAL ADMISSIBILITY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------
An NSE^X composition is admitted by this B-tier
construction only if it specifies:

 C1. layer index set X and finite/infinite analytic regime;
 C2. the SDiff(M)^X configuration group and Lie algebra;
 C3. rho_a>0, eta_a>=0 and PDE force types;
 C4. reciprocal kappa_ab=kappa_ba>=0, or a separately
     audited nonreciprocal interaction sector;
 C5. exact pressure/divergence constraints;
 C6. gauge-covariant source/current/stress port for
     any field-sector insertion;
 C7. no-double-counting source partition and signed
     residual work;
 C8. total-energy/reservoir identity if closed-system
     conservation is claimed;
 C9. spectral-support preservation if a shared 2D3C
     regularity guarantee is claimed;
C10. analytic convergence for infinite families;
C11. no unsupported claim that NSE solution maps
     or weighted velocity averages are group maps.

These are SUFFICIENT conditions for the stated
model's structural identities; they are not a
classification of every conceivable multifluid
or nonlocal interaction theory.
