1. EXACT CANONICAL ANTICOMMUTATION RELATIONS
---------------------------------------------
THEOREM NEC-01: WEDGE_CONTRACTION_CAR.

For i=1,...,d, define on F

  c_i^dagger omega := e^i wedge omega,
  c_i omega := interior_(partial / partial z^i)(omega).

Then for all i,j,

  c_i c_j + c_j c_i = 0,
  c_i^dagger c_j^dagger + c_j^dagger c_i^dagger=0,
  c_i c_j^dagger + c_j^dagger c_i=delta_ij I.

Proof: the wedge product is alternating, contractions anticommute,
and the graded Leibniz formula gives

  i_(e_i)(e^j wedge omega)
     =delta_ij omega-e^j wedge i_(e_i)omega.

Rearrangement yields the mixed CAR. The standard exterior Hilbert
inner product makes
  (c_i)^*=c_i^dagger.

Thus these are bounded finite-dimensional operators and furnish
an exact CAR representation.
