OBJECTIVE
---------
Identify a minimal, noncanonical set of operator *types* and algebraic
identities sufficient to describe a Standard Model input port for a
gauge-compatible, regularity-controlled (NSE)^X layering.

NOT an assertion that a unique finite operator basis generates the
entire nonperturbative Standard Model quantum field theory.
NOT an assertion that bare NSE group identities determine all
Standard Model parameters or its quantum representations.
NOT a proof that an SM-derived interaction obeys NSE regularity.

The final operator collection is minimal in ROLE for this proposed
adapter, NOT proven mathematically smallest or unique.

0. DECLARE THE STANDARD MODEL DATA
-----------------------------------
Physical spacetime Y=R x T_L^3, with spin/tetrad data.
Choose
  K_Gamma=(SU(3)_C x SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y)/Gamma
and its compatible global quotient Gamma, or work locally with
  k_SM=su(3)+su(2)+u(1).
Hypercharge convention Q_electric=T3+Y.

Per minimal family, field representations:
  Q_L=(3,2,+1/6),
  u_R=(3,1,+2/3),
  d_R=(3,1,-1/3),
  L_L=(1,2,-1/2),
  e_R=(1,1,-1),
  H=(1,2,+1/2).
Choose the number of generations, Yukawa matrices,
gauge couplings (g_s,g,g'), Higgs parameters, theta angle,
the chosen renormalization scheme/scale mu_R, and an admissible
state rho or expectation functional omega.

These are DATA, not deducible from NSE Lie algebra identities.

The operator algebra may be taken in a finite gauge-consistent
regularization, a perturbatively renormalized local-field algebra
on a common invariant domain, or a suitable algebraic-QFT setting.
Interacting 4D quantum gauge fields should not be claimed to
possess an explicitly constructed universal nonperturbative
Hilbert-space realization merely from the following formal
identities.

1. GRADED LOCAL FIELD ALGEBRA
------------------------------
THEOREM SMO-01: GRADED_FIELD_AND_STATISTICS_BASE.

Form the Z2-graded algebra of local field polynomials, smeared
against test functions, with bosonic gauge/Higgs operators and
fermionic quark/lepton operators.

For regulated equal-time fermionic operators,
  {psi_A(x),psi_B^dagger(y)}
     =delta_AB delta^3(x-y)
when the chosen canonical field coordinates permit it.

Bosonic canonical pairs obey corresponding regulated CCR
before imposing gauge constraints. A longitudinal gauge
potential is not automatically a physical canonical
observable after Dirac constraints/gauge fixing.

For spacelike-separated gauge-invariant local observables,
graded locality reduces to ordinary commutation of
physical bosonic observables.
Precise domains, ghost signs, distributional smearing
and renormalization are part of this endpoint.

STATUS = FORMAL/REGULATED_STANDARD_QFT_INPUT.

THEOREM SMO-02: GAUGE_GENERATOR_LIE_ACTION.

Choose self-adjoint regulated generators Q_a in a
representation for which the infinitesimal action is
  delta_a O =-(i/hbar)[Q_a,O].

Fix
  [Q_a,Q_b]= i*hbar f_ab^c Q_c
with generator normalization chosen compatibly.

Then Jacobi of operator commutators implies
  [delta_a,delta_b]O = f_ab^c delta_c O.

For local transformations use smeared Gauss generators
G(xi) with
  [G(xi),G(eta)] = i*hbar G([xi,eta])
modulo constraints, boundary terms, and any quantum
central extension/anomaly. An anomaly-free realization
must discharge such obstructions.

STATUS = EXACT ALGEBRA GIVEN REGULATED FIRST-CLASS
GAUGE GENERATOR REPRESENTATION.

2. CONNECTION, CURVATURE AND CONSTRAINT IDENTITIES
--------------------------------------------------
THEOREM SMO-03: COVARIANT_DERIVATIVE_CURVATURE.

For an internal scalar representation rho_R,
  D_mu=d_mu+rho_R(A_mu),
  F_mu nu=d_mu A_nu-d_nu A_mu+[A_mu,A_nu],
and
  [D_mu,D_nu]Phi =rho_R(F_mu nu) Phi.

For a spinor or tangent-tensor field, the complete
covariant derivative commutator ALSO includes its
spacetime/spin curvature action. The displayed
identity is only the INTERNAL gauge contribution.

Bianchi:
  D^A_[lambda F_mu nu] =0.

STATUS = EXACT OPERATOR IDENTITY ON SMOOTH FIELDS
WITH FIXED CONNECTION CONVENTION.

THEOREM SMO-04: FIRST_CLASS_GAUSS_CONSTRAINT.

In an anomaly-free canonically regularized gauge sector,
  G_a := (D_i E^i)_a-rho_a^matter
is constrained to zero on physical states (up to
sign and coupling conventions and nonabelian electric
momentum normalization). The smeared constraints
close under commutators:

  [G(xi),G(eta)] =i*hbar G([xi,eta]).

An anomalous closure, nonzero boundary charge, or
unaddressed gauge fixing would invalidate the
naive first-class statement.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL ON THE CANONICAL CONSTRAINT SYSTEM.

THEOREM SMO-05: BRST_NILPOTENT_PHYSICAL_COHOMOLOGY.

If a gauge-fixed anomaly-free BRST complex is available,
let Q_B be its odd charge, with ghost number +1 and

  Q_B^2=0.

The physical state space is represented by BRST
cohomology at ghost number zero:

  H_phys=ker(Q_B)/im(Q_B),

followed by the physical positivity/completion step
appropriate to the chosen quantization.

A mathematical NSE fluid forcing must be gauge-invariant
or consistently descend to the BRST physical sector.
BRST cohomology by itself supplies no viscosity.

3. HAMILTONIAN AND LOCAL VARIATIONAL OPERATORS
----------------------------------------------
THEOREM SMO-06: QUANTUM_TIME_GENERATOR.

A chosen renormalized, self-adjoint Hamiltonian H_SM
on the physical Hilbert space generates unitary
evolution. For any suitably domain-compatible
time-independent observable O,

  dO_H/dt=(i/hbar)[H_SM,O_H].

For an explicitly time-dependent observable,
add (partial_t O)_explicit.
For a closed time-independent H_SM,

  d< H_SM >/dt=0.

The H_SM sector includes:
 gauge kinetic terms, chiral fermion kinetic terms,
 Higgs kinetic/potential and gauge-invariant Yukawa
 terms, with the chosen couplings and renormalization.

A Lie algebra or representation table alone does NOT
determine these numeric couplings or a state.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL ON SELF-ADJOINT HAMILTONIAN.

THEOREM SMO-07: ACTION_OPERATOR_BASIS_EQUIVALENCE.

For renormalized local EFT or perturbative operators,
integrated actions differing by total derivatives
are equivalent under appropriate boundary conditions.
Operators related by integration by parts, equations
of motion and invertible local field redefinitions
must be handled modulo the chosen perturbative
order and admissibility assumptions; Fierz identities
are additional fermionic operator reductions.

Therefore there is generally NO UNIQUE monomial
operator basis even when the underlying EFT is
specified. A basis is only unique relative to
declared independent representatives and conventions.

For the dimension<=4 minimal SM, the standard
gauge-invariant local terms are a compact generating
action set, but infinitely many composite
observables and renormalization counterterm
composites remain relevant.

STATUS = B_TIER OPERATOR BASIS NON-UNIQUENESS.

4. NOETHER AND FIELD-TO-FLUID EXCHANGE PORTS
---------------------------------------------
THEOREM SMO-08: COVARIANT_CURRENT_WARD.

From an admissible gauge-invariant matter action
S_matter[Phi,A,g], define J^mu by variation of A.

On matter equations of motion and in the absence of
a relevant gauge anomaly,

  D_mu^A J^mu=0.

Individual flavor or chiral currents may fail to
be separately conserved because of Yukawa terms,
masses or anomalies. The closed total gauge current
is the correct input.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL ON ACTION/EOM/ANOMALY CONTROL.

THEOREM SMO-09: STRESS_EXCHANGE_FORCE.

Define gauge/matter stress from a fixed
metric/tetrad variation and correctly allocate
interaction stress. On shell, when the matter
action has no other independently varying sources,

  nabla_mu T_matter^{mu}{}_{nu}
     = <F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k,

  nabla_mu T_gauge^{mu}{}_{nu}
     =-<F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k,

so the total closed stress is covariantly conserved.

The invariant paired quantity
  Q_nu := <F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k
is a covariant energy-momentum EXCHANGE PORT.
For U(1), its spatial components reproduce the
Lorentz force under a specified nonrelativistic
limit.

Interaction terms, Higgs/gravity sectors and
renormalized operator anomalies can add explicitly
declared stress contributions.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL_B_TIER ON FIELD EQUATIONS
AND WARD/VARIATION PREMISES.

THEOREM SMO-10: GAUGE_INVARIANT_OBSERVABLE_ALGEBRA.

The fluid matching must be built from physical
gauge-invariant (or otherwise properly
covariant) operators/expectation values.
A connection A_mu itself transforms affinely;
it is not a gauge-invariant local observable.

The gauge-invariant field energy/stress and
contracted force <F,J> are natural interface
objects, whereas a colored one-point function
J_a^mu cannot be treated as an observer-independent
scalar without a gauge-consistent representation.

STATUS = EXACT TYPE REQUIREMENT.

5. REQUIRED UNIQUE-IDENTIFICATION DATA
----------------------------------------
THEOREM SMO-11: OPERATOR_SIGNATURE_DATA_REQUIREMENTS.

The operator relations above become a
UNIQUE (up to gauge, admissible field redefinitions,
basis changes, and stress improvements) SELECTED
MODEL only AFTER all of the following are chosen:
  (a) global group/bundle and representations;
  (b) complete local action and numerical couplings;
  (c) quantum/gauge regularization and renormalization;
  (d) Hamiltonian realization, constraint/BRST domain;
  (e) physical initial density operator or state;
  (f) boundary and spacetime geometry;
  (g) operator bases and stress improvement convention.

Even this does not uniquely specify a fluid
transport law because coarse-graining map,
closure approximation and environment state
have not been fixed.

not a nonperturbative 4D SM construction and not A_TIER.
