PURPOSE
-------
Derive the exact analogue of the nilpotent BRST operator for the
finite-dimensional auxiliary gradient-shift REDUNDANCY constructed
under frozen SM-05, and determine its gauge-fixing, cohomological,
operator and (NSE)^X implications.

The original frozen SM-06 reference is:

  Q_BRST^2 = 0

for a properly gauge-fixed, anomaly-free Standard Model gauge theory.

THE PRESENT RESULT IS NOT THAT PHYSICAL BRST CHARGE.
Its ghosts are bookkeeping variables for redundant NSE coefficient
coordinates, not electrons, spinors, quantized Standard Model ghosts,
or Yang-Mills matter fields.

0. PREVIOUS EXACT FINITE HODGE MODEL
-------------------------------------
Let M=T_L^3 and choose a finite symmetric nonzero Fourier index set.
Write Q_N for real, mean-zero scalar trigonometric coefficients,
and V_N for the matching real vector trigonometric coefficients.

Let
  G:Q_N -> V_N, G(lambda)=grad lambda,
  C:V_N -> Q_N, C(v)=div v,
  Delta=C G:Q_N->Q_N.

Delta is negative definite and invertible on Q_N.

Define
  P_N=I-G Delta^(-1) C.

Then
  P_N^2=P_N=P_N^*,
  P_N G=0,
  range P_N=V_sigma=ker C,
  V_N=V_sigma direct_sum G(Q_N).

A redundant ambient coefficient v defines the physical velocity
  u=P_N v.

A gauge parameter lambda in Q_N acts by
  v -> v+G(lambda).

This is the additive ABELIAN gradient-shift group.
The quotient V_N/G(Q_N) identifies with V_sigma.

On T*V_N with momentum pi, define
  G_lambda^mom=<pi,G(lambda)>.

The constraints commute:
  {G_lambda^mom,G_eta^mom}=0.

This is an auxiliary constrained representation, not a
native Standard Model Gauss law.

1. GHOST TYPE, PARITY AND BRST RULES
------------------------------------
THEOREM NBR-01: ABELIAN_GHOST_EXTENSION.

Choose a real basis q_a (a=1,...,r) of Q_N,
let g_a=G(q_a) in V_N, and introduce anticommuting
Grassmann-odd ghost coefficients c^a.
Write
  c=sum_a c^a q_a,
  G(c)=sum_a c^a g_a.

The graded algebra of coefficient functions is

  A_min=C^infty(V_N) tensor Lambda^bullet(Q_N^*),

with ghost number 0 for v functions and +1 for c.

Define an odd graded derivation s by
  s v=G(c),
  s c=0,
and extend by the graded Leibniz rule
  s(F G)=s(F)G+(-1)^(parity(F))F s(G).

In coordinate notation on A_min,

  s=sum_a c^a (g_a dot grad_v).

The ghost coefficients are FORMAL Grassmann variables
for gauge shifts; they do not carry physical electron
charge, spin, or particle number.

THEOREM NBR-02: OFF_SHELL_BRST_NILPOTENCY.

The vector fields g_a are constant commuting translations:
  [g_a dot grad_v,g_b dot grad_v]=0.

Then for all F in A_min,

  s^2 F
   =1/2 sum_ab c^a c^b
      [g_a dot grad_v,g_b dot grad_v]F
   =0.

Alternatively, on generators
  s^2v=G(sc)=0,
  s^2c=0.
Since s^2 is an even derivation of the graded algebra,
nilpotency on generators proves s^2=0 everywhere.

No equations of motion, gauge fixing, or fluid
regularity assumptions are required.

2. NONMINIMAL ANTIGHOST AND GAUGE FIXING
----------------------------------------
THEOREM NBR-03: ANTIGHOST_NAKANISHI_EXTENSION.

Introduce independent anticommuting antighost
bar c in Q_N (ghost number -1) and commuting
Nakanishi–Lautrup multiplier B in Q_N (ghost number 0).

Extend s by
  s bar c=B,
  s B=0.

Then s^2 bar c=sB=0 and s^2 B=0.
Therefore s remains nilpotent off shell on the
full nonminimal graded algebra.

CAUTION: keeping B is important for the simple
off-shell nilpotency statement; eliminating it
may alter the off-shell transformation rules.

THEOREM NBR-04: FINITE_FADDEEV_POPOV_GAUGE_FIXING.

Define the gauge condition
  C(v)=div v in Q_N.

Because
  s C(v)=C G(c)=Delta c,

choose an odd gauge-fixing functional
  Psi_gf=<bar c, C(v)+(alpha/2) B>,

where alpha is a real gauge parameter. For alpha=0,
B is the strict gauge-fixing multiplier.

By the GRADED Leibniz rule,

  s Psi_gf
    =<B,C(v)>+(alpha/2)<B,B>
       -<bar c, Delta c>.

The ghost kinetic operator is Delta, independent
of the NSE coefficient v, and invertible on Q_N.
The corresponding finite-dimensional ghost
determinant is proportional to det(-Delta|Q_N),
a nonzero gauge-independent constant.

Thus the ghost action decouples from the NSE
nonlinearity in this ABELIAN redundancy.
This is NOT a universal feature of nonabelian
Yang–Mills BRST gauge fixing.

Because s^2=0,
  s(s Psi_gf)=0,
so the gauge-fixing term is BRST invariant.

3. GAUGE-INVARIANT NSE OBSERVABLE COHOMOLOGY
--------------------------------------------
THEOREM NBR-05: CONFIGURATION_GHOST_ZERO_COHOMOLOGY.

Restrict first to the MINIMAL complex A_min, so it
has no negative ghost degrees.

A ghost-number-zero observable is a smooth F(v).
It is s-closed iff

  sF=sum_a c^a(g_a dot grad_v)F=0,

equivalently
  g_a dot grad_v F=0 for all a.

Because G(Q_N) is a connected translation orbit,
F is invariant under every v->v+G(lambda).

The Hodge direct sum implies exactly

  F(v)=f(P_N v)

for a unique smooth f on V_sigma.
Since A_min has no ghost degree -1, there are
no degree-zero exact boundaries. Hence

  H^0(A_min,s)~=C^infty(V_sigma).

Adding the nonminimal (bar c,B) BRST doublet does
not change this smooth configuration-observable
cohomology when the usual polynomial/contractible
doublet complex is used.

IMPORTANT SEPARATION:
A Hamiltonian BFV complex intended to model full
phase-space reduction needs the constraints G_mom=0,
their ghost momenta/Koszul–Tate mechanism, and suitable
reduction. The minimal configuration BRST cohomology
above is NOT, by itself, a claim that the physical
Hilbert space of a quantum gauge theory has been built.

THEOREM NBR-06: DIFFERENCE_FROM_DE_RHAM_NILPOTENCY.

The earlier frozen SM-04 construction used the
de Rham differential on FULL coefficient space,

  d=sum_i dz^i wedge partial_(v^i),
  d^2=0.

The present BRST differential instead acts ONLY
along longitudinal gradient-shift directions,

  s=sum_a c^a(g_a dot grad_v),
  s^2=0.

Thus
  H^0(s)=functions of P_Nv,
whereas on connected affine V_N
  H^0(d)=locally constant functions
(ordinary degree-zero de Rham cohomology).

The two nilpotent operators have DIFFERENT
complexes, geometrical meanings, and cohomology.
Consequently d^2=0 in SM-04 never by itself
proved the SM-06 reference identity.

4. CANONICAL BFV BRST CHARGE
----------------------------
THEOREM NBR-07: FINITE_ABELELIAN_BFV_CHARGE.

On T*V_N, set the first-class constraints
  G_a=<pi,g_a>,
with {G_a,G_b}=0.

Choose canonical odd ghost/momentum variables
(c^a,P_a) with the standard even graded
Poisson bracket {c^a,P_b}=delta^a_b and
all cross-brackets to the (v,pi) variables zero.

Define the odd ghost-number-one BFV charge
  Omega=sum_a c^a G_a.

Because the G_a commute and have zero Poisson
brackets with the ghosts,

  {Omega,Omega}=0.

The associated BRST differential
  s_FBV F={F,Omega}

is nilpotent by the graded Jacobi identity
(and an even graded Poisson bracket convention).

It gives
  s_FBV v=G(c),
  s_FBV pi=0,
  s_FBV c=0,
and transforms ghost momenta by the constraints
(up to chosen sign convention).

The BFV charge has no
  -(1/2)f_ab^c c^a c^b P_c
term because the gradient-shift Lie algebra
is abelian and its structure constants vanish.

THEOREM NBR-08: FINITE_OPERATOR_BRST_SQUARE_ZERO.

On smooth compactly supported wavefunctions on V_N,
tensored with the finite ghost exterior algebra,
define the commuting translation generators
  Ghat_a=-i*hbar(g_a dot grad_v).

Set
  Qhat_BRST=sum_a c^a Ghat_a,
where multiplication by c^a is the exterior
ghost-creation operator.

The c^a anticommute and the Ghat_a commute,
so on the common invariant test domain

  Qhat_BRST^2
    =(1/2)sum_ab c^a c^b
         [Ghat_a,Ghat_b]
    =0.

This is a direct operator-level formal match to
the SM-06 nilpotency SHAPE.

It does not claim Qhat is a physical self-adjoint
Standard Model BRST charge, nor that kernel(Qhat)
inside the unreduced ambient Lebesgue Hilbert space
is the correct nontrivial physical state space.

Indeed invariance along a noncompact longitudinal
translation direction is not normalizable in L2(V_N);
gauge reduction or rigging must be specified.

5. RESPONSE-ACTION BRST INVARIANCE
----------------------------------
THEOREM NBR-09: NSE_LIFTED_RESPONSE_ACTION_INVARIANCE.

Let F_N(u) be the smooth ambient finite Fourier
acceleration of incompressible NSE, including
specified forcing and, for finite NSE^X,
a graph drag built only from projected velocities.

Define an unconstrained lifted variable
v(t) in V_N, transverse u=P_Nv, momentum response
pi(t) in V_N, and multiplier theta(t) in Q_N.

The deterministic lift is
  dot v=F_N(P_Nv)+G(theta).

Consider the finite response action
  S_0=int dt <pi,
       dot v-F_N(P_Nv)-G(theta)>.

Extend the BRST rules by
  s theta=dot c,
  s pi=0.

Then
  s dot v=G(dot c),
  s F_N(P_Nv)=0,
  s G(theta)=G(dot c).

Therefore the response residual is invariant
and exactly
  s S_0=0.

The full gauge-fixed action
  S_gf=S_0+int dt s Psi_gf
is BRST-invariant:
  s S_gf=0.

Variation with respect to pi gives the lifted
NSE residual; variation with respect to theta
gives the auxiliary momentum constraint
  G^*pi=0,
equivalent to div pi=0.
The transverse gauge C(v)=0 and its time
preservation yield the pressure correction
  theta=-Delta^(-1)C F_N(u).

No claim is made that this deterministic
response action is a microscopic Standard
Model action or a unitary quantum theory.

6. FINITE NSE^X GRAPH COMPATIBILITY
------------------------------------
THEOREM NBR-10: PRODUCT_ABELELIAN_BRST_NSE_POWER_X.

For finite X={1,...,N}, choose independent
ambient spaces V_(N,i), scalar gauge spaces
Q_(N,i), gradient maps G_i and projections P_i.

Introduce independent ghosts c_i, antighosts
bar c_i, multipliers B_i, and compensators
theta_i. Define

  s v_i=G_i c_i,
  s c_i=0,
  s bar c_i=B_i,
  s B_i=0,
  s theta_i=dot c_i,
  s pi_i=0.

Then s^2=0, with all ghosts graded
anticommuting across distinct fluid layers.

The projected physical velocity in each layer
  u_i=P_i v_i
is BRST invariant.

A symmetric graph exchange
  (L_kappa u)_i=sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j)

is BRST invariant because it depends only on
the projected u_i.
Therefore the graph coupling does not spoil
nilpotency or the auxiliary gauge invariance.

The product gauge group is the direct sum
of ABELIAN gradient shift groups, not
the nonabelian internal Standard Model group.

THEOREM NBR-11: REGULARITY_ADMISSION_INDEPENDENT_OF_BRST.

The above BRST complex is an algebraic
constraint/redundancy representation.
It does NOT prove global regularity of
arbitrary 3D NSE or a Standard Model
interaction.

Regularity of an admitted NSE^X interaction
still requires independent analytic input:
  - common 2D3C tangency or Beltrami closure;
  - critical H^(1/2) forcing budget;
  - small 3D perturbation of a globally regular
    reference with exponential stability budget;
  - or an independently established global
    strong PDE theorem.

A generic forcing written in ambient v may break
the gradient-shift redundancy. If the physical
force factors through P_N v (and is projected),
it is compatible with this auxiliary BRST
construction; physical SM gauge symmetry and
source Ward identities remain separate.
