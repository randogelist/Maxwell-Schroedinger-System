This document derives a concrete route from the existing quantum projective
orbit and Maxwell Gaussian length to a reduced two-mode sector of linearized
General Relativity.

The principal conclusions are:

  (1) a global CP1 symplectic match is impossible in the unreduced exact ADM
      cotangent phase space;

  (2) two transverse-traceless gravitational modes admit a U(1) symplectic
      reduction whose reduced phase space is CP1;

  (3) the reduced GR symplectic form has the exact coefficient

        kappa_TT c^3 epsilon^2/G,

      where kappa_TT is computed from the metric-amplitude normalization,
      packet norm, characteristic frequency, and reduction level;

  (4) matching to the Maxwell-Schrodinger quantum form gives

        epsilon = l_P/sqrt(kappa_TT);

  (5) the historical endpoint epsilon=2l_P is equivalent to the explicit
      normalization equation kappa_TT=1/4.

The derivation does not prove that nonlinear Einstein geometry selects
kappa_TT=1/4. It reduces that open assertion to a precise GR normalization and
reduction-level endpoint.


0. FROZEN CONVENTIONS
---------------------

Let

  Sigma := T^3,

and let

  G>0, c>0, hbar>0, epsilon>0.

Define

  l_P := sqrt(G hbar/c^3),
  tau_epsilon := epsilon^2/4,
  a_* := epsilon,
  l_* := epsilon/2.

Let the quantum two-mode orbit be

  O_chi := CP^1,

with Fubini-Study form normalized by

  omega_FS := (1/2) sin(theta) dtheta wedge dphi,
  integral_{CP1} omega_FS = 2 pi.

The Maxwell-Schrodinger action form on this orbit is

  Omega_QM = hbar omega_FS.

The Gaussian transverse datum is

  a_epsilon := exp(tau_epsilon Delta) a_dagger,

whose physical-wave-vector multiplier is

  exp(-epsilon^2 |xi|^2/4).

Thus

  a_* = epsilon,
  l_* = epsilon/2.


1. UNREDUCED ADM EXACTNESS
--------------------------

Let

  Q_ADM := Riem(Sigma)

be the configuration space of spatial Riemannian metrics and let

  P_ADM := T^*Q_ADM

be its kinematical cotangent phase space.

For canonical data (h,pi), define the ADM canonical one-form

  Theta_ADM(h,pi)[delta h,delta pi]
    := integral_Sigma pi^{ij} delta h_{ij} d^3x.

Define

  Omega_ADM := -d Theta_ADM.

THEOREM ADM_KINEMATICAL_EXACTNESS.

  Omega_ADM is exact on P_ADM.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_FROM_DEFINITION.


THEOREM ADM_CP1_GLOBAL_OBSTRUCTION.

For every smooth map

  iota : CP1 -> P_ADM,

one has

  integral_{CP1} iota^*Omega_ADM = 0.

Consequently there is no smooth global map satisfying

  iota^*Omega_ADM = hbar omega_FS

for hbar>0.

DERIVATION.

  iota^*Omega_ADM
    = -d(iota^*Theta_ADM).

Since CP1 is closed,

  integral_{CP1} iota^*Omega_ADM
    = -integral_{CP1} d(iota^*Theta_ADM)
    = 0.

But

  integral_{CP1} hbar omega_FS = 2 pi hbar != 0.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

INTERPRETATION.

A global QM-GR symplectic match cannot occur inside the unreduced exact ADM
cotangent phase space. At least one of the following is required:

  (a) a genuine symplectic quotient with nontrivial second cohomology;
  (b) boundary or edge degrees of freedom;
  (c) a coadjoint-orbit sector;
  (d) only local chartwise matching rather than global CP1 matching.


2. LOCAL DARBOUX MATCHING AND NONSELECTION
------------------------------------------

On the north chart of CP1 define

  J := hbar sin^2(theta/2).

Then

  Omega_QM = dJ wedge dphi.

Let a GR canonical plane have positive symplectic coefficient S:

  Omega_plane := S dQ wedge dP.

Define locally

  Q := sqrt(2J/S) cos(phi),
  P := sqrt(2J/S) sin(phi).

Then

  Omega_plane = dJ wedge dphi = Omega_QM.

THEOREM QM_GR_LOCAL_DARBOUX_MATCHING.

Every positive coefficient S admits a local symplectic matching on a
contractible CP1 chart.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

CONSEQUENCE.

Local Darboux matching alone cannot determine epsilon, kappa, or a Planck
scale. Global reduction data or an independent normalization law is required.


3. LINEARIZED TT GRAVITY CONVENTION
-----------------------------------

Adopt the following physical-time quadratic action convention for a
transverse-traceless spatial perturbation h_ij:

  S_TT[h]
    := (c^2/(64 pi G))
       integral dt integral_Sigma
       [ dot(h_ij) dot(h_ij)
         - c^2 partial_k(h_ij) partial_k(h_ij) ] d^3x.

The canonical momentum density is

  Pi^{ij} = (c^2/(32 pi G)) dot(h_ij).

The induced canonical one-form and symplectic form are

  Theta_TT
    = (c^2/(32 pi G))
      integral_Sigma dot(h_ij) delta h_ij d^3x,

  Omega_TT := -d Theta_TT.

This section is a frozen normalization convention. Any alternate
normalization of the metric perturbation must be translated before numerical
comparison of coefficients.


4. TWO TT PACKETS AND THE SCALE COEFFICIENT
--------------------------------------------

Let

  T^0_{ij,epsilon}, T^1_{ij,epsilon}

be two real TT initial-data profiles satisfying

  partial_i T^a_{ij,epsilon}=0,
  delta^{ij}T^a_{ij,epsilon}=0,

and the orthogonality/scale law

  integral_Sigma
    T^a_{ij,epsilon} T^b_{ij,epsilon} d^3x
      = epsilon^3 I_TT delta_ab,

where

  I_TT>0.

Let

  A>0,
  sigma>0,
  omega_epsilon := sigma c/epsilon.

For real mode coordinates Q_a,P_a define initial data

  h_ij
    := A sum_{a=0}^1 Q_a T^a_{ij,epsilon},

  dot(h_ij)
    := A omega_epsilon
       sum_{a=0}^1 P_a T^a_{ij,epsilon}.

THEOREM GR_TT_TWO_MODE_SYMPLECTIC_FORM.

The pullback of the TT symplectic form to the four-dimensional mode space is

  Omega_TT
    = S_epsilon sum_{a=0}^1 dQ_a wedge dP_a,

where

  S_epsilon
    := (c^3 epsilon^2/G)
       [A^2 sigma I_TT/(32 pi)].

DERIVATION.

Substitution into Theta_TT gives

  Theta_TT
    = (c^2/(32 pi G))
      A^2 omega_epsilon epsilon^3 I_TT
      sum_a P_a dQ_a.

Since omega_epsilon=sigma c/epsilon,

  (c^2/(32 pi G))
  A^2 omega_epsilon epsilon^3 I_TT

    = (c^3 epsilon^2/G)
      [A^2 sigma I_TT/(32 pi)].

Taking -d and choosing the positive canonical orientation yields the stated
form.

5. GLOBAL CP1 BY U(1) SYMPLECTIC REDUCTION
------------------------------------------

Define complex mode variables

  z_a := (Q_a+iP_a)/sqrt(2).

Fix a reduction radius R>0:

  |z_0|^2+|z_1|^2=R^2.

Quotient by the common phase action

  (z_0,z_1) -> exp(i alpha)(z_0,z_1).

The quotient is the Hopf reduction

  S^3_R/U(1) = CP1.

On the north and south sections choose

  z_N
    = R(cos(theta/2), exp(i phi)sin(theta/2)),

  z_S
    = R(exp(-i phi)cos(theta/2), sin(theta/2)).

Up to exact terms, the canonical potential is represented by

  alpha_N
    = S_epsilon R^2 sin^2(theta/2) dphi,

  alpha_S
    = -S_epsilon R^2 cos^2(theta/2) dphi.

Their difference on the overlap is

  alpha_N-alpha_S = S_epsilon R^2 dphi,

while their curvatures agree:

  d alpha_N = d alpha_S
    = S_epsilon R^2 omega_FS.

THEOREM GR_TT_HOPF_REDUCTION_CP1.

The reduced two-mode TT phase space is CP1 with reduced symplectic form

  Omega_GR_red
    = S_epsilon R^2 omega_FS
    = kappa_TT (c^3 epsilon^2/G) omega_FS,

where

  kappa_TT
    := A^2 sigma I_TT R^2/(32 pi).

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

GR_NONLINEAR_REDUCTION_GATE:

GR_ORBIT_IDENTIFICATION_GATE:

  symplectomorphic sphere.

  packet conventions before epsilon can be interpreted as universal.

These are explicit unresolved premises.
