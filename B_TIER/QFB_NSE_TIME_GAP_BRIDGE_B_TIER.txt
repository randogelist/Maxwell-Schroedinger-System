0. FROZEN PERIODIC QFB PREMISES
-------------------------------
M=T^3 unit flat torus, nu>0.
a in L2_sigma,0(M). Let u be a Leray-Hopf
solution with initial datum a.

The named external QFB admission is

  QFB_ADMISSION:
    ||b||_(dot H^(1/2))^2 < 2*pi*nu^2
       -> global regular strong solution from b,

plus the standard weak-strong uniqueness conclusion
from any good restarting time.

This numerical threshold is an EXPLICIT PREMISE,
not a new proof of the threshold constant from
the present note. The general case can instead
use an explicit C_NL critical trilinear constant.

1. ENERGY-WEIGHTED LERAY-HOPF BOUND
------------------------------------
Let
  M0=||a||_2,
  E(t)=||u(t)||_2^2,
  D(t)=||grad u(t)||_2^2,
  H(t)=||u(t)||_(dot H^(1/2))^2,
  A_D(t)=int_0^t D(s)ds.

The standard energy inequality gives for a.e. t
  E(t)+2nu A_D(t)<=M0^2.

Therefore

  int_0^T E(t)D(t)dt
   <=int_0^T [M0^2-2nu A_D(t)] A_D'(t)dt
   =M0^2 A_D(T)-nu A_D(T)^2
   <= M0^4/(4nu).

Homogeneous Fourier interpolation gives

  H(t)^2<= E(t)D(t).

Thus

  int_0^infinity H(t)^2dt <= M0^4/(4nu).

The earlier coarse approach bounded E(t) by M0^2
AFTER integrating D, producing M0^4/(2nu).
The weighted argument is a strict improvement
of the integral upper bound, without extra NSE
regularity or energy equality assumptions.

2. GOOD RESTART AND IMPROVED PERIODIC CUTOFF
---------------------------------------------
For any

  delta > M0^4/(16pi^2 nu^5),

the mean-value argument yields some good time
s in (0,delta) such that

  H(s)^2 < (2pi nu^2)^2
  -> H(s)<2pi nu^2.

Invoke QFB_ADMISSION and weak-strong uniqueness.
Then u is smooth for all t> T_refined, where

  T_refined=M0^4/(16pi^2 nu^5).

No regularity at the endpoint t=T_refined follows,
no singularity existence follows, and recursive
halving is not justified by this estimate alone.

3. CIRCULAR ELECTRON-ADAPTED NSE INPUT
---------------------------------------
On T_L^3, with unnormalized physical integration,
let k=2pi n/L, n!=0 and

  a_epsilon(z)
    =r0 (cos(kz+alpha),sin(kz+alpha),0),
  r0=|lambda|s exp(-epsilon^2k^2/4).

Then

  ||a_epsilon||_2^2=L^3 r0^2.

The two valid sufficient upper cutoffs are

  T_coarse^circ = L^6 r0^4/(8pi^2 nu^5),

  T_refined^circ = L^6 r0^4/(16pi^2 nu^5).

Under a declared fluid density rho_f>0,

  E_NSE(0)=(rho_f L^3/2)r0^2,

and

  T_refined^circ
    = E_NSE(0)^2/(4pi^2 rho_f^2 nu^5).

For the exact circular NSE solution itself, including
the helical electron-current profile,

  u(t)=exp(-nu k^2 t)a_epsilon,

the actual regularity gap is zero for all nu>0.

Hence neither T_coarse nor T_refined is an
actual singularity onset time.

4. COUPLED NSE^X EXTENSION
--------------------------
For a finite symmetric nonnegative graph of
N unforced mean-zero NSE layers with common nu>0,
set

  E0=sum_i ||a_i||_2^2,
  H(t)=sum_i ||u_i(t)||_(dot H^(1/2))^2.

The same energy-weighted proof yields

  int_0^infinity H(t)^2 dt<=E0^2/(4nu).

Let C_NL be the product critical trilinear constant
with small-data sufficient condition

  sqrt(H)<nu/(2C_NL).

Then a sufficient product restart cutoff is

  T_graph=4 C_NL^4 E0^2/nu^5.

The single-layer special numerical constant
2pi nu^2 must NOT be applied to all graph-coupled
systems unless its coupled admission is independently
proved. Product graph dissipation is nonnegative
in all homogeneous Sobolev energy estimates.
