This document composes the frozen energy-refined Quasi-Fefferman-B cutoff with
the exact circular NSE time and energy scales.

The QFB time gap is a worst-case certificate window between the initial datum
and the guaranteed smooth tail. It is not an assertion that a singularity
exists, and it is not the viscous e-folding time of the exact circular mode.


0. FROZEN QFB CUTOFF
--------------------

Let a be mean-zero divergence-free L2 initial data on T_L^3, and let nu>0.
Define the canonical QFB cutoff

  T_star(a,nu)
    := ||a||_2^4/(8 pi^2 nu^5).

Define the energy-refined cutoff

  T_QFB(a,nu)
    := ||a||_2^4/(16 pi^2 nu^5)
     = T_star(a,nu)/2.

The frozen energy-refined QFB conclusion is

  Sing_t(u) subset (0,T_QFB(a,nu)],

and every admissible Leray-Hopf trajectory is smooth for

  t > T_QFB(a,nu).

The endpoint t=T_QFB is not certified. No recursive halving

  T_QFB -> T_QFB/2 -> ...

is established without a new restart estimate.

1. CIRCULAR NSE PROFILE
-----------------------

Let

  T_L^3 := (R/LZ)^3,
  V := L^3,
  k := 2 pi n/L,
  n != 0,

and define

  r0 := |lambda| s exp(-epsilon^2 k^2/4).

The mean-zero circular fluctuation is

  a_epsilon(z)
    := r0 (cos(kz+alpha), sin(kz+alpha), 0).

Since |a_epsilon(x)|=r0,

  ||a_epsilon||_2^2 = V r0^2 = L^3 r0^2.

Therefore

  T_QFB^circ
    = L^6 r0^4/(16 pi^2 nu^5)

    = L^6 lambda^4 s^4 exp(-epsilon^2 k^2)
      /(16 pi^2 nu^5).

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_SUBSTITUTION.

The constant mean transport U0 e3 is excluded from the frozen mean-zero QFB
carrier; equivalently, pass to the comoving frame and apply QFB to the
fluctuation.


2. ENERGY FORM
--------------

The physical fluctuation kinetic energy is

  E_NSE(0)
    := (rho_f/2) ||a_epsilon||_2^2
     = (rho_f L^3/2) r0^2.

Hence

  ||a_epsilon||_2^2 = 2 E_NSE(0)/rho_f,

and the QFB gap becomes

  T_QFB^circ
    = E_NSE(0)^2/(4 pi^2 rho_f^2 nu^5).

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

This formula uses the unnormalized physical L2 norm. The density rho_f is the
same physical energy convention frozen in NSE_TIME_ENERGY_SCALE_BRIDGE_B_TIER.


3. RELATION TO THE EXACT NSE DECAY TIMES
----------------------------------------

The exact circular NSE mode has

  T_amp    = 1/(nu k^2),
  T_energy = 1/(2 nu k^2).

The QFB certificate gap is distinct:

  T_QFB^circ/T_amp
    = L^6 r0^4 k^2/(16 pi^2 nu^4),

  T_QFB^circ/T_energy
    = L^6 r0^4 k^2/(8 pi^2 nu^4)

    = E_NSE(0)^2 k^2/(2 pi^2 rho_f^2 nu^4).

Thus QFB does not replace the viscous decay time. It gives a worst-case waiting
window before regularity is guaranteed for a general admissible trajectory.

For the explicit smooth circular solution itself,

  actual regularity gap = 0.

Therefore T_QFB^circ is conservative certificate time, not the actual onset of
regularity for this exact mode.

4. TAIL CLOCK AFTER THE QFB GAP
-------------------------------

Define the certified tail coordinate

  tau := t - T_QFB^circ.

The regular tail is certified for

  tau > 0.

For the exact circular solution, the amplitude and energy at the certificate
boundary are

  r_QFB
    = r0 exp(-nu k^2 T_QFB^circ),

  E_QFB
    = E_NSE(0) exp(-2 nu k^2 T_QFB^circ).

Under the synchronized phase law

  omega_NSE = k U0 = Delta_E/hbar,

the accumulated phase displacement across the QFB window is

  Delta_phi_QFB
    = -omega_NSE T_QFB^circ
    = -(Delta_E/hbar) T_QFB^circ

modulo 2 pi.

Hence the tail clock can be restarted at tau=0 with the phase origin shifted by
Delta_phi_QFB.

5. OPTIONAL ACTION CALIBRATION
------------------------------

Under the previously declared amplitude-time action calibration

  E_NSE(0) T_amp = hbar,

one has

  E_NSE(0) = hbar nu k^2.

Therefore

  T_QFB^circ
    = hbar^2 k^4/(4 pi^2 rho_f^2 nu^3).

This value depends on the optional action-calibration convention; QFB itself

6. ROOT ENDPOINT
----------------

THEOREM QFB_NSE_TIME_GAP.

Under the frozen energy-refined Quasi-Fefferman-B estimate, the unresolved
positive-time regularity window has length

  T_QFB(a,nu)
    = ||a||_2^4/(16 pi^2 nu^5).

For the circular NSE fluctuation,

  T_QFB^circ
    = L^6 r0^4/(16 pi^2 nu^5)
    = E_NSE(0)^2/(4 pi^2 rho_f^2 nu^5).

Every admissible trajectory is certified smooth for t>T_QFB^circ, while the
exact circular trajectory is already smooth from t=0. The QFB endpoint is a
certificate gap and does not assert a singularity at or before the cutoff.
