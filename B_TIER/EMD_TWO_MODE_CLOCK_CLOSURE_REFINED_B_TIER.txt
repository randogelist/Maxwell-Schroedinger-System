This document refines the one-electron EMD/QR clock chain by replacing the
opaque premise FULL_EMD_TWO_MODE_INVARIANCE with explicit geometric and
spectral conditions.

The principal conclusions are:

  (1) invariance of a proposed two-level EMD manifold is equivalent to
      tangency of the full EMD evolution vector field;

  (2) on an invariant symplectic CP1 manifold, the complete one-electron clock
      is determined by the pullback Hamiltonian h(u,phi);

  (3) rigid two-level phase evolution is equivalent to h being affine in the
      population coordinate and independent of the relative phase;

  (4) a raw two-Fourier-mode ansatz is generically not invariant under
      self-consistent Maxwell or Einstein backreaction, because bilinear
      sources generate difference modes and the field-matter coupling then
      generates sidebands;

  (5) exact physical clock synchronization therefore requires a dressed
      nonlinear invariant CP1 manifold, not merely a linear span of two free
      modes;

  (6) leakage and detuning admit explicit finite-time error bounds.

The reduced QR-TT clock theorem remains exact on its frozen reduced orbit.  The
full sourced Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac realization remains conditional on the
explicit tangency and dressed-orbit endpoints below.


0. FROZEN ONE-ELECTRON SETTING
------------------------------

Let

  P_EMD

be a sufficiently regular Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac phase space after one fixed
3+1 gauge choice.  A point contains the gravitational Cauchy data, Maxwell
Cauchy data, and a Dirac spinor:

  X = (h,K,A,E,Psi).

Let

  F_EMD : P_EMD -> T P_EMD

be the gauge-fixed EMD evolution vector field.  Assume local uniqueness for the
corresponding evolution equation

  dX/dt = F_EMD(X).

Freeze the one-electron sector by

  N_Sigma[Psi] = 1,
  Q_Sigma = -e.

Let

  O_1e := CP1

be a proposed reduced one-electron clock manifold, and let

  iota_1e : O_1e -> P_EMD

be a smooth embedding whose image lies in the one-electron constraint surface.
The embedding is allowed to be nonlinear:

  iota_1e(z)
    = (h_z,K_z,A_z,E_z,Psi_z).

Thus the metric and electromagnetic fields may depend self-consistently on the
projective matter coordinate z.

On the standard nonpolar chart use

  u in (0,1),
  phi in R/(2 pi Z),

and normalize

  omega_FS = du wedge dphi,
  integral_CP1 omega_FS = 2 pi.

Assume the one-electron pullback symplectic form is

  iota_1e^* Omega_EMD = hbar omega_FS.


1. TANGENCY IS EQUIVALENT TO INVARIANCE
---------------------------------------

Choose a smooth splitting along the embedded manifold

  T_{iota_1e(z)} P_EMD
    = image(d iota_1e(z)) direct_sum N_z,

and let

  Pi_z^perp

be the projection onto N_z.

Define the normal evolution residual

  R_tan(z)
    := Pi_z^perp F_EMD(iota_1e(z)).

THEOREM EMD_EMBEDDED_ORBIT_TANGENCY_IFF_INVARIANCE.

Assume local uniqueness of the EMD flow.  Then the following are equivalent:

  (i)  every EMD trajectory beginning in iota_1e(O_1e) remains in that image
       on its interval of existence;

  (ii) F_EMD is tangent to the image at every point;

  (iii) R_tan(z)=0 for every z in O_1e.

DERIVATION.

If the image is invariant, differentiating an embedded trajectory shows that
its velocity belongs to image(d iota_1e), hence R_tan=0.  Conversely, if the
vector field is tangent, it defines a vector field X_1e on O_1e by

  d iota_1e(X_1e)=F_EMD o iota_1e.

The embedded integral curve of X_1e and the ambient EMD integral curve have the
same initial data and velocity equation.  Local uniqueness makes them equal.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

The condition is exact but does not itself construct iota_1e or prove the
required well-posedness class.


2. PULLBACK HAMILTONIAN
-----------------------

Assume P_EMD is symplectic on the reduced gauge-fixed sector, with symplectic
form Omega_EMD and Hamiltonian H_EMD, and assume the tangency condition above.
Define

  h_1e := H_EMD o iota_1e.

THEOREM EMD_PULLBACK_HAMILTONIAN_FLOW.

The induced vector field X_1e on O_1e is the Hamiltonian vector field of h_1e
with respect to

  Omega_1e := iota_1e^*Omega_EMD.

DERIVATION.

For every tangent vector Y on O_1e,

  Omega_1e(X_1e,Y)
    = Omega_EMD(d iota_1e X_1e,d iota_1e Y)
    = Omega_EMD(X_{H_EMD},d iota_1e Y)
    = dH_EMD(d iota_1e Y)
    = dh_1e(Y).

Nondegeneracy of Omega_1e identifies X_1e with X_{h_1e}.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.


3. EXACT POPULATION AND PHASE EQUATIONS
---------------------------------------

Write

  h_1e = h_1e(u,phi).

With

  Omega_1e = hbar du wedge dphi

and convention

  i_{X_h} Omega_1e = dh,

THEOREM EMD_TWO_MODE_POPULATION_PHASE_EQUATIONS.

The induced EMD clock satisfies

  dot(u)
    = (1/hbar) partial_phi h_1e,

  dot(phi)
    = -(1/hbar) partial_u h_1e.

DERIVATION.

For

  X_h = dot(u) partial_u + dot(phi) partial_phi,

one has

  i_{X_h}(hbar du wedge dphi)
    = hbar dot(u) dphi - hbar dot(phi) du.

Equating this with

  dh_1e
    = partial_u h_1e du
      + partial_phi h_1e dphi

gives the formulas.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.


4. RIGID TWO-LEVEL CLOCK CRITERION
----------------------------------

Fix a nonzero target energy gap

  Delta_E := E_1-E_0.

THEOREM EMD_RIGID_CLOCK_AFFINE_HAMILTONIAN_IFF.

On a connected nonpolar chart, the following are equivalent:

  (i)  every induced trajectory has constant population u and the same rigid
       relative-phase rate

         dot(phi) = -Delta_E/hbar;

  (ii)

         partial_phi h_1e = 0,
         partial_u h_1e = Delta_E;

  (iii) there exists a constant C such that

         h_1e(u,phi) = C + Delta_E u.

DERIVATION.

Use the exact population and phase equations.  Constant population for every
point is equivalent to partial_phi h_1e=0.  The common phase rate is equivalent
to partial_u h_1e=Delta_E.  Integration on a connected chart gives the affine
form, and the reverse implication is immediate.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

Thus a nonlinear invariant CP1 manifold may still fail to provide a rigid
clock if its pullback Hamiltonian is nonlinear in u or depends on phi.


5. OPERATOR-LEVEL SUFFICIENT GATE
---------------------------------

Let

  V := span_C{Psi_0,Psi_1}

with orthogonal projection P and complement projection Q=I-P.  Suppose the
spinor equation along a reconstructed field configuration has the form

  i hbar partial_t Psi
    = H_eff[Psi] Psi,

where H_eff[Psi] is self-adjoint on the declared domain.

For Psi in V define the two-by-two matrix

  M_ab(Psi)
    := <Psi_a,H_eff[Psi]Psi_b>.

THEOREM EMD_OPERATOR_LEAKAGE_DIAGONAL_GAP_GATE.

Assume for every normalized Psi in V:

  (A) Q H_eff[Psi]Psi = 0;

  (B) M_01(Psi)=M_10(Psi)=0;

  (C) M_11(Psi)-M_00(Psi)=Delta_E,
      independent of Psi.

Then V is invariant for the spinor equation, the modal populations are
constant, and the relative phase satisfies

  dot(phi)=-Delta_E/hbar.

DERIVATION.

Condition (A) removes normal spinor leakage.  Conditions (B) and self-adjointness
make the projected coefficient equation diagonal:

  i hbar dot(c_a)=M_aa(Psi)c_a.

Hence |c_a| is constant.  The relative phase derivative is

  -(M_11-M_00)/hbar=-Delta_E/hbar.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

This theorem controls only the spinor component.  Full EMD invariance also
requires tangency of the Maxwell and gravitational components of iota_1e.


6. RAW TWO-FOURIER-MODE SIDEBAND OBSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------

Consider a flat-background Fourier ansatz on a spatial torus:

  Psi(x)
    = c_0 u_0 exp(i k_0 dot x)
      + c_1 u_1 exp(i k_1 dot x),

where

  k_0 != k_1,
  c_0 c_1 != 0.

Set

  d := k_1-k_0 != 0.

The Dirac current is quadratic:

  j^mu
    = q_e c bar(Psi) gamma^mu Psi.

THEOREM EMD_TWO_FOURIER_MODE_SOURCE_SUPPORT.

The Fourier support of j is contained in

  {0,+d,-d}.

If at least one cross bilinear

  bar(u_0) gamma^mu u_1

is nonzero, then the corresponding current component contains a nonzero mode at
+d and its conjugate mode at -d.

DERIVATION.

Expanding bar(Psi)gamma^mu Psi yields two diagonal terms with zero wave number
and two cross terms proportional to

  exp(+i d dot x),
  exp(-i d dot x).

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

Assume the Maxwell response contains corresponding potential modes

  A_{+d} exp(i d dot x),
  A_{-d} exp(-i d dot x).

THEOREM EMD_TWO_FOURIER_MODE_SIDEBAND_GENERATION.

The product A Psi contains the wave numbers

  k_0,
  k_1,
  2 k_0-k_1,
  2 k_1-k_0.

For k_0!=k_1, the last two are distinct from both k_0 and k_1.  Therefore the
raw two-mode span is not invariant whenever the corresponding sideband
coefficients are nonzero.

DERIVATION.

The +d field mode multiplied by the k_1 matter mode gives

  k_1+d=2k_1-k_0.

The -d field mode multiplied by the k_0 matter mode gives

  k_0-d=2k_0-k_1.

The remaining products return k_0 or k_1.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

The Einstein stress tensor is also bilinear and generates the same difference
modes.  Metric and spin-connection feedback can therefore create the same
sideband lattice.

COROLLARY EMD_RAW_TWO_MODE_GENERIC_NONINVARIANCE.

A self-consistent two-Fourier-mode EMD sector is generically noninvariant.
Exact closure requires at least one of:

  cross-bilinear cancellation;
  vanishing action of the generated field modes on the sideband amplitudes;
  a symmetry-protected consistent truncation;
  an external fixed-field approximation;
  a nonlinear dressed invariant manifold containing the required harmonics.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_SCOPE_STATEMENT.

The word generic refers to the nonvanishing of the explicitly displayed cross
and sideband coefficients.  The theorem does not exclude exceptional
symmetry-protected configurations.


7. DRESSED TWO-LEVEL MANIFOLD
-----------------------------

Define a dressed one-electron clock manifold by a nonlinear embedding

  iota_dress : CP1 -> P_EMD,

  z |-> (h_z,K_z,A_z,E_z,Psi_z),

where the fields and spinor may contain all harmonics required by
self-consistency, while the reduced physical orbit remains two-dimensional.

THEOREM EMD_DRESSED_TWO_LEVEL_CLOCK_GATE.

Assume:

  D1. iota_dress^*Omega_EMD=hbar omega_FS;

  D2. R_tan(z)=0 for every z;

  D3. H_EMD o iota_dress=C+Delta_E u;

  D4. N_Sigma[Psi_z]=1 and Q_Sigma=-e for every z.

Then the full EMD evolution preserves the dressed manifold and induces the
complete rigid clock

  u(t)=u(0),

  phi(t)=phi(0)-Delta_E t/hbar

on every common interval of the ambient and reduced flows.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

This theorem replaces the earlier abstract invariant-two-mode premise by the
explicit tangency and affine-Hamiltonian obligations D1-D4.


8. MATCHING TO THE QR-TT CLOCK
------------------------------

Let the reduced QR-TT orbit have

  Omega_QR=K_QR omega_FS,

  H_QR=C_QR+K_QR Delta_omega u.

Let

  iota_clock(u,phi)=(u,phi+phi_*).

THEOREM EMD_QR_DRESSED_CLOCK_SYNCHRONIZATION.

Under D1-D4, the following conditions

  K_QR=hbar,

  Delta_omega=Delta_E/hbar,

  phi_QR(t_0)=phi_EMD(t_0)+phi_*

imply

  iota_clock o Phi_t^EMD
    = Phi_t^QR o iota_clock

on every common interval of existence.

Equivalently,

  phi_QR(t)=phi_EMD(t)+phi_*

modulo 2pi.

9. APPROXIMATE TANGENCY AND FINITE-TIME PERSISTENCE
---------------------------------------------------

Let X(t) be an exact ambient solution

  dot(X)=F_EMD(X),

and let Y(t)=iota_dress(z(t)) be a reduced approximate trajectory with residual

  r(t):=dot(Y)-F_EMD(Y).

Assume on a common tubular neighborhood:

  ||F_EMD(X)-F_EMD(Y)|| <= L ||X-Y||,

  ||r(t)|| <= delta,

and X(0)=Y(0).

THEOREM EMD_APPROXIMATE_TANGENCY_GRONWALL_BOUND.

For L>0,

  ||X(t)-Y(t)||
    <= (delta/L)(exp(L|t|)-1).

For L=0,

  ||X(t)-Y(t)|| <= delta |t|.

DERIVATION.

The difference Z=X-Y satisfies

  dot(Z)=F_EMD(X)-F_EMD(Y)-r.

Thus

  d||Z||/dt <= L||Z||+delta,

and Gronwall's inequality gives the stated estimate.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

Let Phi be a phase observable that is C_Phi-Lipschitz on the tubular
neighborhood, and let the reduced QR/EMD rate detuning satisfy

  |Delta_omega-Delta_E/hbar| <= eta.

COROLLARY EMD_QR_CLOCK_PERSISTENCE_BOUND.

With matched initial phase,

  dist_S1(phi_QR(t),Phi(X(t)))

  <= eta |t|
     + C_Phi (delta/L)(exp(L|t|)-1)

for L>0, with the evident delta|t| replacement at L=0.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

Thus exact synchronization corresponds to

  delta=0,
  eta=0.

Approximate synchronization has a finite tolerance horizon determined by the
explicit bound.


10. REFINED DEFECT FUNCTIONAL
-----------------------------

Define nonnegative defects

  D_tan
    := sup_z ||R_tan(z)||^2,

  D_aff
    := sup_z
       [ |partial_phi h_1e(z)|^2
         + |partial_u h_1e(z)-Delta_E|^2 ],

  D_action
    := |K_QR-hbar|^2/hbar^2,

  D_rate
    := |Delta_omega-Delta_E/hbar|^2/omega_*^2,

  D_phase
    := dist_S1(phi_QR(t_0),phi_EMD(t_0)+phi_*)^2.

Define

  D_total
    := D_tan+D_aff+D_action+D_rate+D_phase.

THEOREM EMD_QR_CLOCK_DEFECT_ZERO_IFF_EXPLICIT_GATES.

  D_total=0

if and only if the tangency, affine-Hamiltonian, action, rate, and initial-phase
matching gates all hold.

For tau>0,

  Q_tau := exp(-tau D_total)

converges pointwise to 1 on exact synchronized data and to 0 on every fixed
data set with nonzero defect.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

This scalar filter is not a quantum projector until a self-adjoint operator
realization and spectral hypotheses are supplied.


11. ROOT ENDPOINT
-----------------

THEOREM ONE_ELECTRON_EMD_QR_CLOCK_CLOSURE_REFINED.

Assume:

  H1. a locally unique gauge-fixed one-electron EMD evolution;

  H2. a dressed embedding iota_dress:CP1->P_EMD satisfying D1-D4;

  H3. the explicit reduced QR-TT clock orbit;

  H4. action matching K_QR=hbar;

  H5. spectral matching Delta_omega=Delta_E/hbar;

  H6. matched initial phase modulo a fixed offset phi_*.

Then the dressed one-electron EMD orbit is invariant, its induced Hamiltonian
is a rigid two-level clock, and

  iota_clock o Phi_t^EMD
    = Phi_t^QR o iota_clock

on every common interval of existence.

A raw linear span of two distinct Fourier modes does not generally satisfy H2,
because the self-consistent EMD sources generate explicit sidebands.
