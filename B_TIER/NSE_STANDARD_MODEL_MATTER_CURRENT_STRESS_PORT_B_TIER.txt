SCOPE
-----
Extend the frozen Lie-group base from SDiff(M), the gauge automorphism
algebra, and field-curvature identities to:
  (i) actual Standard Model matter representations;
 (ii) gauge-invariant Yukawa contractions and anomaly test sums;
(iii) on-shell gauge Ward and stress exchange identities;
 (iv) a typed NSE Leray-force adapter;
  (v) an exact current residual that distinguishes the full electron
      current from the projected NSE velocity;
 (vi) electromagnetic energy closure with the missing current stress
      explicitly carried by a separate matter/interaction sector.

The result is a mathematically scoped force-and-conservation interface.
It does not derive NSE as the microscopic Standard Model, identify
classical electron probability current with physical fluid mass
transport, or establish quantum EMD/SM dynamics.

0. FIXED GEOMETRY / CONVENTIONS
-------------------------------
Spatial M=T_L^3, L>0, volume V=L^3; spacetime X=R x M, with
specified flat Lorentz metric and spin structure for the simplest
example, and c=1 in the relativistic Ward identities.
Physical c, hbar, e, m_e, epsilon_0 are retained where needed.

Let K be a compact internal gauge group with Lie algebra k and
an Ad-invariant nondegenerate pairing < , >_k.
Let P->X be a principal K bundle, A a connection, F_A its curvature,
and rho_R a declared representation on associated matter fields.
Assume all fields in the derivations are smooth and periodic,
or have enough Sobolev regularity to justify variations and
integration by parts. All identities are local/on shell as stated.

There are two conceptually different models:
 (A) GAUGE+FLUID REPLACEMENT: fluid is the effective carrier of
     gauge charge; the source is J_fluid+J_res and microscopic
     charged matter removed/replaced in the effective description;
 (B) FULL SM+EXTRA FLUID: microscopic fields remain, so any added
     fluid force must come from a separate invariant interaction
     action, not from counting the same microscopic charge twice.

A choice between (A) and (B) is an explicit model input.

THEOREM SMP-01: SM_MATTER_REPRESENTATION_SIGNATURE.

The local internal Standard Model Lie algebra is

  k_SM=su(3)_C direct_sum su(2)_L direct_sum u(1)_Y.

Fix the convention Q_electric=T3+Y.
Per minimal generation, the classical field types are

  Q_L: (3,2,+1/6),
  u_R: (3,1,+2/3),
  d_R: (3,1,-1/3),
  L_L: (1,2,-1/2),
  e_R: (1,1,-1),
  H:   (1,2,+1/2).

The spinor representations carry chirality and are sections of
spinor bundles tensored with the associated gauge bundles.
Three generations differ by flavor labels and Yukawa couplings.
Minimal SM has no dynamical right-handed neutrino.

The global gauge group may be
  [SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1)]/Gamma
with a compatible integer hypercharge normalization and
Gamma=1,Z2,Z3,Z6; the group form and bundle topologies must
be declared for global rather than local statements.

2. COVARIANT CONNECTION AND ACTION
-----------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-04: COVARIANT_MATTER_DERIVATIVE.

For matter section Phi in representation rho_R,

  D_mu^A Phi = nabla_mu^spin Phi+rho_R(A_mu)Phi,

where the spin term is omitted for scalar fields and coordinate
Christoffel terms are included as appropriate.

With D^A=d+rho(A) convention,

  [D_mu^A,D_nu^A]Phi = rho_R(F_{mu nu})Phi

after subtracting the separate Riemann/spin curvature
commutator when Phi has nontrivial spacetime tensor/spin
indices. Equivalently the complete commutator has
gauge and Riemann curvature components.

For a pure internal associated scalar bundle the displayed
gauge identity is exact without the spin-curvature addition.

Define
  F_A=dA+(1/2)[A wedge A].

The Bianchi identity is
  D^A F_A=0.

3. CURRENT, WARD IDENTITY, STRESS
---------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-06: GAUGE_CURRENT_WARD_IDENTITY.

For a classical matter action S_m[Phi,A,g] that is gauge
invariant under

  delta_xi Phi =rho(xi)Phi,
  delta_xi A =-D^A xi,

define its current J by the consistent source convention
in the first variation

  delta S_m
   =int sqrt(|g|)
      [<E_Phi,delta Phi>+<J^mu,delta A_mu>
       +(1/2)T_m^{mu nu}delta g_{mu nu}].

Under a compactly supported infinitesimal gauge
transformation, integration by parts gives

  0= <E_Phi,rho(xi)Phi>
       +<D_mu^A J^mu,xi>

with the pairing signs fixed by the chosen dual representation.
Therefore

  E_Phi=0 implies D_mu^A J^mu=0

for total gauge current, subject to gauge/anomaly premises.

For nonabelian currents, D_mu J^mu=0 means *covariant*,
not componentwise ordinary, conservation. J^a by itself
is gauge-covariant, not gauge-invariant.

Yukawa terms can exchange currents between fermionic
species; only the appropriate TOTAL gauge current obeys
the closed gauge Ward identity.

THEOREM SMP-07: DIFFEOMORPHISM_STRESS_WARD_IDENTITY.

For S_m[Phi,A,g] invariant under diffeomorphisms,
assume the matter E-L equations and absence of extra
spatially varying external scalar sources or anomalies.
Using the gauge-covariant Lie derivative of A,

  Lie_X A=i_X F_A+D^A(i_X A),

and the gauge Ward identity to remove the pure
gauge term, metric variation yields

  nabla_mu T_m^{mu}{}_{nu}
    =<F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k.

If spacetime-dependent scalar sources or anomalous
terms are present, their Ward contributions must be
added explicitly.

THEOREM SMP-08: YANG_MILLS_STRESS_EXCHANGE.

For the Yang-Mills field with equation
  D_mu F^{mu nu}=J^nu
in the chosen source convention and invariant pairing,
the symmetric gauge stress satisfies

  nabla_mu T_YM^{mu}{}_{nu}
    =-<F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k.

Thus
  nabla_mu (T_YM^{mu}{}_{nu}+T_m^{mu}{}_{nu})=0
for the closed source theory, with correctly accounted
interaction contributions.

The force 4-vector density
  Q_nu=<F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k
is gauge-invariant after the invariant internal pairing.
For U(1), this is the ordinary Lorentz force density.

4. NSE FORCE PORT AND ELECTROMAGNETIC TEST
-------------------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-09: TYPED_NSE_FORCE_PORT.

Suppose a particular field-to-fluid matching prescription
C provides a smooth real spatial force density f_i(x,t)
for each incompressible layer i. Define

  ForcePort_C(L,fields,u_i):=P_sigma f_i,

where P_sigma is the orthogonal Leray projection to
divergence-free vector fields on M. The dynamical equation is

  rho_i [partial_t u_i+(u_i dot grad)u_i]
    =-grad p_i+eta_i Delta u_i
      +f_i-sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j).

Then
  partial_t u_i
   =-P_sigma((u_i dot grad)u_i)
      +(eta_i/rho_i)Delta u_i
      +(1/rho_i)P_sigma f_i
      -(1/rho_i)sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j).

This is an exact equivalence between the NSE pressure and
projected forms FOR THE SPECIFIED BODY FORCE.
It is not a unique map L -> f_i; C is essential.

The general covariant force density
  <F^{nu}{}_{mu},J^mu>_k
must be converted into nonrelativistic variables with
specified densities and a hydrodynamic limit before
feeding into the incompressible NSE forcing term.

THEOREM SMP-10: ABELIAN_LORENTZ_POWER_IDENTITY.

For electromagnetic fields E,B and a charged incompressible
fluid with a smooth charge density rho_q and its assigned
transport current J_fluid=rho_q u, set

  f_fluid=rho_q E+J_fluid cross B.

Then
  u dot f_fluid=J_fluid dot E,

because u dot (u cross B)=0.

On a periodic box, Maxwell's Poynting theorem is

  dot E_EM =-int E dot J_source dx,

for the TOTAL source J_source used in Maxwell equations.
For a field-coupled fluid to conserve electric charge require

  partial_t rho_q + div J_fluid=0

for each closed charge-carrier species, up to specified
conversion/interaction terms. On a compact torus, total
charge must be zero or a physically modeled compensating
background is required by Gauss law.

5. ELECTRON CURRENT ADAPTER RESIDUAL
-------------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-11: ELECTRON_TO_NSE_CURRENT_RESIDUAL.

Let psi be a normalized Pauli/Dirac electron state, with
nonvanishing number density n_psi>0 and physical electric
charge q_e=-e. Let j_num be its number current and

  v_psi := j_num/n_psi,
  J_e :=q_e j_num=q_e n_psi v_psi.

Define a mean-zero divergence-free NSE velocity via the
previously frozen projection:

  a_psi:=P_{sigma,0}v_psi.

Define the residual electric current by

  J_res:=J_e-q_e n_psi a_psi
        =q_e n_psi(I-P_{sigma,0})v_psi.

Then exactly

  J_e=q_e n_psi a_psi + J_res.

No assumption that J_res=0 is justified by projection.
When n_psi is spatially constant, J_res contains the
removed mean velocity and (if present) a longitudinal
component. With nonconstant density, multiplying
P_sigma v by n_psi need not preserve charge continuity.

This is a necessary additional port for any Maxwell
source meant to be exactly the electron current.

THEOREM SMP-12: HELICAL_ELECTRON_RESIDUAL_EXACT.

For the previously frozen one-electron helical state

  psi_e(t,z)
    = (1/sqrt(2L^3)) *
      (1,exp(i(kz+alpha-omega_e t)))^T,

  omega_e=hbar*k^2/(2m_e),
  U_e=hbar*k/(2m_e),
  s_t=(cos(kz+alpha-omega_e t),
       sin(kz+alpha-omega_e t),0),

its full Pauli number-current velocity is

  v_e(t,z)=U_e e_3-U_e s_t,

with constant number density
  n_e=1/L^3.

Its mean-zero projected NSE velocity at t=0 is
  a_e=-U_e s_0.

The first exact current residual is already nonzero:

  J_e(0)-q_e n_e a_e
    =q_e n_e U_e e_3.

In the laboratory frame, compare the full electron
current to the FULL mean-plus-fluctuation NSE solution

  u_NSE(t,z)=U_e e_3
              -U_e exp(-nu*k^2*t)s_t.

The phase frequencies agree because omega_e=kU_e.
However

  J_e(t)-q_e n_e u_NSE(t)
    =q_e n_e U_e
       (exp(-nu*k^2*t)-1)s_t.

This residual vanishes at t=0, but is NONZERO for
t>0 whenever nu>0 and U_e !=0.

Hence the free-electron current is not the viscously
decaying NSE current for all times. The residual
must be assigned to a microscopic matter, interaction,
or reservoir channel, or a different open-system
electron dynamics must be constructed.

6. EXACT POWER-RESIDUAL CLOSURE
--------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-13: ELECTROMAGNETIC_NSE_CURRENT_POWER_RESIDUAL.

For a Maxwell gauge field sourced by

  J_source =sum_i J_fluid,i +J_res,

and fluid forces
  f_i =rho_q,i E+J_fluid,i cross B,

smooth periodic energy balance gives

  dot E_EM =-int E dot(sum_i J_fluid,i+J_res)dx,

  dot E_fluid
    =-D_visc-D_drag
       +int E dot sum_i J_fluid,i dx,

where
  D_visc=sum_i eta_i||grad u_i||_2^2,
  D_drag=sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2.

Consequently,

  dot(E_EM+E_fluid)
    =-D_visc-D_drag
      -int E dot J_res dx.

If separate sectors satisfy

  dot E_thermal =D_visc+D_drag,
  dot E_extra   =int E dot J_res dx,

then exactly

  dot(E_EM+E_fluid+E_thermal+E_extra)=0.

D_visc+D_drag is nonnegative. The residual-current
power can have EITHER SIGN, so the extra matter sector
must not be mislabeled as a positive heat reservoir
without independent constitutive proof.

This theorem is an algebraic conservation identity from
the stated Maxwell and fluid energy equations; it does
not construct a fundamental microscopic interaction
action for E_extra.

7. TWO PHYSICALLY DISTINCT INSERTION PATHS
-------------------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-14: NO_DOUBLE_COUNTING_PARTITION.

PATH A - EFFECTIVE CHARGED-FLUID REPLACEMENT:
  Choose charged fluid currents as effective carriers
  replacing coarse-grained matter currents, and
  keep any unrepresented current J_res in a separately
  modeled sector. Maxwell source is
    J_source=J_fluid+J_res.
  The full microscopic matter energy is NOT counted again
  unless the coarse-grained subtraction is explicit.

PATH B - FULL SM PLUS EXTRA FLUID:
  Retain full microscopic S_SM, including all matter
  currents/stresses. Add an independent interaction action
    S_int[Phi,A,g,u_i,...]
  that is gauge/diffeomorphism invariant.
  Derive exchange currents Q_i^nu by variation, and
  include interaction stress/reservoir as required.
  Do not simply append F J_SM to the fluid while
  retaining unchanged SM stress, because this would
  count the same exchange twice.

Both paths are valid TYPED designs. Neither derives
a unique physical matching without its constitutive
or interaction-action data.

8. COVARIANT NONABELIAN EXTENSION
----------------------------------
THEOREM SMP-15: NONABELIAN_GAUGE_INVARIANT_FORCE.

For a compact gauge Lie algebra with invariant pairing,

  f_nu^YM:=<F_{nu mu},J^mu>_k

is gauge invariant provided F and J transform in the
adjoint/coadjoint representations, with pairing
invariant under Ad.

The current conservation law is D_mu^A J^mu=0,
not partial_mu J^a mu=0 in arbitrary gauge.
Nonabelian fluid charge densities require their own
gauge-covariant transport / Wong or kinetic closure;
there is no universal physical identification with
a real scalar incompressible velocity field.

In the electroweak theory, SU(2)xU(1)_Y is broken by
the Higgs vacuum, and physical electromagnetic charge
and photon fields are the appropriate combinations.
Strong SU(3) color currents cannot be interpreted as
gauge-invariant individual color-charge observables
without additional gauge-invariant constructions.
