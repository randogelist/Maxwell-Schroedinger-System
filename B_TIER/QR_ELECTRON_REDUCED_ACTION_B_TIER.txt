0. FROZEN DATA AND CONVENTIONS
------------------------------

Let

  (M,g)

be an oriented, time-oriented, globally hyperbolic four-dimensional
Lorentzian spin manifold with signature (-,+,+,+).  Let Sigma be a spacelike
Cauchy hypersurface with future unit normal n, induced Riemannian metric h,
and extrinsic curvature convention

  K_ij := -(1/2) L_n h_ij

in unit lapse and zero shift.

Freeze

  q_e := -e < 0,
  m := m_e > 0,
  hbar > 0,
  c > 0,
  G > 0,
  epsilon0 > 0,
  mu0 > 0,
  epsilon0 mu0 c^2 = 1.

Let Psi be a Dirac spinor and A a real U(1) potential.  Define

  F := dA,

  D_mu Psi
    := nabla_mu^S Psi + i q_e A_mu Psi/(hbar c),

  j_e^mu := q_e c bar(Psi) gamma^mu Psi.

Let

  T_mu_nu := T_mu_nu^D + T_mu_nu^EM

be the total Dirac-plus-electromagnetic stress tensor of the frozen
Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac sector.

The coupled equations are

  (i hbar c gamma^mu D_mu - m_e c^2) Psi = 0,

  nabla_mu F^{mu nu} = mu0 j_e^nu,

  nabla_[alpha F_beta_gamma] = 0,

  G_mu_nu = (8 pi G/c^4) T_mu_nu.

The one-electron normalization is

  N_Sigma[Psi]
    := -(1/c) integral_Sigma n_mu
         (c bar(Psi) gamma^mu Psi) dmu_h
     = 1,

and therefore

  Q_Sigma = q_e N_Sigma = -e.

The cosmological constant is frozen to zero in this artifact.


1. QR SOURCE MAP: 3+1 STRESS DECOMPOSITION
------------------------------------------

Define the spatial projector

  h_mu^nu := delta_mu^nu + n_mu n^nu.

Define

  rho_QR := T_mu_nu n^mu n^nu,

  j_i^QR := -h_i^mu n^nu T_mu_nu,

  S_ij^QR := h_i^mu h_j^nu T_mu_nu,

  S_QR := h^{ij} S_ij^QR.

THEOREM EMD_STRESS_3P1_DECOMPOSITION.

The total stress tensor decomposes uniquely as

  T_mu_nu
    = rho_QR n_mu n_nu
      + n_mu j_nu^QR
      + n_nu j_mu^QR
      + S_mu_nu^QR,

where

  n^mu j_mu^QR = 0,
  n^mu S_mu_nu^QR = 0.

DERIVATION.

Insert

  delta_mu^alpha = h_mu^alpha - n_mu n^alpha

on both tensor indices and collect the normal-normal, normal-spatial, and
spatial-spatial components.  The definitions above are exactly the resulting
coefficients.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY.

The electron enters GR through

  (rho_QR, j_QR, S_QR),

not through one scalar mass charge alone.


2. SOURCED QR CONSTRAINTS
-------------------------

Define

  K := tr_h K,

  |K|_h^2 := K_ij K^{ij}.

THEOREM EMD_QR_CONSTRAINTS.

Every sufficiently regular Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac solution induces on every
Cauchy hypersurface the constraints

  R(h) + K^2 - |K|_h^2
    = (16 pi G/c^4) rho_QR,

  D^j(K_ij - K h_ij)
    = (8 pi G/c^4) j_i^QR.

DERIVATION.

The first identity is the double-normal projection of the Einstein equation
combined with the Gauss equation.  The second is the normal-spatial projection
combined with the Codazzi equation.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_CONDITIONAL_ON_SUFFICIENT_REGULARITY.

Define the residuals

  C_H
    := R(h)+K^2-|K|_h^2-(16 pi G/c^4)rho_QR,

  C_M_i
    := D^j(K_ij-Kh_ij)-(8 pi G/c^4)j_i^QR.

THEOREM EMD_QR_CONSTRAINT_PROPAGATION.

Assume the Einstein evolution equations, the Dirac and Maxwell equations, and
sufficient regularity.  If

  C_H = 0,
  C_M = 0

on one Cauchy hypersurface, then the constraints vanish throughout the domain
of dependence.

DERIVATION SPINE.

  Dirac + Maxwell
    -> nabla_mu T^{mu nu}=0;

  contracted Bianchi
    -> nabla_mu G^{mu nu}=0;

  evolution equations
    -> a homogeneous first-order propagation system for (C_H,C_M).

Zero initial residuals imply zero propagated residuals by uniqueness.

4. REDUCED QR ACTION COORDINATE
-------------------------------

PREMISE QR_REDUCED_ORBIT_EXISTENCE.

There exists a reduced gravitational phase space

  P_GR^red

with symplectic form

  Omega_GR^red,

and an oriented embedded closed two-cycle

  iota_QR : O_QR -> P_GR^red,

where

  O_QR is diffeomorphic to CP1.

Freeze the orientation by requiring positive symplectic area.

Define

  K_QR
    := (1/(2 pi)) integral_{O_QR} iota_QR^*Omega_GR^red.

THEOREM QR_REDUCED_ACTION_COORDINATE.

  [K_QR] = action.

Moreover K_QR is invariant under orientation-preserving reparametrization of
O_QR and under symplectomorphisms of the reduced phase space.

DERIVATION.

A symplectic form has action dimension.  Integration over a dimensionless
closed two-cycle preserves that dimension.  Invariance follows from pullback
functoriality and change of variables.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_CONDITIONAL_ON_QR_REDUCED_ORBIT_EXISTENCE.

TOPOLOGICAL AUDIT.

The unreduced ADM cotangent symplectic form is exact and has zero integral over
closed two-cycles.  Therefore a nonzero K_QR must arise after genuine
reduction, from boundary/edge data, or from another non-exact reduced sector.


5. QR PREQUANTIZATION INTEGER
-----------------------------

PREMISE QR_PREQUANTUM_LINE_BUNDLE.

There exists a Hermitian line bundle

  L_QR -> O_QR

with unitary connection whose curvature satisfies

  F_nabla = -(i/hbar) iota_QR^*Omega_GR^red.

THEOREM QR_PREQUANTIZATION_INTEGER.

Under the premise,

  m_QR
    := K_QR/hbar
     = (1/(2 pi hbar))
       integral_{O_QR} iota_QR^*Omega_GR^red

is an integer.  For the positive orientation and nondegenerate symplectic
form,

  m_QR in Z_{>0}.

DERIVATION.

By Chern-Weil theory,

  (i/(2 pi)) integral_{O_QR} F_nabla

is the first Chern number of L_QR on O_QR and therefore belongs to Z.  The
curvature convention gives exactly m_QR.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

SEMANTIC SEPARATION.

  m_QR is a symplectic Chern number.

It is not electron number, electric charge, winding of the Dirac phase, or a
photon occupation number.

8. QR GAUSSIAN RIGGING FILTER
-----------------------------

Let

  qfrak := exp(-tau+i phi),

with

  tau>0,
  phi in R/(2 pi Z).

For r in Z define

  Q_qfrak(r)
    := exp(-tau r^2) exp(i phi r).

Then

  |Q_qfrak(r)| = exp(-tau r^2) <= 1.

THEOREM QR_RIGGING_FILTER_POINTWISE_LIMIT.

As tau -> +infinity,

  Q_qfrak(r) -> 1  if r=0,
  Q_qfrak(r) -> 0  if r!=0.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

OPERATOR FORM.

Let R_hat be a self-adjoint operator with integer spectrum.  Define

  Q_qfrak(R_hat)
    := exp(-tau R_hat^2) exp(i phi R_hat)

by functional calculus.

THEOREM QR_RIGGING_FILTER_PROJECTOR_LIMIT.

  ||Q_qfrak(R_hat)|| <= 1,

and, as tau -> +infinity,

  Q_qfrak(R_hat)

converges strongly to the spectral projector

  1_{ {0} }(R_hat).

DERIVATION.

On every spectral value r in Z the multiplier converges to delta_{r0} and is
uniformly bounded by one.  Dominated convergence in the spectral measure gives
strong convergence.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_CONDITIONAL_ON_R_HAT.

INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY.

For finite tau this is a bounded spectral filter, not an exact projector and
not a dynamical law.


9. DISCRETE QR LENGTH SPECTRUM
------------------------------

PREMISE QR_GAUSSIAN_ACTION_LAW(kappa_GR,epsilon).

For constants

  kappa_GR>0,
  epsilon>0,

one has

  iota_QR^*Omega_GR^red
    = kappa_GR (c^3 epsilon^2/G) omega_FS.

Since integral omega_FS=2pi,

  K_QR
    = kappa_GR c^3 epsilon^2/G.

THEOREM QR_LENGTH_SPECTRUM_FROM_PREQUANTIZATION.

If QR_PREQUANTUM_LINE_BUNDLE also holds, then

  kappa_GR c^3 epsilon^2/G
    = m_QR hbar,

and therefore

  epsilon
    = sqrt(m_QR/kappa_GR) l_P,

where

  l_P := sqrt(G hbar/c^3).

STATUS = CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

Thus, for fixed independently derived kappa_GR, prequantization produces the
conditional length ladder

  epsilon_m = sqrt(m/kappa_GR) l_P,
  m=1,2,3,... .

NONSELECTION AUDIT.

The ladder is not predictive if kappa_GR is adjustable or if the reduction
level is freely chosen.  kappa_GR must be derived independently from the QR
geometry.
