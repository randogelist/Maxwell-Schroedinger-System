PURPOSE
-------
Identify the actual continuity/operator structure within NSE^X
corresponding to the FORM of the Standard Model covariant current
Ward identity

    D_mu^A J^mu = 0

while distinguishing:
 (i) ordinary Abelian continuity from nonabelian covariant continuity;
(ii) transported conserved quantities from NSE incompressibility;
(iii) graph exchange of CHARGE from graph exchange of MOMENTUM;
 (iv) Ward conservation alone from a microscopic current identity;
  (v) structurally gauge-covariant charge transport from the actual
      Standard Model matter-action/quantum Ward theorem.

SM-01 through SM-06 remain frozen. SM-08 remains frozen for its
later independent stress-energy-exchange derivation.

0. DOMAINS, TYPES, AND PHYSICAL BOUNDARIES
------------------------------------------
M=T_L^3 periodic flat spatial torus, L>0.
X={1,...,N}, finite nonempty.
For each i, u_i(t,x) is a smooth real solenoidal NSE velocity:
  div u_i=0.
Smoothness of u_i may be obtained through one of the PREVIOUS
regularity classes (common 2D3C, common Beltrami, small H1/2
global strong solution, or large regular-core perturbation).
Its proof is not repeated or inferred from current conservation.

For each i fix:
  q_i(t,x): smooth real scalar transported charge density,
  d_i>=0: charge diffusion coefficient,
  gamma_ij=gamma_ji>=0, gamma_ii=0: reciprocal charge-exchange rates.

Crucially d_i and gamma_ij are INDEPENDENT of:
  nu_i: fluid kinematic viscosity,
  kappa_ij: momentum-exchange graph conductances.

Nothing in the NSE Lie algebra equates these transport coefficients.

1. SINGLE-LAYER CURRENT CONSERVATION
-------------------------------------
THEOREM NCW-01: INCOMPRESSIBLE_SCALAR_CURRENT_IDENTITY.

Define
  J_i := q_i u_i - d_i grad q_i.

The local charge equation

  partial_t q_i+u_i dot grad q_i
       -d_i Delta q_i = s_i

is equivalent, because div u_i=0, to

  partial_t q_i + div J_i = s_i.

Writing J_i^mu=(q_i,J_i) gives

  partial_mu J_i^mu=s_i.

For the closed single-layer sector s_i=0, the exact ordinary
Abelian Ward-form continuity identity is

  partial_mu J_i^mu=0.

This is a TRANSPORT EQUATION, not a consequence of
NSE velocity incompressibility alone for arbitrary q_i.

The constant density q_i=q_const and d_i=0 case gives
  div(q_const u_i)=0
directly from div u_i=0, but contains no additional
physical charge dynamics.

STATUS = EXACT B_TIER FOR THE DECLARED SCALAR TRANSPORT PDE.

THEOREM NCW-02: MATERIAL_DERIVATIVE_COMMUTATOR.

Let D_{t,u}=partial_t+u dot grad on smooth functions.
For multiplication M_q by a smooth scalar q,

  [D_{t,u}, M_q]
     =M_{D_{t,u} q}

on smooth test functions. The computation follows from
the product rule:
  D_{t,u}(q f)-q D_{t,u} f=(D_{t,u}q)f.

If div u=0, then the time-dependent volume form is preserved
by u and material scalar transport q'= -u dot grad q
is equivalent to local current continuity.

This is an exact TRANSPORT OPERATOR relation, not
a microscopic Noether/Ward quantum operator identity.

2. RECIPROCAL CHARGE-GRAPH CONSERVATION
---------------------------------------
THEOREM NCW-03: NSE_POWER_X_TOTAL_ABELIAN_WARD.

Define the charge graph
  (L_gamma q)_i=sum_j gamma_ij(q_i-q_j),
gamma_ij=gamma_ji>=0.

Assume for each i

  partial_t q_i+div(q_i u_i-d_i grad q_i)
      =-(L_gamma q)_i.                                   (C1)

Sum over i. Because gamma_ij is symmetric, every
unordered edge contributes opposite transfer to
its two endpoints:

  sum_i (L_gamma q)_i=0
pointwise in space and time.

Define the total charge density and spatial current
  Q=sum_i q_i,
  J=sum_i (q_i u_i-d_i grad q_i).

Then EXACTLY

  partial_t Q+div J=0,
  partial_mu J_total^mu=0.                               (C2)

On periodic M,

  d/dt int_M Q dx=0.

This is conservation of the TOTAL transported charge,
even though individual layer charges may exchange.

STATUS = EXACT B_TIER.

THEOREM NCW-04: CHARGE_GRAPH_QUADRATIC_DISSIPATION.

For smooth real charges satisfying (C1), integrate
the i-th equation against q_i, sum, and use
div u_i=0 and periodic integration by parts:

  (1/2) d/dt sum_i ||q_i||_2^2
   = -sum_i d_i||grad q_i||_2^2
     -sum_{i<j}gamma_ij||q_i-q_j||_2^2 <=0.

This is dissipation of CHARGE-DENSITY L2 VARIANCE.
It is NOT decay of the conserved total signed charge
int Q and is not the NSE kinetic-energy dissipation.

If q_i can have either sign the L2 estimate remains
valid; positivity preservation of each q_i additionally
depends on the parabolic/graph maximum principle and
appropriate positivity of the initial charges.

3. GAUGE-COVARIANT NONABELIAN EXTENSION
----------------------------------------
THEOREM NCW-05: COVARIANT_ADJOINT_CURRENT_PORT.

Let K be a compact Lie group with algebra k and an
Ad-invariant positive-definite inner product on k.
Fix a TRIVIAL principal K bundle over spacetime or
work in a local trivialization of a general bundle.
Let A_mu be a smooth connection, and let all charge
densities q_i be sections of the SAME adjoint bundle,
so sums and graph differences are meaningful.

Define
  D_mu^A q = partial_mu q+[A_mu,q].

For each i define an adjoint-valued four-current

  J_i^0=q_i,
  J_i^a=u_i^a q_i-d_i D_a^A q_i,

where u_i^a are real divergence-free velocity components.

Assume the declared gauge-covariant transport equation

  D_mu^A J_i^mu=-(L_gamma q)_i.                          (C3)

Then by summing over i,

  D_mu^A J_total^mu=0,
  J_total^mu=sum_i J_i^mu.                              (C4)

These equalities transform covariantly under local
K gauge transformations, since the connection,
adjoint q_i, and J_i transform consistently and
gamma_ij are gauge-singlet real coefficients.

On expanding (C3), each layer satisfies

  D_t^A q_i +u_i dot D^A q_i
     -d_i D_a^A D_a^A q_i
     =-(L_gamma q)_i

when div u_i=0.

This gives the exact COVARIANT FORM of the SM-07 Ward
identity, but as a declared CLASSICAL ADJOINT FLUID
transport law, not as a theorem derived from the
Standard Model quantum action.

Different quark/lepton representations cannot be
summed naively as adjoint charges; a prescribed
representation-to-current intertwiner or physically
defined total source current is required.

THEOREM NCW-06: GAUGE_COVARIANT_CHARGE_GRAPH_NORM.

Under (C3), with the same invariant inner product
for all adjoint charges and compact periodic M,

  (1/2)d/dt sum_i||q_i||_2^2
   =-sum_i d_i||D^A q_i||_2^2
    -sum_{i<j}gamma_ij||q_i-q_j||_2^2 <=0.

Proof: the adjoint action ad(A_mu) is skew relative
to an invariant pairing, so
  <q_i,D_t^A q_i>=(1/2)partial_t |q_i|^2,
  <q_i,u_i dot D^A q_i>=(1/2)u_i dot grad |q_i|^2;
integrate over periodic M using div u_i=0.
Covariant integration by parts handles the Laplacian.
Graph positivity follows from reciprocal gamma_ij.

This is a GAUGE-INVARIANT quadratic norm
calculation for an adjoint-valued classical
transport model. It does not establish
quantum SM BRST, confinement, or physical
particle flux/charge measurements.

4. EXACT ELECTRON CURRENT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO UNIQUENESS
------------------------------------------------------
THEOREM NCW-07: HELICAL_WARD_NONUNIQUENESS.

Let
  k=2*pi*n/L !=0,
  omega_e=hbar*k^2/(2m_e),
  U_e=hbar*k/(2m_e),
  theta(t,z)=kz+alpha-omega_e t,
  s_t=(cos(theta),sin(theta),0),
  n_e=L^(-3), q_e=-e.

The frozen free Pauli electron spinor has constant
number density n_e and full probability-current
velocity

  v_e(t)=U_e e_z-U_e s_t.

Its physical electric current is
  J_e=q_e n_e v_e.

The frozen smooth full mean-plus-fluctuation
NSE velocity at viscosity nu>0 is

  u_NSE(t)=U_e e_z-U_e exp(-nu*k^2*t)s_t,

with the current-like fluid readout
  J_NSE=q_e n_e u_NSE.

Because each s_t has zero z-component and depends
only on z,

  div s_t=0,  div v_e=0, div u_NSE=0.

As q_e n_e is constant, BOTH currents satisfy
the SAME Abelian Ward form:

  partial_t (q_e n_e)+div J_e=0,
  partial_t (q_e n_e)+div J_NSE=0.

However their difference is

  J_e-J_NSE
    =q_e n_e U_e
       [exp(-nu*k^2*t)-1]s_t.                           (C5)

For t>0, nu>0, k!=0 and U_e!=0 this is nonzero,
while the residual ALSO has zero divergence:

  div(J_e-J_NSE)=0.

THEREFORE EVEN EXACT COMMON CHARGE CONTINUITY
DOES NOT IDENTIFY THE PHYSICAL ELECTRON CURRENT
WITH THE DISSIPATIVE NSE CURRENT.

This is a concrete nonuniqueness obstruction:
a Ward equation constrains divergence but not
the entire solenoidal current or its time
evolution.

6. IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE FROM MOMENTUM GRAPH
--------------------------------------------
THEOREM NCW-10: CHARGE_GRAPH_MOMENTUM_GRAPH_SEPARATION.

NSE momentum drag:
  (L_kappa u)_i=sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j).

Charge transport/exchange:
  (L_gamma q)_i=sum_j gamma_ij(q_i-q_j).

Both operators are positive symmetric weighted
graph Laplacians, but they act on DIFFERENT
spaces and encode different conserved
quantities and constitutive coefficients.

Reciprocal momentum graph alone does NOT
prove SM-07 local charge conservation:
charge density fields, currents and equations
must also be present.

Converse: local charge Ward identity alone
does NOT prove the fluid kinetic-energy
identity or a global PDE regularity theorem.
