This document identifies the minimum additional structure required to assign
an exact Navier-Stokes time scale and a physical kinetic-energy scale to the
frozen synchronized one-electron QM-Maxwell-GR reduced object.

The decisive distinction is:

  the existing merged CP1 evolution is Hamiltonian and phase preserving;

  viscous Navier-Stokes evolution is dissipative and requires an additional
  radial amplitude coordinate.

The exact special-mode chain is

  circular transverse profile
  + physical torus length L
  + viscosity nu
  + density rho_f
  + optional constant transport velocity U0
  -> exact advected circular NSE solution
  -> phase rate omega_NSE = k U0
  -> viscous decay rate gamma_NSE = nu k^2
  -> exact kinetic-energy decay law
  -> contractive phase-radius semigroup
  -> conditional matching to the merged one-electron clock.

No general equivalence between the full Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac system and the
full Navier-Stokes equation is asserted.


0. FROZEN PHYSICAL DATA
-----------------------

Let the physical spatial torus be

  T_L^3 := (R/LZ)^3,

with volume

  V := L^3.

Fix

  L > 0,
  rho_f > 0,
  nu > 0,
  n in Z with n != 0,
  k := 2 pi n/L,
  epsilon > 0,
  s > 0,
  lambda in R,
  U0 in R.

Here

  rho_f : physical mass density used to convert the NSE L2 norm into energy;
  nu    : kinematic viscosity;
  U0    : constant transport velocity in the z direction.

Define the heat-regularized circular amplitude

  r0
    := |lambda| s exp(-epsilon^2 k^2/4).

The associated transverse profile is

  a_epsilon(z)
    := r0 (cos(kz+alpha), sin(kz+alpha), 0).

It satisfies

  div a_epsilon = 0,
  Delta a_epsilon = -k^2 a_epsilon,
  |a_epsilon(x)| = r0.

The profile is reused as an NSE velocity profile. This is a profile map, not a
semantic identity between the electromagnetic field and fluid velocity.


1. EXACT ADVECTED CIRCULAR NSE SOLUTION
---------------------------------------

Define

  gamma_NSE := nu k^2,
  omega_NSE := k U0.

Let

  v(t,z)
    := r0 exp(-gamma_NSE t)
       ( cos(k(z-U0 t)+alpha),
         sin(k(z-U0 t)+alpha),
         0 ),

and

  u(t,x) := U0 e3 + v(t,z).

THEOREM ADVECTED_CIRCULAR_NSE_SOLUTION.

For constant pressure p, the field u solves the unforced incompressible
Navier-Stokes equation

  partial_t u + (u dot grad)u + grad p = nu Delta u,
  div u = 0

for every t >= 0.

DERIVATION.

The transverse field depends only on z and has zero z component. Therefore

  (v dot grad)v = 0.

The constant mean contributes

  (U0 e3 dot grad)v = U0 partial_z v.

Direct differentiation gives

  partial_t v
    = -nu k^2 v - U0 partial_z v.

Hence

  partial_t u + (u dot grad)u
    = -nu k^2 v
    = nu Delta v
    = nu Delta u.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

MEAN-ZERO SCOPE.

If the NSE carrier is required to have zero spatial mean, set U0=0 or pass to
the comoving frame. Then the exact solution remains valid, but the nontrivial
phase clock disappears and only viscous decay remains.


2. EXACT NSE TIME SCALES
------------------------

The mode has complex temporal exponent

  exp[-(gamma_NSE + i omega_NSE)t].

Define the angular phase time

  T_phase := 1/|omega_NSE|

when omega_NSE != 0, and the full phase period

  P_phase := 2 pi/|omega_NSE|.

Define the amplitude e-folding time

  T_amp := 1/gamma_NSE
         = 1/(nu k^2).

Define the kinetic-energy e-folding time

  T_energy := 1/(2 gamma_NSE)
            = 1/(2 nu k^2).

Using k=2pi n/L,

  T_amp
    = L^2/(4 pi^2 nu n^2),

  T_energy
    = L^2/(8 pi^2 nu n^2),

and, when U0 != 0,

  P_phase
    = L/(|n| |U0|).

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.


3. EXACT NSE ENERGY AND DISSIPATION
-----------------------------------

Define the fluctuation kinetic energy

  E_NSE(t)
    := (rho_f/2) integral_{T_L^3} |v(t,x)|^2 dx.

Because |v| is spatially constant,

  E_NSE(t)
    = (rho_f V/2) r0^2 exp(-2 gamma_NSE t).

Thus

  E_NSE(0)
    = (rho_f L^3/2)
      lambda^2 s^2 exp(-epsilon^2 k^2/2).

The viscous dissipation power is

  P_diss(t)
    := rho_f nu integral |grad v|^2 dx
     = rho_f nu V k^2 r0^2 exp(-2 gamma_NSE t).

Therefore

  P_diss(t) = 2 gamma_NSE E_NSE(t),

  dE_NSE/dt = -P_diss = -2 gamma_NSE E_NSE.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

The total kinetic energy also contains the constant mean contribution

  E_mean = (rho_f V/2) U0^2,

which does not dissipate because grad(U0 e3)=0.


4. MINIMAL DISSIPATIVE EXTENSION OF THE MERGED ORBIT
-----------------------------------------------------

The synchronized QM-Maxwell-GR reduced orbit has constant population and a
rotating phase. It cannot represent viscous amplitude decay without an extra
coordinate.

Define the extended reduced carrier

  O_ext := CP1 x R_{>=0}.

Use coordinates

  (u,phi,r).

Define the phase-radius flow

  Phi_t^ext(u,phi,r)
    := (u,
        phi-omega_NSE t,
        r exp(-gamma_NSE t)).

THEOREM NSE_PHASE_RADIUS_SEMIGROUP.

The maps Phi_t^ext form a continuous semigroup for t>=0. Its Koopman generator
on smooth observables is

  G_NSE
    = -omega_NSE partial_phi
      -gamma_NSE r partial_r.

For the energy observable

  E(r) := (rho_f V/2) r^2,

one has

  G_NSE E = -2 gamma_NSE E.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

The old merged Hamiltonian clock is recovered on the phase factor when

  gamma_NSE=0.

For nu>0 the extended evolution is contractive, not unitary. A unitary
representation would require a dilation by additional reservoir variables.


5. MATCH TO THE ONE-ELECTRON CLOCK
----------------------------------

The frozen merged one-electron clock has angular frequency

  omega_QM := Delta_E/hbar,

where

  Delta_E := E1-E0.

THEOREM NSE_QM_PHASE_CLOCK_MATCHING.

The NSE phase clock and one-electron clock agree if and only if

  k U0 = Delta_E/hbar.

Equivalently,

  U0 = Delta_E/(hbar k).

Under equal initial phases,

  phi_NSE(t)=phi_QM(t)

modulo 2pi on their common interval.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

This matches only the phase rate. The viscous rate

  gamma_NSE=nu k^2

has no counterpart in the present unitary CP1 generator.


6. ENERGY-CALIBRATION OPTIONS
-----------------------------

A physical energy bridge requires one additional normalization law. Neither
NSE nor the existing merged sector determines it automatically.

OPTION A: DIRECT KINETIC/GAP CALIBRATION.

Choose a dimensionless constant eta_E>0 and impose

  E_NSE(0) = eta_E |Delta_E|.

Then

  r0^2
    = 2 eta_E |Delta_E|/(rho_f L^3).

OPTION B: NSE ACTION-SCALE CALIBRATION.

Using the amplitude e-folding time, impose

  E_NSE(0) T_amp = hbar.

Then

  E_NSE(0) = hbar nu k^2,

and

  r0^2
    = 2 hbar nu k^2/(rho_f L^3).

OPTION C: ENERGY-EFOLD CALIBRATION.

Using T_energy instead gives

  E_NSE(0) T_energy = hbar

if and only if

  E_NSE(0)=2 hbar nu k^2.

The factor two is purely the distinction between amplitude and energy
e-folding conventions. A frozen bridge must choose one convention explicitly.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL_B_TIER_CALIBRATION.


7. TRANSLATION OF THE NSE REGULARITY CERTIFICATE
-------------------------------------------------

Suppose the pre-existing sufficient certificate gives

  |lambda| < lambda_crit(epsilon,nu)

for regularity of lambda a_epsilon.

The corresponding physical kinetic-energy radius is

  E_crit^NSE(epsilon,nu)
    := (rho_f L^3/2)
       lambda_crit(epsilon,nu)^2
       s^2 exp(-epsilon^2 k^2/2).

Then

  |lambda| < lambda_crit

implies

  E_NSE(0) < E_crit^NSE.

STATUS = EXACT_CONDITIONAL_B_TIER_TRANSLATION.

This is a one-sided certificate image. It is not a singularity threshold, a
phase transition, or a necessary condition for regularity.


8. FULL DYNAMICAL BRIDGE ENDPOINT
---------------------------------

Let

  S_t^NSE

be the NSE semigroup on the selected exact circular sector, and let

  Phi_t^ext

be the phase-radius semigroup above.

Define the profile readout

  iota_NSE(u,phi,r)
    := U0 e3
       + r( cos(kz+phi), sin(kz+phi), 0 ).

THEOREM CIRCULAR_NSE_SEMIGROUP_INTERTWINING.

For t>=0,

  iota_NSE o Phi_t^ext
    = S_t^NSE o iota_NSE.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

A bridge from the full dressed one-electron EMD orbit requires an additional
map

  j_NSE : P_EMD^reduced -> O_ext

such that

  j_NSE o Phi_t^EMD
    = Phi_t^ext o j_NSE.

This is the open cross-theory endpoint

  EMD_TO_NSE_PHASE_RADIUS_SEMICONJUGACY.

Because NSE is dissipative while the frozen EMD/QR orbit is Hamiltonian, the
appropriate relation is a semiconjugacy or reservoir dilation, not an ordinary
symplectic equivalence.


9. MINIMUM DATA REQUIRED
------------------------

To obtain an NSE-relative time and energy scale, the following must be fixed:

  R1. a physical length L;

  R2. a kinematic viscosity nu;

  R3. a physical density rho_f or an effective total mass convention;

  R4. a selected wave number k=2pi n/L;

  R5. a velocity amplitude r0 or an equivalent energy normalization;

  R6. a choice of NSE clock:
      phase clock, viscous amplitude clock, or energy-decay clock;

  R7. for phase synchronization, the rate equation

        k U0 = Delta_E/hbar;

  R8. for energy synchronization, one explicit calibration law;

  R9. for full dynamical comparison, a dissipative phase-radius extension and
      a semigroup intertwining map.

Without R1-R5, the NSE equations supply only dimensionless or per-unit-density
quantities. Without R6-R9, there is no unique map to the merged electron clock.

THEOREM ONE_ELECTRON_QM_MAXWELL_GR_TO_NSE_TIME_ENERGY_SCALE.

Assume:

  H1. the frozen synchronized one-electron merged clock with

        omega_QM=Delta_E/hbar;

  H3. the heat-regularized circular profile with amplitude r0;

  phase angular time

    T_phase=hbar/|Delta_E|,

  full phase period

    P_phase=2pi hbar/|Delta_E|,

  viscous amplitude time

    T_amp=1/(nu k^2),

    E_NSE(0)=(rho_f L^3/2)r0^2,

and exact energy law

    E_NSE(t)=E_NSE(0)e^{-2nu k^2 t}.

The phase-radius extension gives the exact generator

  G_NSE
    = -(Delta_E/hbar)partial_phi
      -nu k^2 r partial_r.
