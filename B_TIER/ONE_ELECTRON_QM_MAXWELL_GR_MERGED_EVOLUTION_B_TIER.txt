0. FROZEN REDUCED REGIME
------------------------

Let

  O := CP1.

On the standard nonpolar chart write

  u in (0,1),
  phi in R/(2 pi Z),

and normalize

  omega_FS := du wedge dphi,
  integral_O omega_FS = 2 pi.

Freeze the one-electron sector by

  N_Sigma[Psi] = 1,
  Q_Sigma = -e.

Assume a dressed one-electron EMD embedding

  iota_EMD : O -> P_EMD,

  iota_EMD(z)
    = (h_z,K_z,A_z,E_z,Psi_z),

whose image lies in the EMD constraint surface and is invariant under the
chosen gauge-fixed EMD evolution.

Define the three gauge-reduced readouts

  iota_QM(z) := Gamma_z
              := |Psi_z><Psi_z|,

  iota_M(z)  := F_z := dA_z,

  iota_GR(z) := gamma_z
              := the selected reduced diffeomorphism class of (h_z,K_z).

The quantum readout uses the rank-one density operator so that a common global
spinor phase is removed.  The Maxwell readout uses F rather than A so that U(1)
gauge is removed.  The GR readout is taken after the declared gravitational
gauge quotient.

Assume the pullback EMD symplectic form and Hamiltonian are

  iota_EMD^* Omega_EMD = hbar omega_FS,

  h := H_EMD o iota_EMD
     = C + Delta_E u,

with

  Delta_E != 0.

Define

  omega := Delta_E/hbar.

With the convention

  i_{X_h}(hbar omega_FS) = dh,

one obtains

  X_h = -omega partial_phi.

The complete reduced flow is therefore

  Phi_t(u,phi)
    = (u, phi-omega t)

for every real t.

4. COMMON KOOPMAN-LIOUVILLE OPERATOR
------------------------------------

Let

  mu_FS := omega_FS/(2 pi)

be the normalized symplectic area measure on O, and define

  K := L^2(O,mu_FS).

Since Phi_t is Hamiltonian, it preserves mu_FS.  Define the Koopman operators

  (U_merge(t) f)(z)
    := f(Phi_t z).

THEOREM TRISECTOR_KOOPMAN_UNITARY_GROUP.

The family U_merge(t) is a strongly continuous unitary group on K.  On the
smooth invariant core its self-adjoint generator is

  L_merge
    := i X_h
     = -i omega partial_phi,

and

  U_merge(t)
    = exp(-i t L_merge).

DERIVATION.

Measure preservation gives

  ||f o Phi_t||_2 = ||f||_2.

The group law follows from Phi_{t+s}=Phi_t o Phi_s.  Differentiation at t=0
gives

  d/dt|_0 U_merge(t)f
    = X_h f
    = -i L_merge f.

The rotation flow is complete; the closure of -i omega partial_phi on the
periodic angular variable is self-adjoint on its standard domain.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

This is the common operator representation of the merged reduced time
evolution.

IMPORTANT SCOPE.
