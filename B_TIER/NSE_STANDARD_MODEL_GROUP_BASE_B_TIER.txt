PURPOSE
-------
Identify and derive the minimum group, Lie-algebra, representation,
principal-bundle, and positive-dissipation identities that must be
available BEFORE a local Standard Model Lagrangian can be attached to
an NSE layering model.

This is a base-structure theorem, not a claim that Navier-Stokes
implies the Standard Model gauge group, nor that arbitrary Standard
Model matter admits a fluid identification.

FROZEN SEPARATIONS
------------------
(A) Fluid relabeling and incompressible transport: SDiff.
(B) Internal local gauge transformations: Gau(P).
(C) Spacetime diffeomorphisms and spin geometry: different symmetries.
(D) N independent fluid labels: SDiff^N, distinct from one common
    physical spacetime and one common gauge connection.
(E) Field theory supplies a conservative action and Ward identities;
    constitutive transport and dissipation are extra physical inputs.
(F) Smooth NSE is generally a viscous semiflow, not a Lie group of
    time transformations; time reversal fails for positive viscosity.

0. DOMAINS AND TYPES
--------------------
Let M := T_L^3 be a compact oriented Riemannian 3-manifold with
standard flat metric, volume form mu and, when spinors are present,
a fixed spin structure. Let X=R x M be spacetime, with Lorentz
metric or tetrad as extra geometric input when needed.

Let N be a finite positive integer.
Let K be a compact connected matrix Lie group with algebra k.
The Standard Model example is

  k_SM = su(3)_C direct_sum su(2)_L direct_sum u(1)_Y.

A declared global realization is

  K_Gamma =
    (SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1))/Gamma,

  Gamma in {1, Z2, Z3, Z6},

using a compatible integer hypercharge convention.

The local Lie algebra is identical for all four Gamma choices;
the global representation/bundle and line-operator spectrum can
depend on Gamma.

Let P -> M be a principal K-bundle. Its associated adjoint bundle
is ad(P)=P x_Ad k. Its gauge transformations form Gau(P), the
group of automorphisms covering the identity on M.

For each material NSE layer i in {1,...,N}, choose

  rho_i>0,  eta_i>=0,
  u_i in X_sigma(M),

and symmetric kappa_ij=kappa_ji>=0 for all i!=j.

1. NSE GEOMETRY AND LIE ALGEBRA
------------------------------
THEOREM GB01: SDIFF_AND_SOLENOIDAL_LIE_ALGEBRA.

  G_fl := SDiff(M)
        = {phi in Diff(M): phi^*mu=mu}.

Its infinitesimal algebra, using the vector-field commutator
convention, is

  g_fl := {u smooth tangent vector field: div_mu u=0},

  [u,v] := nabla_u v - nabla_v u.

For all u,v,w in g_fl,

  div_mu[u,v] = u dot grad(div_mu v)-v dot grad(div_mu u)=0,
  [u,v]=-[v,u],
  [u,[v,w]]+[v,[w,u]]+[w,[u,v]]=0.

PROOF: D_u:=u dot grad is a derivation on scalar functions and
[D_u,D_v]=D_[u,v]. Differential-operator commutators satisfy
Jacobi. Divergence is the Lie derivative of the volume form;
L_[u,v]mu=[L_u,L_v]mu=0.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

THEOREM GB02: NSE_LIE_SYMMETRY_VS_VISCOUS_SEMIFLOW.

The ideal Euler part of NSE is the L2 Euler-Arnold equation on
SDiff(M); its velocity dynamics is

  partial_t u = -P_sigma((u dot grad)u).

The viscous NSE equation is

  partial_t u
    = -P_sigma((u dot grad)u)+nu Delta u +P_sigma f,

and satisfies

  (d/dt) (1/2)||u||_2^2
    = -nu||grad u||_2^2+<u,f>

for smooth periodic incompressible solutions.

Positive viscosity introduces dissipation, so the forward NSE flow
is not a group of reversible time evolution on its natural energy
state space. Thus SDiff is the configuration symmetry base, not a
claim that the viscous dynamics itself is a Lie group.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

2. MULTILAYER KINEMATICS
------------------------
THEOREM GB03: NSE_N_FOLD_PRODUCT.

  G_mat := SDiff(M)^N
  g_mat := g_fl^N,

with componentwise bracket.

The diagonal embedding

  diag:SDiff(M)->SDiff(M)^N,
  diag(phi)=(phi,...,phi)

is a group homomorphism. For an adapter with a single physical
spatial manifold and a single shared gauge field, the common
physical diffeomorphism action is diagonal.

CAUTION:
Independent material relabelings for each constituent fluid are
NOT N independent physical spacetime coordinate systems. There
is no canonical full SDiff(M)^N action on ONE shared gauge field;
such an action requires explicitly defined separate pullbacks
or a compatible groupoid/fiber-product construction.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

THEOREM GB04: GRAPH_EXCHANGE_OPERATOR.

Define

  (L_kappa u)_i
    := sum_j kappa_ij (u_i-u_j).

Then

  sum_i <u_i,(L_kappa u)_i>
    =sum_{i<j} kappa_ij ||u_i-u_j||_2^2>=0.

For connected interlayer graph,

  ker L_kappa = {(u,...,u):u in g_fl}.

The reciprocal drag force in layer i is -(L_kappa u)_i.
This is a positive symmetric dissipative structure on the
product fluid algebra, NOT an additional internal gauge group.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

3. INTERNAL GAUGE GROUP BASE
----------------------------
THEOREM GB05: SM_INTERNAL_GAUGE_LIE_ALGEBRA.

  k_SM = su(3)+su(2)+u(1)

as a direct sum of Lie algebras, real dimension 8+3+1=12.

The pointwise current gauge algebra for a TRIVIAL bundle is

  g_gauge := C^infty(M,k_SM),

with [xi,eta](x)=[xi(x),eta(x)]_k.

For a general principal bundle,

  g_gauge := Gamma(M,ad(P)).

The current bracket is pointwise and satisfies Jacobi because
the finite-dimensional k bracket does.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

THEOREM GB06: LOCAL_SPATIAL_GAUGE_SEMIDIRECT_BRACKET.

For a trivial bundle and a matrix representation rho of k,
define the first-order differential operator

  D_(u,xi):=u dot grad +rho(xi).

Its commutator is

  [D_(u,xi),D_(v,eta)]
    =D_([u,v], u dot grad eta-v dot grad xi+[xi,eta]).

Thus the bracket

  [(u,xi),(v,eta)]
   :=([u,v],u dot grad eta-v dot grad xi+[xi,eta])

defines a Lie-algebra semidirect structure, with the action
convention fixed by the D_(u,xi) representation.
The corresponding group composition convention must be
chosen compatibly (right/left action and inverse pullback
can reverse the sign of the transport component).

This is a local algebraic bridge; it does NOT identify the
SDiff Lie algebra with su(3), su(2), or u(1).

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

4. NONTRIVIAL BUNDLES: WHY ATIYAH STRUCTURE IS REQUIRED
--------------------------------------------------------
THEOREM GB07: GAUGE_AUTOMORPHISM_EXTENSION.

The principal bundle automorphism group Aut(P) covers only those
base diffeomorphisms that admit a lift preserving the bundle.

With volume-preserving restrictions,

  1 -> Gau(P)
    -> Aut_vol(P)
    -> SDiff(M)_[P]
    -> 1,

where SDiff(M)_[P] is the subgroup of volume-preserving base
diffeomorphisms that have an automorphism lift to P.
This sequence is exact at each displayed term; it is
not asserted that all of SDiff(M) acts when P is nontrivial.

If P is globally trivial, a trivialization supplies a split
semidirect group description.

STATUS: B_TIER FROM STANDARD PRINCIPAL-BUNDLE GEOMETRY.

THEOREM GB08: ATIYAH_ALGEBROID_SEQUENCE.

The Lie algebroid

  At(P):=TP/K

sits in the exact sequence of vector bundles

  0 -> ad(P) -> At(P) -> TM -> 0.

Its sections form the infinitesimal bundle-automorphism
algebra. A connection A provides a VECTOR BUNDLE splitting,
but its horizontal lift does not preserve brackets unless
curvature F_A vanishes.

For the volume-preserving subalgebra restrict the anchor
from TM to solenoidal vector fields (or bundle automorphisms
covering such fields).

STATUS: B_TIER, STANDARD ATIYAH IDENTITY.

THEOREM GB09: CURVATURE_CORRECTION_IN_COVARIANT_BRACKET.

Fix the convention D^A=d+rho(A), so

  [D^A_u,D^A_v]-D^A_[u,v]=rho(F_A(u,v)).

Let xi,eta be adjoint-bundle sections and define

  D_(u,xi)^A:=D^A_u+rho(xi).

Then

  [D_(u,xi)^A,D_(v,eta)^A]
  =D^A_[u,v]
   +rho(F_A(u,v)
        +nabla_u^{ad A}eta
        -nabla_v^{ad A}xi
        +[xi,eta]).

Here

  nabla_u^{ad A}eta=u dot grad eta+[A(u),eta]

in a local trivialization.

This is the necessary curvature-dependent bracket in a
connection splitting; the exact sign of the curvature term
in horizontal-vector-field conventions is convention-dependent.

Dropping F_A would generally invalidate the covariant
commutator and the intended gauge-covariant adapter.

5. MATTER REPRESENTATIONS / VARIATIONAL INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------
THEOREM GB10: REPRESENTATION_BASE.

The local Standard Model Lagrangian is not constructed merely
from the group k_SM. It also requires associated bundles of
matter representations, chirality, a spin structure,
connections, and invariant couplings.

Use the hypercharge convention Q=T3+Y. Per generation,
standard representations include:

  Q_L: (3,2,1/6)
  u_R: (3,1,2/3)
  d_R: (3,1,-1/3)
  L_L: (1,2,-1/2)
  e_R: (1,1,-1)
  H:   (1,2,1/2).

A connection A defines covariant derivatives

  D^A psi = d psi +rho_*(A) psi,

supplemented by the spacetime spin connection for fermions.
Its curvature is

  F_A=dA+1/2[A wedge A],

and satisfies the Bianchi identity

  D^A F_A=0.

Gauge transformations act AFFINELY on connections and
linearly in the appropriate associated-bundle
representation on matter fields.

The spinor/fermionic sector is Z2-graded; physical quantum
fermions require anticommuting (Grassmann) variables or
operator-valued fields. They are not ordinary NSE scalar
advected fields. All gauge/anomaly, spin-structure, and
Yukawa invariance conditions must be audited.

STATUS: B_TIER STRUCTURAL IDENTIFICATION.

THEOREM GB11: WARD_CURRENT_STRESS_PORT.

For an admissible gauge-invariant local field action
S_L[Phi,A,g], with fixed variational sign conventions,
define

  E_Phi := delta S_L/delta Phi,
  J := the current from variation of A,
  T_L := the stress from metric/tetrad variation.

Gauge and diffeomorphism invariance imply their appropriate
on-shell Noether/Ward identities. For a standard Yang-Mills
matter coupling, schematically,

  D_mu J^mu=0,
  nabla_mu T_matter^{mu nu}
     =F^{a nu}{}_mu J_a^mu,

with opposite exchange in T_gauge, subject to the
chosen decomposition and interaction conventions.

IMPORTANT:
One cannot assert these identities unchanged for an
arbitrary non-gauge-invariant Lagrangian, an anomalous
quantum field theory, or a choice of stress tensor that
omits interaction contributions.

STATUS: CONDITIONAL_B_TIER ON ADMISSIBILITY / SYMMETRIES.

6. HODGE PRESSURE, DISSIPATION AND FIELD EXCHANGE
-------------------------------------------------
THEOREM GB12: HODGE_LERAY_PORT.

For incompressible constant-density flow, the physical
body force f_i from the field stress/current interface
enters as P_sigma f_i. Gradient components can be
absorbed into pressure, whereas solenoidal components
change the fluid velocity. For nonuniform density
and relativistic fluids this simple projection is
not the correct universal force law.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

THEOREM GB13: MULTILAYER_ENERGY_DISSIPATION.

For smooth periodic NSE layers

  rho_i (partial_t u_i+u_i dot grad u_i)
  =-grad p_i+eta_i Delta u_i+f_i-(L_kappa u)_i,
  div u_i=0,

define

  E_fluid := 1/2 sum_i rho_i ||u_i||_2^2.

Then

  dot E_fluid
   =-sum_i eta_i||grad u_i||_2^2
    -sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2
    +sum_i<u_i,f_i>.

Proof: integrate against u_i, use incompressibility
to kill advection and pressure, integration by parts
for viscosity, and symmetry of kappa_ij for drag.

STATUS: B_TIER COMPLETE.

THEOREM GB14: RECIPROCAL_WARD_RESERVOIR_CLOSURE.

Assume a full field-sector energy balance

  dot E_fields=-sum_i<u_i,f_i>,

with no unaccounted boundary flux, external work,
field self-energy, or time-dependent prescribed sources.

Define

  D_visc :=sum_i eta_i||grad u_i||_2^2,
  D_drag :=sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2,

and supply a reservoir with

  dot E_reservoir=D_visc+D_drag.

Then

  dot(E_fluid+E_fields+E_reservoir)=0.

If reservoir temperature T0>0 is constant and all
other entropy balances are compatible,

  dot S_reservoir=(D_visc+D_drag)/T0>=0.

These are exact algebraic consequences of the stated
power/constitutive premises. No microscopic reservoir
Lagrangian is constructed by this implication.

STATUS: CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

7. A TYPED UNIVERSAL LAGRANGIAN ENTRY PORT
-------------------------------------------
THEOREM GB15: SM_LAGRANGIAN_PLUGIN_SIGNATURE.

A physically meaningful insertion is not a bare
function L -> f_NSE. It is a tuple

  FIELD_SECTOR :=
    (K, P, representations, spin/tetrad data, connection,
     local action S_L, gauge/diffeomorphism Ward identities),

  FLUID_SECTOR :=
    (N, SDiff(M)^N, rho_i, eta_i, graph kappa,
     fluid kinetic energy, equations and constraints),

  MATCHING :=
    (coarse_grained observables, reciprocal Q_i^mu,
     constitutive closure, reservoir bookkeeping,
     gauge/coordinate covariance data).

The field action can be decomposed schematically as

  S_SM =
    S_YangMills + S_Dirac + S_Higgs + S_Yukawa,

with chirality and hypercharge handled inside
the specified representations.

The fluid matching yields source forces f_i. For
on-shell-compatible exchange ports Q_i^nu,

  nabla_mu T_SM^{mu nu}=-sum_i Q_i^nu,
  nabla_mu T_fluid,i^{mu nu}=Q_i^nu

(with reservoirs / interaction stress included in
T_total according to the chosen accounting), so

  nabla_mu T_total^{mu nu}=0.

In general, a nonrelativistic incompressible NSE
equation is recovered only after a declared
hydrodynamic/nonrelativistic limit and a constitutive
closure; full Standard Model dynamics is not itself
an incompressible fluid theory.

STATUS: CONDITIONAL_B_TIER, TYPED SIGNATURE.

8. ROOT THEOREM
---------------
THEOREM NSE_STANDARD_MODEL_GROUP_BASE.

ASSUME:
  H1. Smooth periodic orientable spin spatial manifold M and
      fixed spacetime geometric input when fermions are used.
  H2. SDiff(M) and N independent material fluid layers.
  H3. A chosen globally valid Standard Model gauge group
      K_Gamma and a principal bundle P over M.
  H4. A connection A, its curvature F_A, associated field
      representations and admissible invariant action S_L.
  H5. A fluid forcing/matching law with reciprocal power
      exchange and symmetric nonnegative layer drag.
  H6. The reservoir balance of GB14 for a closed system.

THEN:
  R1. The NSE transport algebra and pointwise SM current algebra
      satisfy Jacobi separately.
  R2. Their trivial-bundle semidirect Lie-algebra bracket is
      well-defined and satisfies Jacobi.
  R3. For nontrivial gauge bundles, the Atiyah algebroid is
      the correct infinitesimal automorphism interface.
  R4. With a connection split, the covariant bracket
      requires the curvature term F_A.
  R5. Product NSE layer symmetry and graph exchange
      produce nonnegative interlayer dissipation.
  R6. Field-force ports satisfy the stated kinetic-energy
      balance, and the closed combined field/fluid/reservoir
      energy is constant.
  R7. The resulting typed group/field/constitutive interface
      can ACCEPT an admissible SM field action without
      equating its microscopic dynamics to NSE.

PROOF:
  R1-R4 from GB01, GB05-GB09;
  R5 from GB03-GB04 and GB13;
  R6 from GB11, GB13-GB14;
  R7 by the explicitly typed construction GB15.

STATUS: B_TIER for the explicitly conditional theorem.
NO CLAIM of full physical SM/NSE equivalence, quantum
field-theoretic reconstruction, or A_TIER closure.
