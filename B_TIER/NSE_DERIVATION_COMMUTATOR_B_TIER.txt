EXACT SCOPE
-----------
This document establishes one operator identity within smooth
incompressible NSE geometry, plus an optional self-adjoint
transport-generator realization. It neither embeds the
Standard Model gauge group into NSE nor constructs an
electron/field interaction.

Do not conflate:
 (i) the antisymmetric Lie bracket [u,v],
(ii) the symmetric NSE cross-convection C(u,v),
(iii) the gauge-covariant curvature commutator, and
(iv) the dissipative NSE solution semiflow.

0. DOMAINS AND CONVENTIONS
-------------------------
M=T_L^3=(R/LZ)^3, L>0, with periodic Euclidean volume.
Let

  g_sigma = {u in C^infty(M;R^3): div u=0},
  g_sigma,0 = {u in g_sigma: integral_M u dx=0}.

For a smooth vector field u, define a derivation
  D_u:C^infty(M;C)->C^infty(M;C),
  D_u f = sum_j u^j partial_j f.

Define the vector-field bracket
  [u,v]^j = sum_i [u^i partial_i v^j
                     -v^i partial_i u^j].

Let P_sigma denote the full Leray projection onto
divergence-free vector fields, including spatial
constant modes.

For u=v the Lie bracket [u,u]=0 regardless of
the NSE nonlinearity B(u,u)=P_sigma((u dot grad)u).

1. DERIVATIONS CLOSE UNDER COMMUTATOR
--------------------------------------
THEOREM NDC-01: TRANSPORT_COMMUTATOR.

For all smooth u,v (even without div-free) and all
smooth complex-valued f,

  [D_u,D_v] f = D_[u,v] f.

PROOF:
  D_u D_v f
    = u^i (partial_i v^j)(partial_j f)
      + u^i v^j partial_i partial_j f,

  D_v D_u f
    = v^i (partial_i u^j)(partial_j f)
      + v^i u^j partial_i partial_j f.

Since the Hessian partial_i partial_j f is symmetric
in i,j, the second-derivative contractions cancel
under exchange i<->j. The remainder is

  [D_u,D_v]f
    = (u^i partial_i v^j-v^i partial_i u^j)
         partial_j f
    = D_[u,v] f.

In addition,
  D_(a u+b v)=aD_u+bD_v
for real a,b.

THEOREM NDC-02: DIVERGENCE_FREE_BRACKET_CLOSURE.

For any smooth u,v on the periodic domain,

  div [u,v]
    =u dot grad(div v)-v dot grad(div u).

Thus for u,v in g_sigma,
  [u,v] in g_sigma.

Moreover,
  [u,v]_j
    =partial_i (u^i v^j-v^i u^j)
when div u=div v=0.

Periodic integration gives
  integral_M [u,v] dx=0,
hence
  [g_sigma,g_sigma] subset g_sigma,0.

In particular g_sigma,0 is a Lie subalgebra
and an ideal of g_sigma.

THEOREM NDC-03: VECTOR_FIELD_JACOBI_VIA_DERIVATIONS.

Operator commutators satisfy
  [D_u,[D_v,D_w]]+cyclic=0.

By NDC-01 the LHS is
  D_([u,[v,w]]+[v,[w,u]]+[w,[u,v]]).

The map u->D_u is injective: if D_u f=0 for
all smooth f, differentiating suitable smooth
periodic coordinate functions (sine/cosine pairs)
shows u=0 pointwise.

Thus
  [u,[v,w]]+[v,[w,u]]+[w,[u,v]]=0.

2. UNITARY TRANSPORT AND THE HERMITIAN GENERATOR
-------------------------------------------------
THEOREM NDC-04: PERIODIC_TRANSPORT_SKEW_SYMMETRY.

Let u be real, smooth and divergence-free.
For smooth complex f,g,

  <f,D_u g>_L2 = -<D_u f,g>_L2.

Proof:
  0=integral_M div(u conjugate(f)g) dx
   =integral_M [conjugate(D_u f)g
                +conjugate(f)D_u g] dx
because div u=0 and M is periodic.

Therefore D_u is skew symmetric on the dense
domain C^infty(M;C).

Let Phi_t be the complete smooth flow generated
by u on compact M. The measure-preserving
composition operators

  K_u(t)f=f circle Phi_t

form a strongly continuous unitary group on L2(M).
Its Stone generator is the skew-adjoint closure
of the transport derivative, whose action on
smooth test functions is D_u.

No assertion is made that the FULL viscous NSE
velocity semiflow is unitary or invertible.

THEOREM NDC-05: HERMITIAN_LIE_GENERATORS.

On smooth common test functions define
  T_u:=i hbar D_u,
  hbar>0.

Since D_u is skew-symmetric,
T_u is symmetric on this test domain and
admits the self-adjoint Stone realization
associated with K_u(t).

The algebraic operator identity is

  [T_u,T_v]
    =(i hbar)^2[D_u,D_v]
    =-hbar^2 D_[u,v]
    =i hbar T_[u,v].

Thus
  [T_u,T_v]=i hbar T_[u,v].

This is formally identical in SHAPE to
the standard Hermitian SM generator convention
  [T_a,T_b]=i hbar f_ab^c T_c.

However, the fluid algebra g_sigma is
infinite-dimensional, while the standard
local internal gauge Lie algebra
  su(3)+su(2)+u(1)
has finite dimension 12 (at each point).

The equality is a representation of the
FLUID Lie algebra. It is NOT an isomorphism
of the fluid algebra with the SM gauge algebra.

CAUTION: unbounded operator commutators are
established on a common invariant smooth core.
A theorem about globally defined commutators
of closed self-adjoint operators would require
additional domain-invariance conditions.

3. EXPLICIT NONZERO PERIODIC EXAMPLE
-------------------------------------
THEOREM NDC-06: NONTRIVIAL_TWO_MODE_COMMUTATOR.

Let k,l in (2pi/L)Z\{0}. Set

  u(x,y,z)=(sin(kz),0,0),
  v(x,y,z)=(0,0,sin(lx)).

Then
  div u=div v=0, mean(u)=mean(v)=0.

Their Lie bracket is

  [u,v]
    =(-k sin(lx) cos(kz),0,
       l sin(kz) cos(lx)),

which is nonzero and has zero divergence.

For f(x,y,z)=cos(lx),

  [D_u,D_v]f
    = k*l sin(lx)^2 cos(kz)
    = D_[u,v] f.

The nonzero operator match is therefore
not merely an abstract Jacobi identity.

4. DISTINCT NSE NONLINEAR INTERACTION OPERATOR
-----------------------------------------------
THEOREM NDC-07: COMMUTATOR_VS_CROSS_ADVECTION.

Define
  B(u,v)=P_sigma((u dot grad)v)
and the symmetric cross interaction
  C(u,v)=B(u,v)+B(v,u).

Then
  [u,v]=B(u,v)-B(v,u)
for u,v divergence-free, because the bracket
is divergence-free so P_sigma[u,v]=[u,v].

Hence

  B(u,v)=1/2 (C(u,v)+[u,v]),
  B(v,u)=1/2 (C(u,v)-[u,v]).

The NSE self-advection B(u,u) is not determined
by the antisymmetric bracket [u,u]=0.
If two velocity layers are superposed, the
symmetric C(u,v) is the interaction term
in B(u+v,u+v); replacing it by [u,v]
would be mathematically incorrect.

For complex formal solenoidal Fourier modes
u=a exp(i k dot x), v=b exp(i l dot x),

  [u,v]
   =i[(a dot l)b-(b dot k)a] exp(i(k+l)dot x),

  C(u,v)
   =i P_(k+l)[(a dot l)b+(b dot k)a]
         exp(i(k+l)dot x),

with P_(0)=I, P_m=I-mm^T/|m|^2 for m!=0.

5. X-FOLD LAYER REPRESENTATION
-------------------------------
THEOREM NDC-08: PRODUCT_TRANSPORT_LIE_REPRESENTATION.

Let X={1,...,N} finite. On
  g_X=g_sigma^N
and
  K_X=L2(M;C)^N
define

  D_U:=direct_sum_i D_(u_i),
  T_U:=i hbar D_U.

Then

  [D_U,D_V]=D_([U,V]_X),
  [T_U,T_V]=i hbar T_([U,V]_X)

on smooth test vectors, where the product
bracket is componentwise.

For X infinite, a direct-sum self-adjoint
representation requires chosen summability
and domain conditions; the pointwise algebra
itself remains well-defined.

A weighted velocity average
  U -> sum_i w_i u_i
is NOT in general a Lie algebra map, as proved
in the frozen NSE_POWER_X_GROUP_STRUCTURE_B_TIER.
Thus the product representation does not
manufacture a single internal Standard Model
gauge field automatically.
