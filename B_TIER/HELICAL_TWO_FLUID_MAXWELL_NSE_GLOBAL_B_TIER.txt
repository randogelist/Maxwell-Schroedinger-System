1. HELICAL BASIS AND EXACT CLOSURE
-----------------------------------
Define smooth real transverse helices
  h_1(z)=(cos(kz),sin(kz),0),
  h_2(z)=(-sin(kz),cos(kz),0),
  e_z=(0,0,1).

They satisfy
  div h_a=0, mean(h_a)=0, |h_a|=1,
  h_1·h_2=0,
  partial_z h_1=k h_2,
  partial_z h_2=-k h_1,
  curl h_a=-k h_a,
  Delta h_a=-k^2 h_a,
  h_1 cross h_2=e_z,
  e_z cross h_1=h_2,
  e_z cross h_2=-h_1.

Make the ANSATZ for each fluid
  u_s(t,z)=V_s(t)e_z+a_s(t)h_1(z)+b_s(t)h_2(z).

Electromagnetic fields:
  E(t,z)=E_z(t)e_z+E_1(t)h_1(z)+E_2(t)h_2(z),
  B(t,z)=B_1(t)h_1(z)+B_2(t)h_2(z).

Hence
  div u_s=0, div E=0=rho_total/epsilon_0,
  div B=0.

The curl and cross-product operations preserve this
finite-dimensional Fourier/vector ansatz.
Every scalar pressure p_s may be spatially constant.

  (u_s·grad)u_s

Furthermore,

  u_s cross B
    =(-V_s B_2)h_1
     +(V_s B_1)h_2
     +(a_s B_2-b_s B_1)e_z.

The full PDE reduces EXACTLY to 3 ODEs per species:

4. POSITIVE FIELD+FLUID ENERGY AND EXACT WORK TRANSFER
------------------------------------------------------
Define total fluid kinetic energy

  E_fl
   :=(V/2)sum_s rho_s(
           V_s^2+a_s^2+b_s^2
        ).

Define electromagnetic field energy

  E_EM
   :=V/2[
       epsilon_0 (E_z^2+E_1^2+E_2^2)
       +(1/mu_0)(B_1^2+B_2^2)
     ].

The fluid kinetic identity is

  d E_fl/dt
   = -V sum_s eta_s k^2(a_s^2+b_s^2)
     -V kappa [
         (V_1-V_2)^2
        +(a_1-a_2)^2
        +(b_1-b_2)^2
       ]
     +V n0 sum_s q_s[
        V_s E_z+a_s E_1+b_s E_2].

Proof:
  - Coriolis-like advection pieces kV_s exchange a_s,b_s

    u_s·(u_s cross B)=0;

  - viscous dissipation is negative in transverse modes.

  d E_EM/dt

       V_s E_z+a_s E_1+b_s E_2].
