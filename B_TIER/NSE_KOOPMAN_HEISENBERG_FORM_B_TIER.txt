0. FIXED FINITE GALERKIN FRAMEWORK
----------------------------------
Fix a periodic torus M=T_L^3 and viscosity nu>0.
Choose a finite-dimensional real, mean-zero, divergence-free
Fourier Galerkin subspace V_N with orthonormal real L2 basis
{e_1,...,e_d} (multiple layers and a finite symmetric
graph may be included in the same finite product space).

Set
  u_N(z,x)=sum_(j=1)^d z_j e_j(x),  z in R^d.

Define the solenoidal convection coefficient vector
  (B_N(z,z))_j=<P_sigma((u_N dot grad)u_N),e_j>,

and a constant symmetric positive-semidefinite linear
dissipation matrix
  R_N=nu A_N+L_kappa,N >=0.
On the mean-zero Fourier space nu>0 makes R_N positive
definite. The graph term is nonnegative if its edges
are reciprocal, with nonnegative conductances.

The finite-dimensional ODE is
  dot z = b_N(z):= -B_N(z,z)-R_N z.                  (E1)

The projected convection satisfies exact energy cancellation
  z dot B_N(z,z)
    =<u_N,(u_N dot grad)u_N>
    =0
for all z, by incompressibility and periodic integration.

With a chosen positive constant rho for physical kinetic energy,
define
  E_N(z)=rho/2 * |z|^2,
  D_N(z)=rho * z^T R_N z >=0.

Thus
  b_N dot grad_z E_N=-D_N.                         (E2)

All finite-dimensional coefficient functions are smooth
polynomials, but do not claim that arbitrary spectral
Galerkin projection preserves the Lie Jacobi identity.

1. COMPLETE FINITE GALERKIN FLOW
---------------------------------
THEOREM NKH-01: GLOBAL_BIDIRECTIONAL_FINITE_FLOW.

For the ODE (E1), the Euclidean norm satisfies
  (1/2) d/dt |z(t)|^2= -z(t)^T R_N z(t).

For t>=0:
  |z(t)|<=|z(0)|.
For t<=0:
  |z(t)| <= exp(||R_N||*|t|)|z(0)|.

The second estimate follows from the differential
inequality and reversing the time parameter.
Neither direction can reach infinite norm in
finite time. Since b_N is a smooth polynomial
vector field, the finite ODE has a unique
complete flow
  Phi_t:R^d->R^d for all t in R,
  Phi_(t+s)=Phi_t circle Phi_s,
  Phi_(-t)=Phi_t^(-1).

It consists of smooth diffeomorphisms
with positive Jacobian determinant.

CRITICAL CAVEAT:
This finite-N invertibility does NOT establish
a reversible infinite-dimensional viscous
NSE flow or a bounded inverse as N tends
to infinity. Backward diffusion amplification
scales with the largest retained frequency.

THEOREM NKH-04: UNITARY_HALF_DENSITY_FLOW.
