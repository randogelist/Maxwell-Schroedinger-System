GOAL
----
Given finitely many fluid layers coupled by (NSE)^X, identify actual
interaction operators and sufficiently explicit hypotheses which
preserve a pre-existing global regularity class.

Three disjoint mechanisms are developed:
 (A) EXACT SYMMETRY TANGENCY: force and graph couplings remain inside
     a common 2D3C translation-invariant sector; no amplitude bound;
 (B) EXACT BELTRAMI TANGENCY: all layer fields lie in the same signed
     curl eigenspace and couplings preserve it; no amplitude bound;
 (C) PERTURBATIVE CRITICAL ABSORPTION: fully 3D leakage is permitted,
     but a quantitative H^{1/2} budget absorbs it.

A fourth mechanism (D) is a controlled perturbation around a large
regular reference, recovering the FORM of NSE_DECOMP_EXT with a
proved bootstrap criterion under an integrable background norm.

DO NOT CLAIM:
  - arbitrary couplings preserve 3D global NSE regularity;
  - standard-model field forces automatically satisfy these budgets;
  - the frozen exponential cutoff NSE_DECOMP_EXT is automatically
    inherited by all coupled systems;
  - rough L2 2D3C backgrounds automatically admit this H12 restart
    without a finite-entry theorem;
  - A-tier without native Lean compiler and theorem-level audit.

0. FINITE-LAYER OPERATOR-LEVEL BASE
-----------------------------------
Take M=T_L^3, X={1,...,N}, N>=1 finite.
Let A=-P_sigma Delta be the positive mean-zero Stokes operator,
Lambda=(-Delta)^{1/2} on nonzero solenoidal Fourier modes.
Let
  H_0= direct_sum_{i=1}^N L2_sigma,0(M),
  V_s= direct_sum_{i=1}^N dot H^s_sigma,0(M).

Fix equal positive kinematic viscosities nu>0 and unit densities
for the critical estimates. Unequal rho_i>0 and eta_i>0 are allowed
in the L2 energy identity after weighting, but require a separately
tracked coercivity constant for the H^{1/2} bootstrap.

Let kappa_ij=kappa_ji>=0 and kappa_ii=0. Define

  (L_kappa u)_i=sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j).

Let B(u,v)=P_sigma[(u dot grad)v]. Each mean-zero layer solves

  partial_t u_i +B(u_i,u_i)+nu A u_i
                   +(L_kappa u)_i =g_i,

where g_i=P_sigma f_i is a given force ACCELERATION and, in the
regularity theorems below, has spatial zero mean. All data/forces
are smooth if a smooth-in-time-and-space conclusion is requested.

THEOREM RI-01: COERCIVE_STOKES_GRAPH_OPERATOR.

Define the linear operator
  K_X=nu diag(A,...,A)+L_kappa.

Then
  <u,K_X u>_{H_0}
     =nu sum_i ||grad u_i||_2^2
      +sum_{i<j} kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_2^2 >=0.

With the standard self-adjoint Stokes realization and finite
symmetric layer matrix L_kappa, K_X is positive self-adjoint on
the product Stokes domain. The graph term is bounded and commutes
with Lambda^s because kappa_ij are spatially constant.

For all smooth u and s>=0,

  sum_i <Lambda^s u_i,Lambda^s(L_kappa u)_i>
    =sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_{dot H^s}^2>=0.

Thus arbitrary LARGE graph conductances are analytically
BENEFICIAL in the critical energy estimate. Positivity is lost
if one substitutes an unconstrained directed matrix.

1. INTERACTION ADMISSIBILITY CATEGORIES
---------------------------------------
THEOREM RI-02: TANGENT_AND_LEAKAGE_FORCE_DECOMPOSITION.

Let S be a closed linear spatial sector preserved by the Stokes
operator, Leray projection and NSE nonlinearity at the vector
fields of S. Let P_S be its orthogonal projector and Q_S=I-P_S.

For a candidate interaction force g_i, define

  g_i^parallel=P_S g_i,
  g_i^leak=Q_S g_i.

Exact tangency condition:
  g_i^leak=0 for every i and every state in S^X.

Perturbative admissibility condition:
  sum_i ||g_i^leak||_{L2_t dot H^{-1/2}_x}^2
     <= declared small budget.

The latter condition is NOT automatically sufficient without
a stability theorem relative to the chosen S-reference.
The proof below supplies precisely such a theorem when
the reference has integrable W^{2,infinity} size.

2. UNRESTRICTED 2D3C COUPLING
------------------------------
THEOREM RI-03: COMMON_2D3C_TANGENT_PORT.

Fix one primitive lattice direction n and define the sector
  S_n={smooth divergence-free u: (n dot grad)u=0}.

For the Cartesian case n=e_y, the field has 3 velocity
components depending only on (x,z).

Assume:
  - every u_i(0) lies in S_n;
  - all graph terms use spatially constant kappa_ij;
  - every external g_i is divergence-free, smooth,
    and remains in S_n, with smooth bounded data on
    every compact time interval;
  - nu>0 and X finite.

The derivatives, pressure Poisson solver, Laplacian, Leray
projection and graph drag commute with n translations.
Thus the coupled system preserves S_n^X.

Writing u_i=(w_i^x,b_i,w_i^z) and x2=(x,z), we obtain
coupled 2D NSE for w_i and a coupled passive-scalar
advection/diffusion system for b_i. With graph drag
and smooth forcing the planar vorticities obey

  partial_t zeta_i +w_i dot grad_2 zeta_i
    =nu Delta_2 zeta_i
     -sum_j kappa_ij(zeta_i-zeta_j)
     +curl_2 g_i^planar.

For g_i=0 the exact identity is

  (1/2) d/dt sum_i ||zeta_i||_2^2
    +nu sum_i||grad_2 zeta_i||_2^2
    +sum_{i<j} kappa_ij||zeta_i-zeta_j||_2^2=0.

For smooth forced data, the force term can be estimated on any
compact time interval. Finite-dimensional layer graph damping
does not introduce a singular source. Standard 2D parabolic
regularity yields global smoothness on all finite intervals
for smooth locally bounded-in-time forcing.

NO AMPLITUDE SMALLNESS is required for the initial fields,
the 2D3C-tangent force, or the graph conductances, subject to
the stated smooth/locally bounded forcing condition.

CAVEAT: an interaction respecting an individual layer's
separate 2D plane is insufficient if these planes differ.
A COMMON n is required for this mechanism.

3. UNRESTRICTED SIGNED BELTRAMI COUPLING
----------------------------------------
THEOREM RI-04: COMMON_BELTRAMI_TANGENT_PORT.

Fix a nonzero real curl eigenvalue lambda and let

  E_lambda={smooth solenoidal mean-zero u:
               curl u=lambda u}.

On a periodic torus E_lambda is finite-dimensional.
For u in E_lambda,

  Delta u=-lambda^2 u,
  (u dot grad)u=grad(|u|^2/2),
  B(u,u)=0.

If every layer u_i(0) belongs to the SAME E_lambda,
the graph drag is spatially constant, and every forcing
g_i(t) belongs to E_lambda with locally integrable
coefficient functions, the NSE equations reduce to
the finite-dimensional linear system

  dot u_i= -nu lambda^2 u_i
          -sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j)+g_i(t).

The solution is given by an operator/matrix exponential
and Duhamel. It stays in E_lambda and is globally smooth
for every finite initial amplitude and any finite positive
conductance matrix.

If g_i=0, all amplitudes decay. If g_i is only locally
integrable, the velocity is absolutely continuous in
time (not necessarily C-infinity in time); for smooth
g_i it is smooth in time.

Different curl signs or different eigenvalues can lose
the common Beltrami cancellation.

4. CRITICAL H^{1/2} SMALL-INTERACTION THEOREM
---------------------------------------------
THEOREM RI-05: SMALL_CRITICAL_FORCED_GRAPH_GLOBAL_REG.

Assume all layers have mean-zero H^{1/2} initial data and
g_i in L2([0,infinity); dot H^{-1/2}_sigma,0).
Define

  H(t)=sum_i ||u_i(t)||_{dot H^{1/2}}^2,
  D(t)=sum_i ||u_i(t)||_{dot H^{3/2}}^2,
  F(t)=sum_i ||g_i(t)||_{dot H^{-1/2}}^2.

Let C_NL>0 be a fixed constant in the critical estimate
for each smooth divergence-free component

  |<Lambda^{1/2}B(u,u),Lambda^{1/2}u>|
    <= C_NL ||u||_{dot H^{1/2}}
               ||u||_{dot H^{3/2}}^2.

This is the standard 3D critical Sobolev product estimate
with the torus length/normalization frozen in C_NL.

Pair the i-th NSE equation with Lambda u_i; sum to obtain

  (1/2)H' +nu D
    +sum_{i<j}kappa_ij||u_i-u_j||_{dot H^{1/2}}^2
    <= C_NL sqrt(H) D +sqrt(F) sqrt(D).

Let
  delta=nu/(2*C_NL).

On the bootstrap interval sqrt(H)<=delta,

  C_NL sqrt(H)D <=(nu/2)D,

and Young's inequality gives

  sqrt(F)sqrt(D)
    <=(nu/4)D+F/nu.

Hence

  (1/2)H'+(nu/4)D<=F/nu,
  H(t)<=H(0)+(2/nu)integral_0^t F(s)ds.

The sufficient global smallness criterion is

  H(0)+(2/nu) integral_0^infinity F(s)ds < delta^2.

It prevents reaching the critical threshold and, together
with standard local H^{1/2} well-posedness and continuation
for the forced NSE graph system, supplies a global strong
solution. For smooth forcing/data, standard parabolic
regularity yields smoothness at every finite positive time.

NOTE: f_i may represent an interaction *port* only after
its force regularity, boundary/mean compatibility and
small-norm budget have been proven. Merely naming an SM
field current does not establish the bound.

5. PERTURBATIONS AROUND A LARGE REGULAR CORE
----------------------------------------------
THEOREM RI-06: REGULAR_CORE_LEAKAGE_STABILITY.

Let U=(U_i) be a specified smooth globally regular
MEAN-ZERO reference solution of the same graph-coupled
NSE model with tangent force g^0, on [0,infinity).

Let u=U+w solve the equation with force g^0+h and
initial perturbation w(0)=w0; assume w0 mean-zero,
h_i in L2_t dot H^{-1/2} and smooth/locally admissible
where higher regularity is asserted.

The exact perturbation equation is

  partial_t w_i+nu A w_i+(L_kappa w)_i
   +B(U_i,w_i)+B(w_i,U_i)+B(w_i,w_i)=h_i.

For a smooth mean-zero background define

  M_U(t)^2=sum_i ||U_i(t)||_{W^{2,infinity}}^2.

The standard Sobolev multiplication bounds yield

  ||B(U_i,w_i)+B(w_i,U_i)||_{dot H^{-1/2}}
   <= C_mix ||U_i||_{W^{2,infinity}}
              ||w_i||_{dot H^{1/2}},

with a torus/normalization-dependent C_mix.

Let
  H_w(t)=sum_i||w_i(t)||_{dot H^{1/2}}^2,
  D_w(t)=sum_i||w_i(t)||_{dot H^{3/2}}^2,
  F_w(t)=sum_i||h_i(t)||_{dot H^{-1/2}}^2.

The energy estimate is

  (1/2)H_w'+nu D_w
    +graph_H12
      <=C_NL sqrt(H_w) D_w
       +C_mix M_U sqrt(H_w) sqrt(D_w)
       +sqrt(F_w)sqrt(D_w).

Fix the bootstrap threshold

  delta_w=nu/(4*C_NL).

When sqrt(H_w)<=delta_w, use Young on the mixed
background and forcing terms:

  C_mix M_U sqrt(H_w) sqrt(D_w)
    <=(nu/4) D_w+(C_mix^2/nu) M_U^2 H_w,

  sqrt(F_w)sqrt(D_w)
    <=(nu/4) D_w+F_w/nu.

The nonlinear term is at most (nu/4)D_w.
Consequently

  H_w'<= beta_U(t) H_w+(2/nu)F_w(t),

where
  beta_U(t)=(2*C_mix^2/nu)M_U(t)^2.

Define the background amplification budget

  B_U:=int_0^infinity beta_U(t)dt.

Assume B_U<infinity. Gronwall gives

  H_w(t)
    <=e^{B_U}
       [H_w(0)+(2/nu)int_0^infinity F_w(s)ds].

Therefore the sufficient strict condition

  e^{B_U}
    [H_w(0)+(2/nu)int_0^infinity F_w(s)ds]
       < delta_w^2

keeps the perturbation globally below its critical
continuation threshold. Standard critical-class local
existence/continuation then gives global regularity of
u=U+w; for smooth datum and force, global smoothness.

This theorem genuinely allows a FULLY 3D perturbation w
about a large 2D3C or Beltrami reference U. The strength
of the interactions is judged by their OFF-REFERENCE
H^{-1/2} budget and the integrable reference size,
not by the total kinetic energy of U.

It is sufficient but not necessary. The constant can be
improved using more refined anisotropic commutator and
paraproduct estimates; this is not assumed here.

6. RELATION TO FROZEN NSE_DECOMP_EXT
-------------------------------------
THEOREM RI-07: DECOMP_EXT_STYLE_INTERACTION_GATE.

The pre-existing unforced single-layer class has the
canonical decomposition

  a0=Pi u0 - mean(u0),  r0=(I-Pi)u0,

with r0 in H^{1/2} and an exponential smallness gate

  ||r0||_{H^{1/2}}
  exp(C ||a0||_2^2/nu^2) < c nu.

For finite NSE^X introduce a globally smooth graph-coupled
reference U(0)=(a0_i), all in a common 2D3C symmetry class,
and perturbation r0=(r0_i).

RI-06 supplies the new EXPLICIT interaction gate

  exp(B_U/2) *
     [sum_i ||r0_i||_{dot H^{1/2}}^2
      +(2/nu)sum_i||h_i||_{L2_t dot H^{-1/2}}^2]^(1/2)
      <delta_w,

where B_U is the background stability budget from RI-06.

If a separate analytic bound

  B_U <= C_base sum_i||a0_i||_2^2/nu^2

is proved, one recovers an exponential formula similar
to NSE_DECOMP_EXT.

But that bound is NOT established for every nonlinear
graph-coupled 2D3C core by the group algebra alone. It
must be proved in the proper anisotropic function spaces
to claim a full extension of the frozen NSE_DECOMP_EXT.

Rough L2 2D3C cores and positive-time smoothness require
a finite-entry and integrability bridge near t=0.
RI-06 uses smooth reference cores only.

Relation to literature:
Iftimie (1999), 3D NSE as perturbation of 2D NSE,
Bull. Soc. Math. France 127, 473–517,
DOI 10.24033/bsmf.2358.
That result uses anisotropic Sobolev/Besov spaces and
cannot be assumed to apply verbatim to arbitrary new
nonlocal NSE^X interactions.

7. PHYSICAL STANDARD MODEL FORCE PORT
--------------------------------------
THEOREM RI-08: GAUGE_INVARIANT_FORCE_LEAKAGE_GATE.

For a gauge-invariant field-theory exchange force
  Q_i^nu=<F^{nu}{}_{mu},J_i^mu>_k
(or a general invariant stress-exchange source),
declare a nonrelativistic matching and set

  g_i=P_sigma( spatial_force_i/rho_i ).

When a common spatial symmetry sector S is selected,
define the actual diagnostic
  Leak_S(g)=(I-P_S)g.

A coupling remains within the EXACT regularity class
of RI-03/RI-04 if
  Leak_S(g)=0
and the force is otherwise smooth, locally controlled,
and tangent to the relevant Beltrami/2D3C structure.

If not exactly tangent, the 3D leakage is acceptable
through RI-05/RI-06 only when its L2 dot H^{-1/2}
budget is small and all additional background terms
satisfy the declared stability assumptions.

The field/matter action must separately satisfy
the proper gauge Ward identities, charge continuity,
reciprocal field power and current-residual bookkeeping;
analytic regularity does NOT imply physical gauge
consistency or the converse.

8. WHAT IS NOT CURRENTLY AN ALLOWED UNCONDITIONAL PORT
------------------------------------------------------
- Arbitrary rank-3 Fourier coupling with no critical
  norm control or invariant structural cancellation.
- A quantum/Standard Model interaction inserted as a
  formal symbol without gauge-covariant current/stress
  and precise force-space bounds.
- Independently regular 2D3C layers in DIFFERENT planes
  with large graph exchange and no common symmetry:
  the combined interaction can generate rank-3 modes.
- General BMO^{-1} outsider data with unproved continuity
  of nonlinear/forcing operators in the appropriate
  critical Koch-Tataru spaces.
- Transfer of NSE_DECOMP_EXT from unforced single-layer
  geometry to arbitrary NSE^X dynamics without the
  background amplification and finite-entry proof.
- Identification of QFB cutoff with a real singularity
  interval or a quantum uncertainty time.

9. ROOT THEOREM
---------------
THEOREM NSE_POWER_X_REGULARITY_PRESERVING_INTERACTION_PORT.

Under finite X, constant common viscosity nu>0,
symmetric nonnegative graph exchange and sufficient
smoothness/local existence:

 A. If the interactions are tangent to the SAME common
    2D3C sector and locally smooth, all finite-energy
    smooth initial states in that sector remain globally
    smooth, irrespective of coupling amplitude.

 B. If interactions preserve a common signed curl
    eigenspace E_lambda, the dynamics reduces to a
    forced finite-dimensional linear ODE and remains
    globally smooth for locally integrable forcing,
    irrespective of finite amplitude.

 C. If the initial global H^{1/2} norm and external
    interaction forcing satisfy RI-05, the coupled
    NSE system has global critical-strong solutions.

 D. If a globally smooth reference U obeys the
    integrable W^{2,infinity} budget and the deviation
    satisfies RI-06, the full perturbed 3D solution
    remains globally regular, even for arbitrarily
    large finite reference kinetic energy.

The result is a set of SUFFICIENT coupling admissions,
not a necessary-and-sufficient characterization of all
possible regularity-preserving Standard Model fields.
