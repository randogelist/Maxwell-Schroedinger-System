0. CONVENTIONS AND DOMAIN
------------------------
Let M=T_L^3, L>0, spatial flat periodic torus, and
I be an interval of time; Y=I x M.
Assume a smooth real divergence-free velocity u(t,x),
smooth real kinematic pressure p(t,x), and smooth
real body FORCE PER UNIT MASS f(t,x) satisfy

  partial_t u+(u dot grad)u
    =-grad p+nu Delta u+f,
  div u=0,
  nu>=0.

The pressure is measured in units of velocity squared,
i.e. p=p_physical/rho for constant fluid density rho.

Define vorticity and Bernoulli scalar

  omega:=curl u,
  b:=p+(1/2)|u|^2.

An arbitrary constant coupling normalization lambda
with dimensions inverse (velocity x length) is fixed.
For example lambda=m_eff/hbar is a dimensional
choice, not an electron or gauge identification.

NCC-01  HYDRODYNAMIC_ONE_FORM.

  D_i^a = partial_i +i lambda u_i,
  D_0^a = partial_t -i lambda b.

This is an honest connection in the mathematical

connections to those generated by NSE data; it

2. CURVATURE COMMUTATOR
-----------------------
NCC-02  CURVATURE_COMMUTATOR_OPERATOR_IDENTITY.

The curvature is the real 2-form

  F := da.

For arbitrary smooth spacetime vector fields X,Y,
direct expansion yields

  ([D_X^a,D_Y^a]-D_[X,Y]^a) phi
     = i lambda F(X,Y) phi.

In coordinate directions, [partial_mu,partial_nu]=0:

  [D_mu^a,D_nu^a]phi
    = i lambda F_mu nu phi,

  F_mu nu = partial_mu a_nu-partial_nu a_mu.

In particular

  F_ij=partial_i u_j-partial_j u_i,
  F_0i=partial_t u_i+partial_i b.

This is EXACTLY the algebraic shape of the
internal scalar Standard Model covariant
commutator (one-dimensional U(1) representation),
not an SM field-strength identification.

4. NSE AS AN EQUATION FOR TEMPORAL CURVATURE
---------------------------------------------
NCC-04  NSE_TEMPORAL_CURVATURE_IDENTITY.

Use the vector calculus identity

  (u dot grad)u
    =grad(|u|^2/2)-u cross omega.

The NSE momentum equation becomes

  partial_t u+grad b
    =u cross omega+nu Delta u+f.

Therefore

  F_0i
    =(u cross omega)_i
      +nu Delta u_i+f_i.

Equivalently, since div u=0 implies
  Delta u=-curl omega,

  F_0i
    =(u cross omega)_i
      -nu(curl omega)_i+f_i.

The pair of curvature equations

  F_ij=epsilon_ijk omega_k,
  F_0i=(u cross omega)_i+nu Delta u_i+f_i,

together with the definition of a=u^flat-b dt,
is EXACTLY the smooth forced NSE momentum identity
written in curvature variables (plus div u=0).

5. BIANCHI AND VORTICITY TRANSPORT
----------------------------------
NCC-05  BIANCHI_VORTICITY_IDENTITY.

Because F=da,

  dF=d^2 a=0.

The spatial part is div omega=0, since omega=curl u.
The time-space part is the integrability identity

  partial_t omega =curl F_0,

where (F_0)_i=F_0i.
Substitute NCC-04 to obtain

  partial_t omega
    =curl(u cross omega)+nu Delta omega+curl f.

This is the smooth 3D vorticity equation.
It includes vortex stretching through
curl(u cross omega), not merely a linear
diffusion equation.

Thus fluid vorticity dynamics is exactly
compatible with the auxiliary abelian Bianchi
identity and the NSE temporal curvature law.
The Bianchi identity ALONE does not imply
the NSE momentum equation.

------------------------------------------
