GOAL
----
Define which further mathematical OPERATORS must be independently
specified to turn Standard Model quantum observables into admissible
regularity-preserving (NSE)^X interactions.

The missing core is not another bare gauge group. It is the
operator interface

  [quantum gauge-invariant observables]
     -> [coarse-grained number/current/stress data]
     -> [covariant force and residual]
     -> [symmetric dissipative generator + memory]
     -> [NSE layer graph]
     -> [quantitative H1/2 regularity gate].

Do not identify these maps with the Standard Model unless a
microscopic derivation is supplied. The distinction between
operator algebra, state, projection, and constitutive parameters
is essential.

2. EXACT CURRENT AND FORCE RESIDUAL
------------------------------------
THEOREM SMN-02: CURRENT_RESIDUAL_NO_DOUBLE_COUNTING.

Let a matched classical number/current readout J_SM
be specified. Define an admitted fluid current
  J_fluid=sum_i q_i n_i u_i
with whatever conservation laws the fluid species
satisfy.

Set EXACTLY
  J_res:=J_SM-J_fluid.

Then
  J_SM=J_fluid+J_res.

If the full current obeys continuity,
  partial_t rho_SM+div J_SM=0,

and the fluid charge density satisfies its own law,
then the residual charge/current satisfy the
DIFFERENCE continuity equation. This cannot in
general be replaced by zero without proof.

For Pauli electron number velocity v=j/n
and a=P_sigma,0 v,

  J_res=q n(I-P_sigma,0)v.

For the free helical electron compared with
viscous NSE current, J_res is nonzero at t>0
despite an exact initial profile match.

3. THE EXACT MEMORY OBSTRUCTION
--------------------------------
THEOREM SMN-04: MORI_ZWANZIG_BLOCK_OPERATOR_IDENTITY.

For a finite-dimensional micro-observable state
z(t) obeying the LINEAR system
  dot z=L z,
choose orthogonal projections P and Q=I-P.
Write x=Pz and y=Qz. Then

  dot x=PLP x+PLQ y,
  dot y=QLP x+QLQ y.

Solving the second equation yields EXACTLY

  dot x(t)
   =PLP x(t)
    +PLQ exp(t QLQ) y(0)
    +int_0^t PLQ exp((t-s)QLQ)
                   QLP x(s)ds.

Thus the exact projected dynamics includes
  - a local term,
  - a fluctuating initial-orthogonal term,
  - a memory convolution.

The result also has appropriate semigroup
generalizations with operator-domain premises.

A local Markovian viscosity/graph exchange can be
obtained only under an extra fast-decay/scale
separation or effective-action limit.
The Standard Model operator commutators alone
do not set the value of nu or kappa.

STATUS = EXACT FINITE-DIMENSIONAL B_TIER IDENTITY.

4. POSITIVE QUANTUM DISSIPATION (OPTIONAL MARKOV LIMIT)
-------------------------------------------------------
THEOREM SMN-05: MARKOVIAN_COMPLETE_POSITIVITY_GATE.

For an explicitly finite regulated physical
quantum Hilbert space, an admitted Markovian
open-system generator may have the GKSL form

 dot rho= -(i/hbar)[H,rho]+
   sum_alpha gamma_alpha (
       L_alpha rho L_alpha^dagger
       - (1/2){L_alpha^dagger L_alpha,rho}
   ),

where gamma_alpha>=0 and all L_alpha are
bounded matrices of the stated finite model.

This preserves trace and complete positivity.
Gauge covariance and preservation of the
physical Gauss/BRST state space are ADDITIONAL
requirements on the jump operators.

A local thermal KMS/fluctuation-dissipation
relation is an additional assumption for a
thermal equilibrium-compatible effective theory.

This does NOT say the closed Standard Model itself
has a fundamental Lindblad generator. Open
dynamics arise after a system/environment split.

THEOREM SMN-06: NSE_GRAPH_POSITIVE_OPERATOR.

For fixed finite layer graph the bounded graph
matrix commutes with every common spatial
spectral multiplier Lambda^s. Thus the
graph term contributes positive dissipation at
all Sobolev levels where pairings exist.

THEOREM SMN-09: SMALL_CRITICAL_SM_FORCE_ADMISSION.

Suppose the force matching produces actual
mean-zero divergence-free accelerations
g_i(t) in L2([0,infinity);dot H^(-1/2))
for a finite graph, common nu>0.

Set
  H0=sum_i||a_i||_(dot H^(1/2))^2,
  F0=sum_i||g_i||_(L2_t dot H^(-1/2))^2.

Let C_NL>0 be an accepted 3D critical
trilinear estimate constant in the prior
regularity-preserving interaction module.

The sufficient condition

  H0+(2/nu) F0
   < (nu/(2 C_NL))^2

implies global critical-strong regularity
under standard local critical well-posedness,
weak-strong uniqueness and continuation.

For forces dependent on the evolving quantum
and fluid states, the same conclusion requires
the bound to be established ALONG the coupled
solution (or by a closed bootstrap/feedback
inequality). A prechosen exogenous force
estimate cannot be applied circularly to
an unproved SM-driven force trajectory.

7. NO-UNIQUE-TRANSPORT THEOREM
-------------------------------
THEOREM SMN-11: SM_OPERATOR_IDENTITIES_DO_NOT_FIX_NSE_COEFFICIENTS.

Fix any abstract SM operator algebra satisfying
all gauge/BRST/Hamiltonian/Ward identities.
Choose two distinct viscosities
  nu1>0, nu2>0, nu1 != nu2
and use the same classical initial velocity
a != 0 as NSE data in two candidate reduced
models with otherwise identical SM identities.

Both reduced model definitions are compatible
with the same SM algebraic constraints, but the
short-time kinetic dissipation laws differ:

  (d/dt)E_NSE^{(nu1)}|_0
    =-nu1||grad a||_2^2,

  (d/dt)E_NSE^{(nu2)}|_0
    =-nu2||grad a||_2^2

for unforced single-layer smooth data.

Thus the bare SM symmetry/commutator identities
cannot uniquely fix viscosity or layer-graph
conductances. They require a chosen state,
observable projection, scale, environment and
microscopic transport-coefficient computation.

This is an explicit underdetermination
counterexample, not a physical assertion that
both assignments are realized in nature.
