INTERPRETATION
--------------
The symbol (NSE)^X denotes a family of Navier–Stokes layers indexed
by a set X. NSE solutions do not form a group under arbitrary
superposition; for positive viscosity their time evolution is a
forward dissipative semiflow, not an invertible time-action group.

The correct group-level object is the X-fold configuration group
of volume-preserving diffeomorphisms. This is not a derivation
of Standard Model gauge symmetry from hydrodynamics.

0. DOMAIN AND CONVENTIONS
------------------------
M := T_L^3, L>0, oriented smooth spatial torus, volume form mu.
Let X be an arbitrary nonempty INDEX SET of fluid layers, not
the spacetime manifold. Write spacetime as Y=R x M.

G := SDiff(M)={smooth phi:M->M diffeomorphism with phi^*mu=mu}.
g := X_sigma(M)={smooth u: div_mu u=0}, bracket
  [u,v]=u dot grad v-v dot grad u.

P_sigma: full Leray projection to solenoidal vector fields,
including constant spatial modes. Positive viscosity nu>0 is
an independent constitutive parameter.

1. X-INDEXED PRODUCT GROUP
-------------------------
THEOREM NXG-01: PRODUCT_SDIFF_GROUP.

Define
  G_X=Map(X,G)=Product_{a in X} G,

with pointwise multiplication
  (phi*psi)_a := phi_a circle psi_a,
  identity (id)_a=id_M,
  inverse (phi^{-1})_a=(phi_a)^{-1}.

Associativity, identity, and inverse laws hold because they
hold in each coordinate.

For FINITE X this is the ordinary finite smooth
diffeomorphism-group product.
For ARBITRARY X it is always an abstract group and a
topological group under the product topology, but a
choice of function-space topology/manifold model is
required before claiming any smooth infinite-dimensional
Lie group or PDE well-posedness.

THEOREM NXG-02: COMPONENTWISE_LIE_ALGEBRA.

For finite X,
  g_X=Product_{a in X} g,
  [u,v]_X=( [u_a,v_a] )_{a in X}.

The bracket is bilinear, antisymmetric, satisfies Jacobi,
and preserves divergence-free constraints componentwise.

For arbitrary X the same operations define a pointwise
abstract Lie algebra; a functional-analytic Lie algebra
compatible with a chosen group topology requires additional
continuous-operation and domain hypotheses.

THEOREM NXG-03: DIAGONAL_GROUP_HOMOMORPHISM.

Define
  Delta:G->G_X,  Delta(phi)=(phi)_{a in X},
  delta:g->g_X,  delta(u)=(u)_{a in X}.

Then
  Delta(phi circle psi)=Delta(phi)*Delta(psi),
  delta([u,v])=[delta(u),delta(v)]_X.

These are injective for X nonempty.

A diagonal common diffeomorphism is a legitimate common
Eulerian-coordinate symmetry of all layers.

2. WEIGHTED AVERAGING IS NOT A GROUP MAP
----------------------------------------
THEOREM NXG-05: WEIGHTED_AVERAGE_LIE_NO_GO.

For finite |X|>=2, choose real weights w_a, and define
  A_w(u)=sum_a w_a u_a.

Then
  A_w([u,v]_X)=sum_a w_a[u_a,v_a],

but
  [A_w(u),A_w(v)]
     =sum_{a,b} w_a w_b [u_a,v_b].

For indices i != j with w_i w_j != 0, choose u supported
only in layer i and v only in layer j, with
[u_i,v_j] != 0 in g (such solenoidal fields exist).
Then
  A_w([u,v]_X)=0
while
  [A_w(u),A_w(v)]=w_i w_j[u_i,v_j] != 0.

Hence a genuine multiweight sum is not a Lie-algebra
homomorphism on the independent layer algebra.

Special homomorphisms do exist: projection to a single
coordinate (weight 1 at that coordinate, zero elsewhere),
or restriction to a diagonal subalgebra with normalized
weights sum_a w_a=1.

THIS IS THE DECISIVE OBSTRUCTION to treating a weighted
mean velocity as an automatically defined common
spatial/gauge representation of the X-fold fluid group.

3. THE STANDARD MODEL GAUGE FACTOR
----------------------------------
THEOREM NXG-06: INDEPENDENT_INTERNAL_GAUGE_SYMMETRY.

Fix a principal K-bundle P->Y, where for a
Standard Model choice
  Lie(K)=su(3)_C + su(2)_L + u(1)_Y,
  K=[SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1)]/Gamma
with a compatible declared global quotient Gamma.

Gauge transformations Gau(P) and volume-preserving
material-label group G_X are DISTINCT notions.

On a trivial bundle and a chosen common physical
spatial diffeomorphism group G, the gauge and spatial
symmetries may be combined using a specified
semidirect action (pullbacks), and locally the Lie
algebra has the familiar bracket

  [(u,xi),(v,eta)]
   =([u,v],u dot grad eta-v dot grad xi+[xi,eta]_K).

The above semidirect action uses ONE common spatial
diffeomorphism. It cannot automatically be extended
to independent components of G_X acting on a SINGLE
shared gauge connection via a weighted-average
homomorphism, by NXG-05.

For nontrivial P, use compatible bundle automorphisms
Aut(P), and restrict to base diffeomorphisms admitting
lifts. Its Atiyah sequence is

  0 -> ad(P) -> At(P)=TP/K -> TY -> 0,

with a connection split and a curvature-corrected
Lie bracket. The global bundle need not split.

4. SPECTRAL SUBGROUP STRUCTURE
------------------------------
THEOREM NXG-08: FOURIER_LAYER_LIE_SUBALGEBRA.

For additive subgroup Gamma of (2pi/L)Z^3,
define
  g_Gamma={u in g: supp(uhat) subset Gamma}.

The Lie bracket of single Fourier modes has wave number
k+ell. Hence g_Gamma is a Lie subalgebra; Fourier
multiplication, Leray projection and the Laplacian
preserve support in Gamma.

For distinct layers Gamma_a the smallest common
additive subgroup is
  Gamma_X=span_Z( union_{a in X} Gamma_a ).

The individual Gamma_a may not be preserved after a
cross-layer interaction, while the common Gamma_X is
preserved for local polynomial fluid terms and a
graph-drag coupling without additional external
field wave numbers.

THEOREM NXG-09: COMMON_TWO_DIRECTIONAL_INVARIANCE.

For finite X, if Gamma_X has real linear rank <=2,
there is a nonzero common spatial direction n such
that for every initial layer
  (n dot grad)u_a(0)=0.

The unforced NSE self-advection, viscosity, pressure
and pairwise pointwise drag preserve this common
translation symmetry. Thus a coupled model with
only those forces stays within a 2D3C geometry as
long as its solution exists. Global smoothness for
smooth data and positive viscosities follows by
the finite coupled 2D vorticity/passive-scalar
argument, detailed in the second-stage composition
document.

If Gamma_X has rank 3, this argument does not
certify full 3D regularity (even though special
three-dimensional Beltrami configurations remain
globally regular).
