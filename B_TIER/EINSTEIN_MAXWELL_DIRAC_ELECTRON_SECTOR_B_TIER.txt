The frozen object is a classical-field, one-electron-normalized
Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac sector.  It contains rest mass, electric charge,
spin 1/2, electromagnetic backreaction, and gravitational backreaction.
It is not full QED and does not by itself establish existence of a globally
regular stationary one-electron solution.

The exact B-tier content is structural:

  action -> Euler-Lagrange equations;
  gauge covariance;
  Dirac-current conservation;
  one-electron charge normalization;
  Maxwell-source compatibility;
  matter/field stress exchange;
  total stress conservation and Bianchi closure;
  3+1 constraint equations;
  compact-charge obstruction;
  stationary-bilinear implication;
  transverse a_dagger defect decomposition.

Existence, uniqueness, global stability, particle interpretation in quantum
field theory, and the construction of a stationary isolated solution remain
separate endpoints.


0. FROZEN GEOMETRIC DATA
------------------------

Let

  (M,g)

be an oriented, time-oriented, globally hyperbolic four-dimensional Lorentzian
spin manifold with signature (-,+,+,+).  Let

  S M -> M

be its complex Dirac spinor bundle, equipped with a compatible spin connection

  nabla^S,

a Lorentzian spinor adjoint bar(Psi), and Clifford multiplication gamma.
The spinor convention is fixed so that the Dirac number current defined below
is future causal for positive-frequency solutions.

Let

  A in Omega^1(M;R)

be a U(1) gauge potential and

  F := dA.

Freeze the electron constants

  q_e := -e < 0,
  m := m_e > 0,

and the positive constants

  hbar > 0,
  c > 0,
  epsilon0 > 0,
  mu0 > 0,
  G > 0,

with

  epsilon0 mu0 c^2 = 1.

The gauge-spin covariant derivative is

  D_mu Psi
    := nabla^S_mu Psi
       + i q_e A_mu Psi/(hbar c).

Under a real gauge function Lambda,

  A'   = A + d Lambda,
  Psi' = exp(-i q_e Lambda/(hbar c)) Psi,

one has

  D'_mu Psi'
    = exp(-i q_e Lambda/(hbar c)) D_mu Psi.


1. JOINT ACTION
---------------

For cosmological constant Lambda_GR, define

  S_EMD[g,A,Psi]
    := integral_M dVol_g [
         c^3(R_g-2 Lambda_GR)/(16 pi G)
         - F_{mu nu}F^{mu nu}/(4 mu0)
         + L_D(g,A,Psi)
       ],

where

  L_D
    := (i hbar c/2)
       [ bar(Psi) gamma^mu D_mu Psi
         - (D_mu bar(Psi)) gamma^mu Psi ]
       - m_e c^2 bar(Psi) Psi.

All variations below are smooth and compactly supported, or satisfy boundary
conditions that remove the corresponding boundary terms.

THEOREM EMD_ACTION_EULER_LAGRANGE.

Critical points of S_EMD satisfy the coupled Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac equations
recorded in Sections 2-4.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_FROM_STANDARD_VARIATION.

No existence assertion is contained in this theorem.


2. DIRAC ELECTRON EQUATION
--------------------------

The charged Dirac equation is

  (i hbar c gamma^mu D_mu - m_e c^2) Psi = 0.

The adjoint equation is

  i hbar c (D_mu bar(Psi)) gamma^mu
    + m_e c^2 bar(Psi) = 0.

Define the number current

  N^mu := c bar(Psi) gamma^mu Psi,

and the electric current

  j_e^mu := q_e N^mu
          = -e c bar(Psi) gamma^mu Psi.

THEOREM EMD_DIRAC_CURRENT_CONSERVATION.

Every sufficiently regular solution of the Dirac equation and its adjoint
satisfies

  nabla_mu N^mu = 0,
  nabla_mu j_e^mu = 0.

DERIVATION.

Expand

  nabla_mu(bar(Psi) gamma^mu Psi).

Metric-spin compatibility gives nabla gamma = 0.  Substitute the Dirac and
adjoint equations.  The two mass terms cancel, and the U(1) connection terms
cancel because the bilinear is gauge invariant.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.


3. MAXWELL EQUATIONS
--------------------

The Maxwell equations are

  nabla_mu F^{mu nu} = mu0 j_e^nu,

  nabla_[alpha F_{beta gamma]} = 0.

The second equation is the identity dF=0 when F=dA.

THEOREM EMD_MAXWELL_SOURCE_COMPATIBILITY.

The Maxwell source equation is compatible with the Dirac source because

  nabla_nu j_e^nu = 0.

Equivalently, applying nabla_nu to the Maxwell equation yields zero on both
sides.  The left side vanishes because F is antisymmetric and the Ricci tensor
is symmetric.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.


4. EINSTEIN EQUATION AND STRESS TENSORS
---------------------------------------

The electromagnetic stress tensor is

  T_EM_{mu nu}
    := (1/mu0)
       [ F_{mu alpha} F_nu^alpha
         - (1/4) g_{mu nu} F_{alpha beta}F^{alpha beta} ].

The on-shell symmetric Dirac stress tensor is

  T_D_{mu nu}
    := (i hbar c/4) [
         bar(Psi) gamma_mu D_nu Psi
         + bar(Psi) gamma_nu D_mu Psi
         - (D_mu bar(Psi)) gamma_nu Psi
         - (D_nu bar(Psi)) gamma_mu Psi
       ].

The Einstein equation is

  G_{mu nu} + Lambda_GR g_{mu nu}
    = (8 pi G/c^4) (T_D_{mu nu}+T_EM_{mu nu}).

THEOREM EMD_STRESS_EXCHANGE.

For sufficiently regular coupled solutions,

  nabla_mu T_D^{mu nu}
    = F^nu_lambda j_e^lambda,

  nabla_mu T_EM^{mu nu}
    = -F^nu_lambda j_e^lambda.

Consequently,

  nabla_mu(T_D^{mu nu}+T_EM^{mu nu}) = 0.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_FROM_FIELD_EQUATIONS.

THEOREM EMD_BIANCHI_CLOSURE.

The total stress conservation law is compatible with the Einstein equation
because

  nabla_mu G^{mu nu}=0,
  nabla_mu g^{mu nu}=0.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.


5. ONE-ELECTRON NORMALIZATION
-----------------------------

Let Sigma be a spacelike Cauchy hypersurface with future-directed unit normal
n and induced volume dmu_Sigma.  Define

  N_Sigma[Psi]
    := -(1/c) integral_Sigma n_mu N^mu dmu_Sigma.

For positive-frequency Dirac data this quantity is nonnegative.  Current
conservation and the divergence theorem imply that N_Sigma is independent of
the Cauchy hypersurface, under the declared decay or flux conditions.

Define the electric charge

  Q_Sigma[Psi]
    := -(1/c) integral_Sigma n_mu j_e^mu dmu_Sigma.

Since j_e=q_e N,

  Q_Sigma = q_e N_Sigma.

THEOREM EMD_ONE_ELECTRON_CHARGE.

If

  N_Sigma[Psi]=1,

then every Cauchy hypersurface in the same globally hyperbolic development has

  N=1,
  Q=-e.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

SCOPE NOTE.

This is a classical-field normalization.  It does not by itself identify Psi
with a one-particle vector in interacting quantum electrodynamics.


6. 3+1 CAUCHY CONSTRAINTS
-------------------------

Let h be the induced spatial metric on Sigma and K its second fundamental form.
Let

  T_tot := T_D + T_EM,

  rho_tot := T_tot(n,n),

  J_i := -T_tot(n,e_i).

Then the Einstein constraints are

  R(h) + (tr_h K)^2 - |K|_h^2
    = 2 Lambda_GR + (16 pi G/c^4) rho_tot,

  D^j(K_{ij}-(tr_h K)h_{ij})
    = (8 pi G/c^4) J_i.

The electromagnetic constraints are

  D_i E^i = rho_e/epsilon0,
  D_i B^i = 0,

where

  rho_e := -(1/c) n_mu j_e^mu.

THEOREM EMD_CAUCHY_CONSTRAINT_COMPATIBILITY.

If the matter and Maxwell evolution equations hold and the constraints are
satisfied on one Cauchy hypersurface, then the contracted Bianchi identity,
current conservation, and Maxwell identities give the standard constraint
propagation system.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_AT_THE_FORMAL_IDENTITY_LEVEL.

A complete Sobolev well-posedness theorem remains an external analytic input.


7. ISOLATED AND COMPACT SECTOR VARIANTS
---------------------------------------

7.1 ASYMPTOTICALLY FLAT ISOLATED VARIANT

Define

  EMD_ELECTRON_SECTOR_AF(g,A,Psi)

by the coupled field equations, asymptotically flat boundary conditions,
finite ADM/electromagnetic/Dirac energy in the selected function class, and

  N_Sigma[Psi]=1.

Then

  Q=-e

without a compensating charge background.

STATUS = EXACT_SECTOR_DEFINITION.

Existence of a regular stationary isolated solution is not asserted.


7.2 COMPACT NEUTRALIZED VARIANT

If Sigma is compact and boundaryless, Maxwell Gauss law implies

  integral_Sigma rho_total dmu_h = 0.

Therefore a one-electron contribution Q_e=-e requires a compensating total
charge +e.  Introduce a background current j_bg and stress tensor T_bg such
that

  nabla_mu j_bg^mu = 0,

  integral_Sigma rho_bg dmu_h = +e,

  nabla_mu T_bg^{mu nu}
    = F^nu_lambda j_bg^lambda.

The field equations become

  nabla_mu F^{mu nu}
    = mu0(j_e^nu+j_bg^nu),

  G+Lambda_GR g
    = (8 pi G/c^4)(T_D+T_EM+T_bg).

THEOREM EMD_COMPACT_CHARGE_NEUTRALITY.

A compact boundaryless Maxwell sector cannot contain net charge.  Therefore
an electron-normalized compact sector is necessarily a neutralized composite
sector rather than an isolated electron sector.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

The former subtraction by a uniform reference is recovered only after a
specific background model and volume normalization are chosen.  In GR the
background must carry both current and stress-energy.


8. STATIONARY SUBSECTOR
-----------------------

Let K be a complete timelike Killing field in a stationary region.  Assume

  L_K g = 0,

  L_K A = d chi_K

for a gauge function chi_K.  Let the gauge-covariant spinorial Lie derivative
satisfy

  L_K^A Psi = -i omega Psi

for real omega.

THEOREM EMD_STATIONARY_BILINEARS.

Under the stationary ansatz,

  L_K j_e = 0,
  L_K T_D = 0,
  L_K T_EM = 0,
  L_K F = 0.

Thus all gauge-invariant classical observables are stationary although the
spinor may acquire a phase along the Killing flow.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_CONDITIONAL_ON_THE_STATIONARY_ANSATZ.

OPEN ENDPOINT EMD_STATIONARY_EXISTENCE.

stationary solution of the full Einstein-Maxwell-Dirac equations, or prove a
precise obstruction in the frozen class.

STATUS = OPEN_ANALYTIC.


9. TRANSVERSE a_DAGGER PLACEMENT
--------------------------------

Let

  F = F_self + F_dagger.

Define the exact defect current

  j_def^nu
    := (1/mu0) nabla_mu F^{mu nu}
       - j_e^nu
       - j_bg^nu.

THEOREM EMD_A_DAGGER_CURRENT_DEFECT.

The full electromagnetic field is self-consistent exactly when

  j_def=0.

The a_dagger sector has three exact classifications:

  (A) source-free dressing:

        nabla_mu F_dagger^{mu nu}=0;

  (B) electron/background-supported dressing:

        its divergence is contained in j_e+j_bg;

  (C) externally maintained dressing:

        j_def != 0.

The Einstein equation always receives the stress tensor of the complete field
F, including interference terms between F_self and F_dagger.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

No present theorem proves that the specified a_dagger profile is generated by
a one-electron Dirac current.


10. NONRELATIVISTIC PAULI LIMIT
-------------------------------

After extracting the rest-energy phase and separating large and small Dirac
components, the formal c -> infinity limit gives a two-component Pauli spinor
psi with

  Pi_A := -i hbar grad + e A,

  i hbar partial_t psi
    = [ Pi_A^2/(2m_e)
        - e Phi
        + m_e U
        + (e hbar/(2m_e)) sigma.B
      ] psi

at the leading Dirac magnetic moment g=2.

The nonrelativistic electric current decomposes as

  J_e
    = -(e/m_e) Re(psi^dagger Pi_A psi)
      + curl M,

  M
    = -(e hbar/(2m_e)) psi^dagger sigma psi.

The leading mass density is

  mu_m = m_e psi^dagger psi.

THEOREM EMD_NONRELATIVISTIC_PARAMETER_MATCH.

At formal leading order, the EMD electron sector carries

  electric charge = -e,
  inertial/rest mass = m_e,
  spin = 1/2,
  magnetic moment g=2.

STATUS = FORMAL_ASYMPTOTIC_B_TIER.

A uniform error estimate and convergence theorem are separate analytic
endpoints.  The anomalous magnetic moment g-2 is outside the classical EMD
system.


11. FERMIONIC PROFILE TRANSFER
------------------------------

On a fixed Cauchy slice with one-particle spinor Hilbert space H_e, choose
normalized reference and profile spinors u_0,u_chi.  Define

  C_chi^spin := c^dagger(u_chi)c(u_0).

On the one-particle sector,

  C_chi^spin = |u_chi><u_0|,

  [C_chi^spin,(C_chi^spin)^dagger]
    = P_{u_chi}-P_{u_0},

  [N_hat,C_chi^spin]=0.

In a fixed spatial trivialization the relative charge density is

  q_e diag(P_{u_chi}-P_{u_0}).

THEOREM EMD_FERMIONIC_PROFILE_TRANSFER.

The number-preserving profile-transfer operator carries a relative electric
density and spin density while preserving total fermion number.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_ON_THE_ONE_PARTICLE_SECTOR.

The identification with an interacting QED one-electron state remains open.


12. ROOT SECTOR PREDICATE
-------------------------

Define

  EINSTEIN_MAXWELL_DIRAC_ELECTRON_SECTOR(g,A,Psi)

iff:

  (i)   (M,g) is an oriented, time-oriented, globally hyperbolic Lorentzian
        spin spacetime;

  (ii)  Psi satisfies the Dirac equation with q_e=-e and m=m_e;

  (iii) F=dA satisfies Maxwell's equations with the declared total current;

  (iv)  g satisfies Einstein's equation with all declared stress tensors;

  (v)   N_Sigma[Psi]=1;

  (vi)  the selected isolated or neutralized boundary conditions hold;

  (vii) every additional a_dagger/background source is represented explicitly
        in the current and stress tensors.

THEOREM EINSTEIN_MAXWELL_DIRAC_ELECTRON_SECTOR_HULL.

Every sufficiently regular member of this sector satisfies:

  (A) U(1) gauge covariance;

  (B) conserved fermion number N=1;

  (C) conserved electric charge Q=-e for the Dirac component;

  (D) Maxwell-source compatibility;

  (E) exact Dirac/electromagnetic stress exchange;

  (F) conserved total stress-energy and Bianchi compatibility;

  (G) the Einstein and Maxwell Cauchy constraints;

  (H) compact neutrality when the Cauchy surface is closed;

  (I) stationary gauge-invariant observables under the stationary ansatz;

  (J) exact classification of any added transverse field by its defect current.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER_STRUCTURAL.

This theorem does not assert that the sector is nonempty under a chosen global
stationary, asymptotically flat, compact, or stability condition.
