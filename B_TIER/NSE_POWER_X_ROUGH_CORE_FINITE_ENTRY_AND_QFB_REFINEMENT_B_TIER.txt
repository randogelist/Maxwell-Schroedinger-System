0. FINITE NSE^X SETUP
---------------------
Let M=T_L^3, X={1,...,N}, N finite nonempty, with periodic
unnormalized integration. All fluid layers have COMMON nu>0,
unit densities and zero spatial means. Fix a constant symmetric
nonnegative graph kappa_ij=kappa_ji>=0.

On product H_X=(L2_sigma,0)^N and product dot H^s, define

  A=-P_sigma Delta, B(u,v)=P_sigma[(u dot grad)v],
  (L_kappa u)_i=sum_j kappa_ij(u_i-u_j).

The unforced coupled system is

  partial_t u_i+B(u_i,u_i)+nu A u_i
      +(L_kappa u)_i=0.                                    (E1)

Smooth solutions satisfy exactly

  E'(t)+2 nu D(t)+2 Q(t)=0,                          (E2)

where
  E(t)=sum_i ||u_i(t)||_2^2,
  D(t)=sum_i ||grad u_i(t)||_2^2,
  Q(t)=sum_{i<j} kappa_ij ||u_i(t)-u_j(t)||_2^2>=0.

For graph Leray-Hopf solutions assume the standard energy
inequality from t=0:

  E(t)+2nu int_0^t D(s)ds+2int_0^t Q(s)ds<=E0   (E3)

for all admissible times and almost every t, where E0=E(0).
The required weak-solution existence and energy inequality follow
from standard Galerkin estimates for finite symmetric graph systems,
but kernel-level formalization is not asserted.

1. IMPROVED QUARTIC ENERGY BUDGET
---------------------------------
THEOREM FR-01: ENERGY_WEIGHTED_QUARTIC_LERAY_BOUND.

Let A_D(t):=int_0^t D(s)ds. From (E3),

  E(t)<=E0-2nu A_D(t)

for almost every t. Since A_D is nondecreasing and
A_D'(t)=D(t) for almost every t,

  int_0^T E(t)D(t)dt
   <=int_0^T (E0-2nu A_D(t))A_D'(t)dt
   = E0 A_D(T)-nu[A_D(T)]^2
   <= E0^2/(4nu).

Thus, also over the full half line,

  int_0^infty E(t)D(t)dt<=E0^2/(4nu).              (Q1)

The coefficient 1/4 is sharper than 1/2 from the naive
inequality E(t)<=E0 combined with the total dissipation.

For the critical Sobolev scalar

  H(t):=sum_i||u_i(t)||_(dot H^(1/2))^2,

the Fourier/Cauchy interpolation yields

  H(t)
    <= sqrt(E(t))*sqrt(D(t)),

  H(t)^2<=E(t)D(t).

Therefore

  int_0^infty H(t)^2 dt <= E0^2/(4nu).           (Q2)

Proof relies only on (E3), P_sigma energy cancellation,
nonnegative graph drag, and homogeneous spectral
interpolation on a mean-zero periodic torus. It does
NOT require smoothness at time t=0.

2. SHARPENED QFB PERIODIC CUTOFF / GENERAL PRODUCT CUTOFF
----------------------------------------------------------
THEOREM FR-02: QFB_REFINED_SINGLE_LAYER_PERIODIC.

Assume the FROZEN SINGLE-LAYER admission:

  (||b||_(dot H^(1/2))^2<2pi nu^2)
    -> global regular strong NSE trajectory from b

and weak-strong uniqueness after the restarting time.

For N=1 define
  Theta=2pi nu^2.

Take
  T > E0^2/(4nu Theta^2)
    = ||a||_2^4/(16 pi^2 nu^5).                  (Q3)

Then from (Q2) there exists an admissible Lebesgue
time s in (0,T) with

  H(s)^2 < Theta^2, hence H(s)<Theta.

At such a time, apply the small-data admission and
weak-strong uniqueness. The original Leray-Hopf
trajectory is smooth for every t>T_cut, where

  T_cut=||a||_2^4/(16 pi^2 nu^5).

This improves the earlier 1/(8pi^2) coarse cutoff
for the periodic problem itself under exactly the
same frozen small-data admission. It does not
create a lower bound on singularity time or an
independent physical uncertainty time.

This reopens/corrects the PREVIOUS artifact's
statement that 1/(16pi^2) could only be imported
from a distinct QFA whole-space branch; the sharper
energy-weighted inequality is valid directly on
the periodic torus.

THEOREM FR-03: COUPLED_GRAPH_QFB_X_ENTRY.

For N finite, let C_NL>0 satisfy the standard
critical trilinear estimate

  |<Lambda^(1/2) B(v,v),Lambda^(1/2)v>|
  <=C_NL ||v||_(dot H^(1/2))
           ||v||_(dot H^(3/2))^2.

For the product system with symmetric nonnegative
graph, a sufficient small-data threshold is

  sqrt(H(s)) < delta,
  delta:=nu/(2 C_NL).

This follows from the graph-positive critical
energy estimate and standard local critical
H^(1/2) continuation. Define

  Theta_X:=delta^2=nu^2/(4 C_NL^2).

By (Q2), for any

  T> E0^2/(4nu Theta_X^2)
    = 4 C_NL^4 E0^2/nu^5,                       (Q4)

some admissible s<T satisfies H(s)<Theta_X.

The graph-coupled system is then globally regular
from s, by the small critical restart. Weak-strong
uniqueness identifies every Leray-Hopf solution
with this strong continuation for t>s.

Hence the finite graph system has an explicit
eventual-regulator cutoff

  T_graph <= 4 C_NL^4 E0^2/nu^5,

under the standard analytic restart theorems.
No claim that C_NL has been identified with the
specific single-layer 2pi constant.

3. ROUGH 2D3C PRODUCT CORE
--------------------------
THEOREM FR-04: ROUGH_2D3C_GRAPH_FINITE_ENTRY.

Assume the initial core a=(a_i) satisfies

  a_i in L2_sigma,0(M),  partial_y a_i=0
  in distributions,  i=1,...,N.

Let U(t) denote the unforced coupled graph solution
(E1) restricted to the y-invariant sector. Decompose

  U_i=(w_i^x,b_i,w_i^z),
  w_i=(w_i^x,w_i^z),  b_i=U_i^y.

The planar fields solve a FINITE coupled two-dimensional
Navier-Stokes system with symmetric nonnegative linear
drag; the b_i solve linear advection-diffusion with
the same drag and the prescribed planar velocities.

The 2D kinetic energy and planar vorticity estimates
are global. With L2 initial data, standard 2D
parabolic smoothing plus the passive-scalar regularity
give

  U in C([0,infinity);H_X)
       intersection C^infty((0,infinity)xM)^N.

For EVERY t0>0,

  U(t0) in C^infty_sigma,0(M)^N.

Moreover on the zero-mean torus, the unforced
graph-coupled system has Poincare decay in L2.
Standard 2D higher-regularity and parabolic
long-time estimates imply for each fixed t0>0

  B_U(t0)
    :=(2 C_mix^2/nu)
      int_{t0}^infinity
          sum_i ||U_i(t)||_(W^(2,infinity))^2 dt
    < infinity,                                         (R1)

where C_mix is a fixed critical product estimate
constant. The integral may grow without bound as
t0 downarrow 0 for a rough L2 core, so no
UNIFORM B_U(0) is claimed.

The existence of the smooth globally regular
2D3C core is standard PDE input, not a newly
proved full 3D rough-data theorem.

4. SMOOTHLY SWITCHED FULLY 3D INTERACTION
-----------------------------------------
THEOREM FR-05: POST_ENTRY_CRITICAL_3D_FORCE.

Fix t0>0 and the U from FR-04. Let h=(h_i) be a
smooth divergence-free mean-zero interaction
acceleration, with

  h_i(t,x)=0 for t<=t0,

and with finite critical force budget

  F_*:=sum_i int_{t0}^infinity
            ||h_i(t)||_(dot H^(-1/2))^2 dt.

Let
  delta_w=nu/(4 C_NL).

Assume the strict admission

  exp(B_U(t0)) * (2/nu) F_* < delta_w^2.         (R2)

Define the forced graph equation for t>=0:

  partial_t u_i+B(u_i,u_i)+nu A u_i
       +(L_kappa u)_i=h_i(t).

Set u(t)=U(t) for t<=t0. On t>t0 set
  u=U+w, w(t0)=0.

The perturbation w solves

  partial_t w_i+nu A w_i+(L_kappa w)_i
       +B(U_i,w_i)+B(w_i,U_i)+B(w_i,w_i)=h_i.

Let
  H_w(t)=sum_i||w_i(t)||_(dot H^(1/2))^2,
  D_w(t)=sum_i||w_i(t)||_(dot H^(3/2))^2,
  F(t)=sum_i||h_i(t)||_(dot H^(-1/2))^2.

Assume the standard critical multiplication estimate
  ||B(U_i,w_i)+B(w_i,U_i)||_(dot H^(-1/2))
   <= C_mix ||U_i||_(W^(2,infinity))
            ||w_i||_(dot H^(1/2)).

Pairing with Lambda w_i, and using positive
graph dissipation, Young's inequality gives
on the bootstrap interval sqrt(H_w)<=delta_w:

  H_w' <= beta_U(t) H_w +(2/nu)F(t),

where
  beta_U(t)
   =(2 C_mix^2/nu)sum_i||U_i(t)||_(W^(2,infinity))^2.

Gronwall and H_w(t0)=0 yield

  sup_{t>=t0} H_w(t)
    <= exp(B_U(t0))*(2/nu)*F_*
    < delta_w^2.

Thus w cannot exit the critical smallness regime.
Local forced critical strong-solution continuation
extends it for all t>=t0, with u smooth whenever
the force is smooth; on (0,t0] it equals the
original 2D3C smooth U.

CONCLUSION:
  u in C([0,infinity);H_X)
       intersection C^infty((0,infinity)xM)^N.

It has a fully THREE-DIMENSIONAL velocity for
suitable h and U, despite its arbitrarily rough
2D3C L2 initial data. Interaction magnitude is
restricted by (R2), not by a bound on E(0).

The construction does NOT prove regularity for a
three-dimensional perturbation already present at
t=0 for arbitrarily rough U(0). Switching at
positive time is an explicit necessary premise
of this particular proof.

5. EXPLICIT INJECTABLE THIRD-DIRECTION FOURIER TEST
----------------------------------------------------
THEOREM FR-06: THREE_DIMENSIONAL_FORCING_AMPLITUDE.

Suppose U has x- and z-dependent Fourier modes
but is independent of y. Let q=2pi r/L, r in Z\{0},
and fix a selected layer i0.

Let chi in C_c^infty((t0,infinity);R), not identically
zero, and choose the divergence-free mean-zero test

  h_i(t,x,y,z)
     = 1_{i=i0} * alpha*chi(t)*sin(qy)*e_x.

Its divergence is zero because the x component
is independent of x. It lies outside the y-invariant
sector. Its homogeneous critical forcing norm is

  F_* =alpha^2 [L^3/(2 |q|)] *||chi||_(L2_t)^2.

Sufficient interaction amplitude:

  |alpha|
   < [nu^(3/2)*sqrt(|q|)/
      (4 C_NL L^(3/2)||chi||_L2)]
        *exp(-B_U(t0)/2).                       (R3)

This is algebraically equivalent to (R2), because
  delta_w^2=nu^2/(16 C_NL^2).

Under (R3), the forced solution remains smooth
for all t>0. New y-dependence is generated
for t after the support begins.

7. EXPLICIT MATHEMATICAL AND PHYSICAL LIMITS
---------------------------------------------
The finite-entry theorem does not establish:
 (i) full rank-3 NSE regularity without force control;
(ii) arbitrary energy quantized 2-electron dynamics;
(iii) physical NSE-electron current equality;
(iv) a universal energy-time uncertainty principle;
 (v) an intrinsic time delay before electron interaction;
(vi) any necessity for interactions to switch on late
     in nature; it is a mathematical sufficient design.
(vii) full coupling to Standard Model quantum fields.

The cutoff QFB/refined is a general certificate horizon,
not the actual singularity time of the regular classes.
No recursive time halving is derived.

8. ROOT THEOREM
---------------
THEOREM NSE_POWER_X_ROUGH_CORE_INTERACTION_FINITE_ENTRY.

Under the finite symmetric graph NSE^X model and
the standard 2D3C positive-time smoothing theorem,
for every zero-mean L2 initial datum sharing a common
2D3C symmetry and every t0>0, there exists a
positive epsilon_force(t0,U,nu,C_NL,C_mix) such that
all smooth mean-zero solenoidal forces h supported
after t0 with

  ||h||_(L2((t0,infinity);dot H^(-1/2))^X)
     <epsilon_force

produce globally smooth positive-time solutions.
One explicit admissible choice is

  epsilon_force
   =nu^(3/2)/(sqrt(32)*C_NL)
     *exp(-B_U(t0)/2).

Additionally any finite graph Leray-Hopf solution
has an eventual critical smallness restart time
bounded by (Q4). For N=1 with the frozen threshold
2pi nu^2, (Q3) gives the improved periodic QFB
certificate 1/(16pi^2).

  FR-04 -> FR-05 -> FR-06,
