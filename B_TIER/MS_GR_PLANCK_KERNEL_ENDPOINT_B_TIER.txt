------------------

1. EXACT GAUSSIAN KERNEL SCALE
------------------------------

THEOREM A_DAGGER_KERNEL_SCALE_IDENTIFICATION.

On the physical Fourier wave vector xi, the operator

  exp((epsilon^2/4) Delta)

has multiplier

  exp(-epsilon^2 |xi|^2 / 4).

The historical Gaussian gravitational kernel has multiplier

  exp(-a_*^2 |xi|^2 / 4)
  = exp(-l_*^2 |xi|^2).

Therefore equality of the two multiplier conventions is equivalent to

  a_* = epsilon,
  l_* = epsilon/2.

DERIVATION.

On the unit torus, mode k in Z^3 has physical wave vector

  xi_k = 2 pi k,

and

  Delta exp(2 pi i k dot x)
    = -|xi_k|^2 exp(2 pi i k dot x).

Hence

  exp((epsilon^2/4) Delta)

multiplies that mode by

  exp(-epsilon^2 |xi_k|^2/4)
  = exp(-pi^2 epsilon^2 |k|^2).

On R^3, the corresponding convolution kernel is

  K_epsilon(x)
    = (pi epsilon^2)^(-3/2)
      exp(-|x|^2/epsilon^2).

Its Fourier multiplier is exactly

  exp(-epsilon^2 |xi|^2/4).

The periodic kernel is the lattice periodization of K_epsilon. Therefore the
kernel parameter appearing in exp(-a_*^2|xi|^2/4) is exactly epsilon.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

SCOPE NOTE.

This theorem identifies conventions. It does not identify the physical origin
of epsilon and does not imply epsilon is a Planck-scale quantity.


2. THE QUANTUM TWO-MODE ORBIT
-----------------------------

Let

  H_m := L^2(T^3; C).

Let e_0(x)=1 and let chi in H_m satisfy

  ||e_0||_2 = ||chi||_2 = 1.

Assume chi is not a complex scalar multiple of e_0. Define

  c_0 := |<e_0,chi>|,
  s_0 := sqrt(1-c_0^2),

and choose a unit vector xi orthogonal to e_0 such that, after a constant phase
choice,

  chi = c_0 e_0 + s_0 xi.

The projective two-mode orbit is

  O_chi := P(span_C{e_0,xi})
         isomorphic to CP^1.

A normalized representative is

  psi(theta,phi)
    := cos(theta/2) e_0
       + exp(i phi) sin(theta/2) xi,

where

  0 <= theta <= pi,
  phi in R/(2 pi Z).

THEOREM MS_PROFILE_TRANSFER_CP1.

The number-preserving profile-transfer unitary generated in the two-mode
sector acts within O_chi, and chi is represented by

  theta_chi = 2 arccos(c_0)

with the appropriate relative phase.

3. FUBINI-STUDY ACTION SCALE
----------------------------

Define the normalized Fubini-Study two-form on O_chi by

  omega_FS
    := (1/2) sin(theta) dtheta wedge dphi.

Then

  integral_{O_chi} omega_FS = 2 pi.

The Schrödinger action one-form on the unit sphere is

  Theta_QM := hbar Im <psi,d psi>.

THEOREM MS_FUBINI_STUDY_ACTION_SCALE.

On the two-mode chart,

  Theta_QM
    = hbar sin^2(theta/2) dphi
    = (hbar/2)(1-cos theta) dphi,

and therefore

  Omega_MS := d Theta_QM
            = hbar omega_FS.

Consequently,

  integral_{O_chi} Omega_MS = 2 pi hbar.

DERIVATION.

Differentiate psi(theta,phi). Orthogonality of e_0 and xi gives

  <psi,d psi>
    = i sin^2(theta/2) dphi.

Taking the imaginary part and differentiating yields

  d Theta_QM
    = (hbar/2) sin(theta) dtheta wedge dphi.

STATUS = EXACT_B_TIER.

NORMALIZATION AUDIT.

The coefficient hbar depends on the explicit convention

  integral_{CP^1} omega_FS = 2 pi.

If a different normalization is used, both the GR coefficient and the quantum
coefficient must be transformed by the same convention before comparison.

6. CONDITIONAL PLANCK-KERNEL ENDPOINT
-------------------------------------

THEOREM MS_GR_PLANCK_KERNEL_ENDPOINT.

Assume:

  H1. G>0, c>0, hbar>0, epsilon>0;

  H2. MS_FUBINI_STUDY_ACTION_SCALE:

        Omega_MS = hbar omega_FS;

  H3. GR_GAUSSIAN_SYMPLECTIC_PULLBACK(epsilon):

        iota_epsilon^* Omega_GR
          = [c^3 epsilon^2/(4G)] omega_FS;

  H4. SYMPLECTIC_MATCHING(epsilon):

        iota_epsilon^* Omega_GR = Omega_MS;

  H5. omega_FS is nonzero.

Then

  epsilon = 2 sqrt(G hbar/c^3).

Equivalently,

  epsilon = 2 l_P,
  a_*     = 2 l_P,
  l_*     = l_P,
  S_epsilon = hbar,
  Xi_epsilon = 1.

DERIVATION.

From H2-H4,

  [c^3 epsilon^2/(4G)] omega_FS
    = hbar omega_FS.

Since omega_FS is nonzero,

  c^3 epsilon^2/(4G) = hbar.

Therefore

  epsilon^2 = 4G hbar/c^3.

Because epsilon>0,

  epsilon = 2 sqrt(G hbar/c^3)
          = 2 l_P.

The remaining identities follow from

  a_*=epsilon,
  l_*=epsilon/2,
  S_epsilon=c^3 epsilon^2/(4G),
  Xi_epsilon=S_epsilon/hbar.

STATUS = CONDITIONAL_B_TIER.

solenoidal and mean-zero. Consequently, every positive epsilon defines the

Therefore the existing Maxwell-Schrodinger and heat-regularization structure
forms an epsilon-parametrized family. The existence of this family alone does
