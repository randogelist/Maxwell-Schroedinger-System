Frozen TT action convention:

```text
S_TT=(c^2/(64 pi G)) integral [dot h^2-c^2|grad h|^2].
```

For two TT profiles with norm law

```text
integral T_a:T_b = epsilon^3 I_TT delta_ab
```

and `omega_epsilon=sigma c/epsilon`, the two-mode ansatz gives

```text
Omega_TT=S_epsilon sum_a dQ_a wedge dP_a
S_epsilon=(c^3 epsilon^2/G)(A^2 sigma I_TT/(32 pi)).
```
